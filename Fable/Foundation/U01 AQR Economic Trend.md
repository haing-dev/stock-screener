## 3. Mechanism audit

[[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C02-3|QP01-C02-3]], [[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C04-8|QP01-C04-8]]). Steps 1-2 rest on citations and assertions; only the end result is tested.

| Cross-asset price momentum relabelled | Nothing. supported elsewhere. The paper does not establish that *economic* news is what markets under-react to.

## 7. Forward-looking logic

Inputs said to lead prices: 12-month changes in consensus GDP and CPI forecasts, in the export-weighted exchange rate,
in 2-year yields, and the equal mix of 12-month equity excess return and credit-spread change, per country and
consumption-weighted for commodities, mapped by Exhibit 1 ([[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C04-1|QP01-C04-1]]..[[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C04-6|QP01-C04-6]], [[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C04-16|QP01-C04-16]]). Horizon: monthly positions on
12-month signals; the decay of the edge is never measured (forward_looking_inputs), hence T1. The logic should stop
working when (a) prices decouple from fundamentals for long: June 1999-Dec 2000, econ -18% while price trend +35%
([[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C03-6|QP01-C03-6]], [[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C09-5|QP01-C09-5]]); (b) macro volatility is low: the 2010s at 0.4 ([[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C06-3|QP01-C06-3]]); (c) policy is predictable or pinned: (reader) at
the zero bound 2009-2015 the 12-month change in 2-year yields was near zero in the US, Japan and Germany, so the
monetary theme, the paper's second-best, was mute, consistent with the weak 2010s though untested in the paper;
forward guidance that prices the whole path on announcement removes the drift behind C02-6b; (d) the sign map breaks:
inflation-bearish-equities ([[QP01 - Economic Trend (AQR Brooks, Feilbogen, Ooi, Akant, April 2023)#^QP01-C04-6|QP01-C04-6]]) presumes an inflation-dominated regime; in a deflation scare rising inflation
forecasts are good for equities (reader); (e) more capital chases the same signals (managed futures $198bn, C02-1b).
