   [[MT044 - Drawdowns, Diversification, and the Hidden Risks in Today’s Markets - Systematic Investor#^MT044-C04-2|MT044-C04-2]]), so a few years of results carry no information and nothing can be timed; the only durable edge is
   operational: continuous vol-scaled sizing by forecast strength, breadth before styles, trading no faster than costs
   allow, a priori weights, no overrides ([[MT137 - Rob Carver on Trend Following, Skew, and the Total Portfolio Shift - Systematic Investor -#^MT137-C01-1|MT137-C01-1]], [[MT140 - Robert Carver Ran a Multi-Billion Dollar Systematic Portfolio for Man AHL.
