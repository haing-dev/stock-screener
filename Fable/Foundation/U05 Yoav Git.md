## 1. Verdict

1. Core logic: trend P&L is the harvest of positive return autocorrelation created by sustained imbalances whose
   structure differs by market and changes over time (rate gradients and coupons in bonds and EM FX, slow supply
   response in commodities, value traders and option dealers in equities); the signal is a commodity worth about 0.2
   Sharpe per market, so the edge is market selection by structure, a diversification multiple set by the average beta
   to the first factor rather than by market count, risk scaling and cost-aware execution; drawdowns are years in which
   fewer than about 40% of markets trend, not model failure ([[MT029 - CTAs- The Good, The Bad, & The Misunderstood - Top Traders Unplugged 148#^MT029-C08-1|MT029-C08-1]], [[MT223 - Why Trend Thrives When Inflation Returns - Systematic Investor - Ep.373#^MT223-C15-2|MT223-C15-2]], [[MT205 - Unpacking the Chaos- What Today’s Market Signals Really Mean - Systematic Investor 343#^MT205-C12-2|MT205-C12-2]], [[MT221 - Why Trend Following Still Works in Tough Markets - Systematic Investor - Ep.381#^MT221-C10-2|MT221-C10-2]]).
2. Grounding: assertion almost throughout. Numbers are from memory or relayed (Carver's smile, CFM's 50% pairwise
   correlation, Quantica, the SG trend indicator, a QIS haircut study); his own tests (ERAS inflation study,
   autocorrelation paper, COT regression) are described, never shown; the one track-record figure is "about 12% after
   fees over three years" with no volatility ([[MT024 - Building an Inflation-Proof Portfolio - Systematic Investor - Ep.384#^MT024-C11-3|MT024-C11-3]], claim text). He sells asset-class-specific and
   alternative-market trend and concedes several theories are unproven. Direction credible, sizes unknown.
3. Our book already matches his practice where matching is cheap: vol-scaled multi-speed trend with carry inside the
   price series ([config.py:19](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/config.py#L19); [CHANGES.md:18](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/CHANGES.md#L18)), dynamic vol sizing ([vol.py:4-10](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/vol.py#L4-L10)), per-market risk caps ([book.py:200-201](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/book.py#L200-L201)),
   a long design window, no model change on pain ([TESTING_PROTOCOL.md:29-32](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/docs/research/global_macro/TESTING_PROTOCOL.md#L29-L32)). Our trial log agrees with him twice:
   spot-only trend lost (1.39 vs 1.48, [CHANGES.md:18](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/CHANGES.md#L18)) as his "carry in the series raises Sharpe" predicts
   ([[MT024 - Building an Inflation-Proof Portfolio - Systematic Investor - Ep.384#^MT024-C14-1|MT024-C14-1]]), and signal variants failed while construction changes passed ([CHANGES.md:14-24](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/CHANGES.md#L14-L24)).

   [book.py:9](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/book.py#L9), 138-139) where he says only the average beta to the first factor counts, and our concentration (fx_krw

## 7. Forward-looking logic

Inputs he says lead prices, with the chain step: (1) rate gradients and carry from central-bank divergence create
sustained flows and autocorrelation in bonds and EM FX over months to years; the edge vanishes with the gradient, as DM

(2) Supply shocks against
slow supply response (storage, a year for crops and cattle, 20 years for a mine, power plants for AI demand) force
price to adjust over about a year, so commodity trend is harvested slowly and stops when supply catches up or inflation
falls ([[MT024 - Building an Inflation-Proof Portfolio - Systematic Investor - Ep.384#^MT024-C04-4|MT024-C04-4]], C07-4, C07-3; [[MT029 - CTAs- The Good, The Bad, & The Misunderstood - Top Traders Unplugged 148#^MT029-C09-3|MT029-C09-3]]; [[MT223 - Why Trend Thrives When Inflation Returns - Systematic Investor - Ep.373#^MT223-C08-X2|MT223-C08-X2]]). (3) The inflation regime: above about 4.5% bonds earn
CPI-minus and co-move with equities and commodity trend pays; inflation evolves slowly, matching trend's speed, while
equity crises happen in days and allow only a second response ([[MT024 - Building an Inflation-Proof Portfolio - Systematic Investor - Ep.384#^MT024-C06-1|MT024-C06-1]]; [[MT223 - Why Trend Thrives When Inflation Returns - Systematic Investor - Ep.373#^MT223-C13-2|MT223-C13-2]]). erode the short horizon first: retail 0DTE selling gives dealers positive gamma whose hedging sells rallies and buys
dips, so one-day equity autocorrelation turned negative and dip-buying has worked for ten years; short expiries are
spreading to bond futures and oil ([[MT190 - Trend Following Is Changing Faster Than Ever - Systematic Investor - Ep.409#^MT190-C09-1|MT190-C09-1]], C09-2, C08-4, C10-X2). (reader) Our 1-month leg is the part of our
Sharpe that depends on trading within days of month end ([CHANGES.md:77-79](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/CHANGES.md#L77-L79)) and the horizon he says is eroding;

after 2012. (5) Vol spikes make all vol-scaled traders cut together, adding next-day volatility; an extended trend makes
historical vol under-forecast the next day and the trend level adds 5-10% to vol-forecast accuracy ([[MT205 - Unpacking the Chaos- What Today’s Market Signals Really Mean - Systematic Investor 343#^MT205-C10-2|MT205-C10-2]],
C10-3, claim text). (6) Speculator share of front-month volume marks a market about to stop trending for a

It stops
working when nothing is in imbalance (vol compression, winner share under about 40%, [[MT221 - Why Trend Following Still Works in Tough Markets - Systematic Investor - Ep.381#^MT221-C11-1|MT221-C11-1]]), when a market's
