## 1. Verdict

1. Core logic: one market's trend is a weak edge (a Sharpe of 0.1 in Robertson's arithmetic), so performance comes
   from the number of independent price drivers, and that choice has two faces: liquid macro futures carry the crisis
   payoff at a long-run Sharpe of about 0.5-0.6, idiosyncratic alternative markets carry 0.8-0.9 with no crisis
   payoff; speed splits the same way (slow for Sharpe, fast for crises, the middle not interpolable); so universe,
   speed and wrapper (standalone, bond substitute, or ported onto equity beta with about 40% cash) follow the
   investor's objective, and the allocation is held, never timed ([[MT111 - Navigating Market Chaos - Systematic Investor 336#^MT111-C06-1|MT111-C06-1]]; [[MT158 - Taking the Trend Following Temperature - Systematic Investor 304#^MT158-C07-1|MT158-C07-1]], C07-2; [[MT083 - Inside Q1’s Chaos - Systematic Investor - Ep.394#^MT083-C06-5|MT083-C06-5]],
   C07-2; [[MT014 - Adapt or Perish- Why Systematic Investors Must Evolve in 2024 - Systematic Investor 325#^MT014-C05-3|MT014-C05-3]], C05-4; [[MT127 - Portable Alpha, Trend Following & AI Investing ft. Harry Moore - Systematic Investor - Ep#^MT127-C13-4|MT127-C13-4]]; [[MT158 - Taking the Trend Following Temperature - Systematic Investor 304#^MT158-C08-1|MT158-C08-1]]).
2. Grounding: assertion plus house studies described on air (a market-mix study on about 900 markets, a
   portable-alpha Monte Carlo on the S&P plus SG Trend); no AHL return, cost or capacity figure; numbers from
   memory; a Man product behind each main claim (reader). Sound as arithmetic, weak as magnitudes, and they supply
   the counter-evidence themselves: core markets did best in 2022, the diversifiers had not helped for a couple of
   years by 2025, alternative power markets ranged in 2026 ([[MT014 - Adapt or Perish- Why Systematic Investors Must Evolve in 2024 - Systematic Investor 325#^MT014-C16-2|MT014-C16-2]]; [[MT111 - Navigating Market Chaos - Systematic Investor 336#^MT111-C05-2|MT111-C05-2]]; [[MT127 - Portable Alpha, Trend Following & AI Investing ft. Harry Moore - Systematic Investor - Ep#^MT127-C05-4|MT127-C05-4]]).
3. For our book: we hold one end of their frontier: 62 traditional liquid markets (reader), the end they credit with
   crisis payoff and a 0.5-0.6 long-run trend Sharpe (our design era: 1.22 gross, protocol_diagnostics:16); the regime
   they say starves it, policy-pinned 2011-19, lies in our holdout. Their remedy, breadth, is throttled here: of the
   11 alternative commodities waiting on Bloomberg nine are relatives of markets we already trade (reader); under the
   30% cap they are worth about +0.02 Sharpe (reader), below the 0.05 bar; and as coded they would join the panel with
   the data file, with no registered challenger ([panel.py:563-567](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/47b83657bf56f68c1c3241e7cdf8ddc94c2026b3/gm/panel.py#L563-L567)).
4. On the standing disputes. Vol-sizing speed: AHL is dynamic (with Carver, Baltas, Git) but warns that an
   over-responsive estimator misses the rebound (Kaminski's caveat); no numbers; the hybrid stands. Market
   selection: with Baltas against choosing by trendiness, with Git that drivers not counts matter, against Git's "50
   versus 200 is no different"; it adds a crisis axis (three amendments to U05-T1). Carry: Moore sides with Kaminski
   only for a full liquidity break, with Baltas and Git on the average (U03-T2 splits its windows). Execution
   timing: silent.
5. Action: at most two Holm slots, both conditional: the universe challenger the plan already implies (T2; none under
   the breadth route of section 6) and a Batch 3 challenger on how often the trend signs are re-read (T3), since a
   synthetic check puts most of our exposure to AHL's 3-4-month whipsaw in the monthly decision. First gate the
   alternative commodities and run the macro-loading split (T1). One decision for the user: the breadth route, settled
   together with U02's cost and U03's risk routes.
