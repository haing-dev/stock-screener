## 1. Verdict

1. Core logic: markets are a human system with no fixed laws, so a model is worth only its profit on data it has not
   seen, no statistic of a drifting record (the Sharpe ratio least of all) certifies quality, and every edge that pays
   draws capital until it decays, the simple and fast ones first; the answer is a research process, not a system:
   trend stays the core at an expected Sharpe near 0.5, traded slower, with weak non-trend signals and genuinely
   different markets added in a fixed mix, low leverage, strict control of data mining and no switching of speed by
   regime ([[QP14 - Making Money from Mathematical Models (David Harding, Phil. Trans. R. Soc. Lond. A 1994)#^QP14-C05-2|QP14-C05-2]], C05-1b; [[QP03 - A Critique of the Sharpe Ratio (David Harding, Winton)#^QP03-C01-4|QP03-C01-4]]; [[MT070 - Harding Rewrites Winton's Quant Playbook in Search for Returns#^MT070-C01-2|MT070-C01-2]], C02-1; [[MT177 - The Secrets of Quantitative Investing - ft. Simon Judes - Top Traders Unplugged 139#^MT177-C04-1|MT177-C04-1]], C01-2, C13-1, C02-3, C10-1).
2. Grounding: the papers SHOW nothing. The Sharpe critique is sound as logic ([[QP03 - A Critique of the Sharpe Ratio (David Harding, Winton)#^QP03-C02-3|QP03-C02-3]]), unmeasured in size, and it
   flatters the house style (reader). The decay claim rests on a recalled record ("nearly one for 30 years", then
   "around a half"; [[MT070 - Harding Rewrites Winton's Quant Playbook in Search for Returns#^MT070-C06-1|MT070-C06-1]], [[MT177 - The Secrets of Quantitative Investing - ft. Simon Judes - Top Traders Unplugged 139#^MT177-C04-1|MT177-C04-1]]), house research described without numbers and a correlation of two
   trending series ([[MT177 - The Secrets of Quantitative Investing - ft. Simon Judes - Top Traders Unplugged 139#^MT177-C19-1|MT177-C19-1]]); crowding as the cause is disputed by Harding's own cash-and-leverage arithmetic
   ([[MT070 - Harding Rewrites Winton's Quant Playbook in Search for Returns#^MT070-C02-4|MT070-C02-4]]) and by four earlier units. Winton's record (section 3) supports "returns fell, capital chases
   records", not the remedy: the 2019 move into equities is reported without a performance figure, and in 2023 the
   diversified programme is three-quarters trend ([[MT070 - Harding Rewrites Winton's Quant Playbook in Search for Returns#^MT070-C01-3|MT070-C01-3]]; [[MT177 - The Secrets of Quantitative Investing - ft. Simon Judes - Top Traders Unplugged 139#^MT177-C01-2|MT177-C01-2]]).
3. For our book: their operating conclusions are already in the config (fixed shares set partly on Winton's 75/25, no
   regime switching, growth theme off, no equity carry: [config.py:38](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/config.py#L38), 62; [signals.py:230](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/signals.py#L230); [book.py:106](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/book.py#L106)). Our four
   families are published premia, which by their logic decay first; the live target of 0.4-0.8 says as much
   (TOOL_INVENTORY:35). We differ where their real-money evidence is strongest, speed: our design Sharpe leans on a
   1-month leg traded on day one ([CHANGES.md:25-27](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/gm/CHANGES.md#L25-L27), 77-79), the kind of edge Winton says went first. The design panel
   cannot settle it (trend family 1.08 then 1.30 across the eras, [gap_review_crosscheck:16](https://github.com/haing-dev/stock-screener/blob/6aee2bdb9ececf7cd9c470e70454237a236ab3fe/docs/research/global_macro/gap_review_crosscheck_2026-10-31.md#L16); the fast blend's lead over
   6/12 is 0.28 on 1990-2010 and 0.15 on 2000-10, timing_grid rows 9, 14): any decay sits in the holdout.
4. The Sharpe critique against our bar: (a) paired dSR compares two books over the same months with no distributional
   assumption, so the stationarity objection largely cancels; (b) tests 2 and 5, and the holdout's Sharpe floor with
   a drawdown bound, already have the form of his remedy, return under a risk constraint; (c) exposed: the tail test
   and the registry's max-fall guard read realised losses only, and a Sharpe-ranked choice is blind to a clipped
   right tail and to levered quiet markets; (d) the level statements (the 0.3 floor, the power table, a selection
   frozen at 2010) assume the stationarity he denies. Grade: logic strong, size unknown, second order by Carver's
   bootstrap (U02 section 3). The bar does not change; diagnostics and two registry dimensions follow.
5. Action: no new Holm slot. Three zero-slot diagnostics (T1 shape audit of the bar; T2 real against simulated trend
   by decade and speed; T4 Sharpe moved by each data correction) and two procedure dimensions for the registry, each
   settled by a rule fixed in advance on design data: S, the selection statistic (T1), and P, frozen levels or a
   continuing procedure in the holdout (T3). Holdout pass probabilities are stated at half the design Sharpe as well.
