## 3. Pods

### Carry (existing book)

- **Chain.** A forward price is set by people passing on a price risk; whoever takes it earns the gap if the price does not move (reader). [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve|QP08]] shows only that a forward must be biased ([[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C03-3|QP08-C03-3]]); by row 12 the payer must be named class by class (quotes in row 11).
  - Currencies (ASSERTED): a risk premium; the implied payers are savers who accept low rates on currencies that hold up in bad states.
  - Commodities (ASSERTED): producers who insure against price falls, or hedgers on either side ([[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C05-7|QP08-C05-7]]).
  - Bonds: pattern, no mechanism. The quote names no payer; claim text of [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C05-9|QP08-C05-9]] mentions a liquidity premium and preferred habitat; borrowers who fix a long rate are my reading (reader).
  - They keep paying because the need for safety or insurance stays (reader).
- **Rivals.** Currencies: pay for losses in bad states ([[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C04-5|QP08-C04-5]]); for a jump that "has not materialized" (C05-3), so the sample flatters carry; or for a central bank regaining trust (C05-4), so no bad state follows. Commodities: insurance, so pay follows hedgers' net position; or storage (C05-8), so the slope only reports scarcity. The predictions are mine (reader); none is tested here. The one cited test across classes leans to risk: carry "is partially explained by recession and volatility risk" ([[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C05-1|QP08-C05-1]], words only; the paper is dated 2012 in [[QP15 - Value and Momentum Everywhere (Asness, Moskowitz, Pedersen)#^QP15-C04-5|QP15-C04-5]]).
- **Grade: weak on this pack.** One sellers' paper; one sample; before costs; emerging markets included (claim text of [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C29-1|QP08-C29-1]]) with the survivorship of row 3; no figure for carry alone in a quote. [[QP15 - Value and Momentum Everywhere (Asness, Moskowitz, Pedersen)|QP15]] only cites a carry paper. This grades the pack, not the literature.
- **Rule.** Markets: bonds and notes, currencies, four energy futures (digest). Signal: bond yield minus the 3-month rate; 3-month rate gap to the dollar; slope between the first two energy contracts. Version 1, directional: long if carry is positive, short if negative. Version 2, cross-asset: position by rank of carry inside the class. Weights, sizing and the four weekly tranches are the charter's.

Doubt: [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve|QP08]] finds the forms alike (row 3) and does not say which part is paid, class-wide or relative. Row 4 is exact only for positions proportional to the signal; a sign version and a rank version are not, so the average is a mix of the two forms, not exactly directional carry with half its class-wide position hedged (reader; section 7 item 3). Fixed: markets; no equities, since equity carry rises with the dividend yield Value already ranks on (row 13). Dropped: roll-down, the gain as a bond ages down a rising curve ("carry + roll down", [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C10-4|QP08-C10-4]]); it needs a second maturity per market, shown on LSEG only, licence for live use unverified (digest).

### Value (existing book)

## 5. Diagnostics (zero slots, design months to 2010-12)

**X1. Is energy carry a calendar pattern?** Signals only.
- Why: [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve|QP08]] measures commodity carry with "the first future and the contract exactly expiring one year later" ([[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C10-3|QP08-C10-3]]) and takes the calendar out of equity carry (C10-2). Our rule uses neighbouring contracts (digest); in a seasonal market their slope is partly the expected seasonal price change (reader).
- Compute: per energy market, over all month-ends with a signal, the share of the carry signal's variance explained by its twelve calendar-month averages. Engine signal.
- Gate: 0.25 or more (a materiality line fixed here, not tuned) replaces that market's signal, in both versions, by the signal minus the average, over all earlier months of the same calendar month (at least five; no signal before), of the signal minus its trailing 12-month average (the 12 month-ends up to and including that month-end, as in [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve|QP08]]'s "1Y moving average", [[QP08 - Dissecting Investment Strategies in the Cross Section and Time Series (Baz, Granger, Harve#^QP08-C10-2|QP08-C10-2]]). Registered with the A1 design changes before any run. Below: no change.

**X2. Is Value paid by prices coming back, or by income?** Design-era returns in two parts.
- Why: the thesis says cheap markets get dearer; if the pay is all income, the leg is carry (rows 13, 14).
- Compute: per Value leg (equities, bonds, currencies) and version, split each market's monthly excess return into income (one twelfth of: dividend yield minus 3-month rate; bond yield minus 3-month rate; rate gap to the dollar; at the prior month-end) and price change (the rest; for bonds it includes roll-down, which cannot be split off without a second maturity). Apply the leg's positions to each part and sum, before costs. Engine panel.
- Gate: computed on the leg's averaged position (equal-weight average of its versions, as traded), over all design months in which the leg holds a position; a price part of zero or less enters the leg in the thesis register (1.5) as paid by income; its design-era return may then not be cited for the Value thesis in the spec, the batch review or the hold-out verdict. Positive: no entry. No position changes. The split by leg goes into the Value and Carry specs.
