# Harry Moore

Systematic trend-following researcher at Man Group (AHL; former actuary) who sees trend as a cash-efficient, liquid, rules-based diversifier whose job is to protect and fund a portfolio in the left tail. Trend has no long-term view, follows prices across assets (short bonds and equities or long commodities as narratives change), is zero or negatively correlated to equities in the worst periods, and needs only about $20-25 of cash per $100 of exposure because it manages to risk, so margin falls as volatility rises. From that he builds portfolios: market mix chosen by objective (traditional markets give the best crisis Sharpe, alternative markets the best long-run Sharpe; market count is a poor measure of diversification), a mostly-trend mandate (70-80%+), some fast models kept alongside slow ones, and portable alpha (trend on top of index beta) with about 40% cash (10% margin plus 30% unencumbered) so forced beta selling happens about once in 100-200 years. Drawdowns of a 0.6 Sharpe strategy can reach 33% for years and do not show it is broken; rules that deallocate in drawdowns underperform. In 2026 he stays balanced on whether the AI trade is a bubble or right for ten years, and wants a portfolio that survives both, with trend as the one holding uncorrelated in a 30-40% equity crash. Evidence is simulation, Monte Carlo, principal-component decomposition over about 900 markets and historical comparisons, with cautious statements about single episodes.

## Mechanisms
- **Traditional markets win in crises, alternatives win long run**: crisis -> macro factors (risk on/off, growth, inflation, policy) explain more variance -> traditional markets load on them -> higher crisis Sharpe → alternative markets have idiosyncratic drivers -> higher long-run Sharpe; max crisis Sharpe overweights gold, crude, dollar vs pound; max long-run overweights hydropower, iron ore, China commodities. Fails when: Not stated; March 2026 not tested in the paper ([[MT083 - Inside Q1’s Chaos - Systematic Investor - Ep.394#^MT083-C07-1|MT083-C07-1]], [[MT083 - Inside Q1’s Chaos - Systematic Investor - Ep.394#^MT083-C07-2|MT083-C07-2]], [[MT083 - Inside Q1’s Chaos - Systematic Investor - Ep.394#^MT083-C07-3|MT083-C07-3]], [[MT083 - Inside Q1’s Chaos - Systematic Investor - Ep.394#^MT083-C06-5|MT083-C06-5]])

Fails when: Trend gets some turns wrong; inflation shock with whipsaws; theme positions reverse (July 2026) ([[MT127 - Portable Alpha, Trend Following & AI Investing ft.
