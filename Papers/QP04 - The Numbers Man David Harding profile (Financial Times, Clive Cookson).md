# The Numbers Man: David Harding profile (Financial Times, Clive Cookson)

**Authors:** David Harding (Winton Capital Management); Clive Cookson (Financial Times, interviewer)

**Summary:** Question: how does Winton's founder describe the scientific, data-driven basis of his systematic hedge fund. Method: FT magazine profile and interview (Nov 2016), no data tests. Conclusion: edge comes from many weak signals across markets found by empirical research, with continual replacement as signals decay.

## Argument map

**Question:** How does a large systematic fund (Winton) make money from markets, and what is its research philosophy?

**Thesis:** Markets are imperfect because humans over-react, under-react and panic; strong inefficiencies get arbitraged away, so a scientific, empirical process that combines many weak signals across many markets and keeps renewing them is the source of edge. ([[QP04 - The Numbers Man David Harding profile (Financial Times, Clive Cookson)#^QP04-C03-8|C03-8]], [[QP04 - The Numbers Man David Harding profile (Financial Times, Clive Cookson)#^QP04-C03-4|C03-4]], [[QP04 - The Numbers Man David Harding profile (Financial Times, Clive Cookson)#^QP04-C03-3|C03-3]], [[QP04 - The Numbers Man David Harding profile (Financial Times, Clive Cookson)#^QP04-C03-9|C03-9]], [[QP04 - The Numbers Man David Harding profile (Financial Times, Clive Cookson)#^QP04-C03-2|C03-2]])

### Forward-looking inputs (reader synthesis)
(reader) Weather (soil moisture, cloud cover) and supply-chain inventories lead agricultural and other commodity prices over weeks to months via supply surprises; seasonal factors lead via recurring patterns. These stop working when widely exploited (crowding) or when market structure changes.

### Reader inferences (not stated by the authors)
- (reader) The weak-signals-plus-breadth logic is the fundamental law of active management (IR ~ IC x sqrt(breadth)), not named in the article.
- (reader) Over-reaction and slumbering inaction map to reversal and momentum respectively.
- (reader) The article is a profile, so its evidential value is low; use only as design philosophy.
