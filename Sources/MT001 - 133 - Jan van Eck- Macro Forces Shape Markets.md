**Summary:** Jan van Eck (CEO of Van Eck, $230bn AUM) argues big outside forces (fiscal and monetary policy, deregulation, technology, demographics) shape markets and that long-term rate regimes bias history. ## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Jan van Eck argues that outside forces (fiscal and monetary policy, deregulation, technology, demographics, war) shape markets, so he builds portfolios from those forces rather than from market-internal theory, and reads every long backtest with the 40-year falling-rate regime in mind. In practice he stays fully invested in equities except when the Fed tightens aggressively, replaces part of the bond sleeve with real assets, rebalances, diversifies internationally (India above all), and uses valuation only as a secondary check inside a trend.
