# 133 - Jan van Eck- Macro Forces Shape Markets

**Speakers:** Jan van Eck (CEO, Van Eck, guest); Alex (host, Insightful Investor podcast)

**Summary:** Jan van Eck (CEO of Van Eck, $230bn AUM) argues big outside forces (fiscal and monetary policy, deregulation, technology, demographics) shape markets and that long-term rate regimes bias history. He stays fully invested in equities except in aggressive Fed tightening, prefers real assets to bonds as diversifier, rebalances, and uses valuation only as a secondary check inside trends. Views: India and international diversification, AI/tech judged by market structure and moats, caution on narrative stocks without valuation support.

**Approach:** global macro, discretionary top-down, fiscal and monetary policy, structural themes, real assets diversification, valuation within trend, rebalancing
