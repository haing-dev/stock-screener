# 2024 Trend Following Recap- Key Insights from Top Traders - Systematic Investor 327

**Speakers:** Neils Kostner Larsen (host); Katy Kaminski (quant researcher, trend following); Nick Baltas (quant, systematic strategies); Andrew Beer (trend replication manager); Rob Carver (systematic trader); Mark Rzepczynski (macro and trend researcher); Rich Brennan (trend follower); Alan Dunn (macro/systematic); Jim Kasang (macro/geopolitics panelist)

**Summary:** Nine-person year-end panel on 2024 trend following: slow, large-market trend beat fast; August V-shaped shock hurt medium speeds; dispersion driven by speed, universe, purity and de-risking. Also vol selling and structured products pinning indices, crowding, whether illiquid markets trend more, market efficiency, AI use, and 2024 mistakes.

**Approach:** trend following, managed futures, trend replication, volatility selling, cross-sectional reversion, single stock momentum, manager dispersion

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** The panel explains 2024 trend following as a slow-and-large-market year: only equities and a few commodities trended, policy-expectation noise punished fast and medium speeds, and August's V-shaped shock hurt medium-speed models. Dispersion among managers came from speed, universe, purity and de-risking, while vol selling and structured products pinned the index; the panel debated crowding, whether illiquid markets trend more, market efficiency and AI.
