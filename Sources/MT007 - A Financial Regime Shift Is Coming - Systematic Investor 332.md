# A Financial Regime Shift Is Coming - Systematic Investor 332

**Speakers:** Mark (guest, quantitative macro researcher and professor, Boston); Alan Dunne (guest host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Top Traders Unplugged with Mark: January 2025 regime shift with record policy uncertainty but a quiet VIX, and the split between measurable risk (volatility) and non-measurable uncertainty. Mark argues trend following and flexible beta suit regime change, convergent multistrat strategies carry hidden risk, and models should be stress-tested on uncertainty spikes.

**Approach:** trend following, regime change, uncertainty indices, macro risk management, multi-strategy

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.88 - first reader caught the core mechanisms; missed VVIX data, the lagged-correlation forecast, the new-administration starting conditions, monetary-policy uncertainty as a term-premium source, the analogue-data caveat and the leverage-caution conclusion

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Mark argues that the Biden-to-Trump handover is a regime change that can be measured with newspaper-based uncertainty indices, which in January 2025 were at their highest since 2020 while the VIX stayed below 20; because risk has a measurable part (volatility) and a non-measurable part (uncertainty), volatility understates true risk and models built on stable correlations and mean-reverting equilibria are exposed. He therefore favours divergent, assumption-light strategies (trend following, flexible 'beta flex' exposure) over convergent multistrat books, wants models stress-tested on past uncertainty spikes and on nonlinearity/correlations going to one, and warns that banks extending more leverage to multistrats is dangerous when uncertainty is high.
