# AI, Inflation, and the Portfolio That Refuses to Sit Still - Systematic Investor - Ep.399

**Speakers:** Alan Dunn (co-host, rules-based/trend and macro investor; launching Regime Adaptive Fund); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged; transcript 'Neil Castro Blast')

**Summary:** Top Traders Unplugged Systematic Investor Ep.399 (May 2026): Dunn and Kaastrup-Larsen discuss AI and Iran-war supply shocks, trend-following status, Graham Capital's macro-across-monetary-regimes paper and AQR's Jordan Brooks interview. Dunn announces a Regime Adaptive Fund (40% equity / 20% bonds+gold / 40% trend risk). Main conclusions: rate volatility and dispersion drive trend/macro opportunity; trend is a transparent tactical allocation overlay; diversification brings its own periodic pain.

**Approach:** trend following, macro, return stacking, strategic vs tactical allocation, stagflation, portfolio construction

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.93 - first reader was thorough (84 claims); missed AI import leakage, military-spending inflation, the speakers' disagreement on inflation vs rate drivers, inflation surprise, vol-target dispersion and strategy-specific stress correlation.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Alan Dunn argues that markets face a collision of two unprecedented shocks, an AI positive supply shock that also brings an inflationary capex demand impulse and an Iran-war energy supply shock, which leaves central banks in a dilemma, pushes governments toward fiscal dominance, and can hit stocks and bonds together; that the answer is a portfolio pairing strategic beta with a large trend sleeve that works as transparent tactical asset allocation. Niels adds that trend and macro opportunity may depend on inflation instability rather than rate activity, that markets are increasingly flow- and positioning-driven, and that unconstrained trend including equities suits that world. Dunn announces the Regime Adaptive Fund built on this logic.
