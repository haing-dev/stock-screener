**Speakers:** Alan Dunn (co-host, rules-based/trend and macro investor; launching Regime Adaptive Fund); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged; transcript 'Neil Castro Blast')

**Summary:** Top Traders Unplugged Systematic Investor Ep.399 (May 2026): Dunn and Kaastrup-Larsen discuss AI and Iran-war supply shocks, trend-following status, Graham Capital's macro-across-monetary-regimes paper and AQR's Jordan Brooks interview. Dunn announces a Regime Adaptive Fund (40% equity / 20% bonds+gold / 40% trend risk).
