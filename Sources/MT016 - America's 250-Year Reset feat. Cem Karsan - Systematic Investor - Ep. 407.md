# America's 250-Year Reset feat. Cem Karsan - Systematic Investor - Ep. 407

**Speakers:** Cem Karsan (guest, options and volatility strategist); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Karsan argues positioning and flows dominate markets and that the US is moving toward state-directed capitalism: backstopping Treasuries and a sovereign wealth fund buying equities by printing money, with a Warsh/Bessent good cop-bad cop split. He maps a three-legged stool (flows, macro, administration) onto the calendar: calm to September, volatile to the midterms, weaker post-midterm until a mid-2027 push; plus the 'summer of George' vol-compression and dispersion regime. Opening segment covers June 2026 CTA performance and vol-manager frustration.

**Approach:** positioning/reflexivity, options/volatility flows, policy regime analysis, trend following update, seasonality/calendar

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.85 - first reader caught all main mechanisms and calendar calls; missed mostly caveats, host challenges (Norway, China property, FOMC) and a few supporting data points.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Karsan argues that positioning, not news, drives markets, and that converging pressures (debt, populism, structural inflation, a too-big-to-fail equity market, the China currency contest) will push the US into state-directed capitalism: a Treasury backstop plus a money-printed sovereign wealth fund buying trillions of equities, with Warsh and Bessent as good cop and bad cop. He maps flows, macro and the administration onto a calendar (calm to September, volatile to the midterms, weaker after, with government buying from after the midterms to mid-2027) and says not to bet against a state-managed market in the short to medium term. The host Kaastrup-Larsen mostly probes the plan (Norway analogy, China's property bust, FOMC mechanics) and supplies the June 2026 CTA data.
