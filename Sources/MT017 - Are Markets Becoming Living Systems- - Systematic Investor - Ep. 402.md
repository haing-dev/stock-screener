# Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402

**Speakers:** Richard Brennan (guest, systematic trend-following manager and researcher); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Brennan argues markets are complex adaptive systems whose price behaviour is driven by endogenous feedback; passive investing (price-insensitive, about 60% of US equity funds) dilutes balancing agents and thins the price-sensitive float, making trends, overshoots and cascades more likely. He presents an agent-based model (about 25% chartists produces a phase transition) matching the statistical fingerprint of 68 futures markets over 40 years, concluding trend following harvests a structural, hardening feature. Also covers May 2026 CTA numbers, a SocGen sentiment survey and ATR sizing.

**Approach:** trend following, complex adaptive systems, passive investing structure, market inelasticity, agent-based modelling

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Richard Brennan (Systematic Investor Ep. 402, recorded about 27 May 2026) argues that markets are complex adaptive systems whose trends, crashes and volatility clustering emerge from reinforcing and balancing feedback among agents rather than from news or fair value. Passive investing (about 60% of US equity fund assets, more with benchmark-huggers) is price-insensitive: it dilutes the balancing agents and thins the price-sensitive float, making directional moves, overshoots and cascades more likely and raising the price impact per dollar of flow (multiplier about 5, range 3-7). His agent-based model (about 20-25% chartists triggers a phase transition) reproduces the Hurst/kurtosis fingerprint of 68 futures markets over 40 years, which he takes to mean trend following harvests a structural, hardening feature, not a temporary inefficiency. Kaastrup-Larsen probes with structured products and pod shops and reports May 2026 CTA data and a SocGen survey.

**Worldview premises**
- Markets are complex adaptive systems: many participants with partial information, each adapting to the others and changing the environment; prices, trends and cascades emerge from interactions, not from any one decision. A market is not a reflection of fundamental value or the economy. _[theoretical, stated; support: Two years of his own work on complex adaptive systems; the starling murmuration analogy.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C02-2|C02-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C10-1|C10-1]])
- The agent ecology (who is at the table: value, passive, momentum, vol-targeting, dealers) determines market behaviour; the key question about a market is which agents populate it and how they interact, not fair value, the macro backdrop or central bank action. Fundamentals, the economy and the banking system are loosely coupled autonomous systems, not antecedents of the market. _[theoretical, stated; support: Asserted from his framework; he says the 'binding correlation' between systems is not always true on evidence.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C02-3|C02-3]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C03-1|C03-1]])
- What matters about an agent is whether and in which direction it conditions behaviour on price: momentum algos, vol-targeting funds and stop losses amplify moves; value investors, counter-trend traders, market makers and rebalancing pensions absorb them; passive allocates by index rule regardless of price. _[theoretical, stated; support: Classification of agent types; no data.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C03-2|C03-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C03-3|C03-3]])
- Passive investing is rational for individuals, but the aggregate effect of price-insensitive capital changes market structure; the argument is structural, not about whether to own index funds. Passive holds about 60% of US equity fund assets, with price-insensitive ownership higher once benchmark-hugging institutions are included, and the same logic extends across ETFs in fixed income, commodities, currency and multi-asset products. _[institutional, stated; support: Commonly cited 60% figure; he uses US equity data because it is cleanest.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C01-1|C01-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C02-1|C02-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C04-1|C04-1]])
- The most important structural change of the past two decades is the shift from price-sensitive to price-insensitive ownership, more than HFT, algorithmic trading or mega-cap dominance, which are downstream effects. _[theoretical, stated; support: Asserted; mega-cap concentration is described as an emergent product of participant feedback.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C05-4|C05-4]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C10-X1|C10-X1]])
- Classical equilibrium finance treats markets like physical systems (clocks) and deviations as noise, but participants observe and imitate each other, so this is a category error; the model persists because it yields spreadsheets and portfolio methods, whereas the feedback view requires a research programme. _[theoretical, stated; support: Argument by analogy; no survey of the finance literature.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C10-2|C10-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C11-1|C11-1]])
- News is a trigger, not the main driver: most price movement cannot be linked to news, and the size and duration of a move depends on internal feedback ('the match starts the fire; the dryness of the wood determines its extent'). _[empirical, stated; support: Microstructure research (Bouchaud, Gabaix, Cohen) finding that most price movement is unattributable to news and large moves occur without proportional catalysts.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C11-2|C11-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C11-3|C11-3]])
- Reflexivity: price-sensitive participants' reactions reshape the market they observe, a recursive loop in which the output of the system becomes its next input; passive participants are not part of that loop. The cascade matters more than the spark, and trends outlast any catalyst. _[theoretical, stated; support: Soros reflexivity; the sequence breakout, momentum buying, volatility change, vol targeting, rebalancing.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C12-1|C12-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C12-2|C12-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C12-3|C12-3]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C12-R1|C12-R1]])
- Markets overshoot fundamental value because fundamentals are a separate system and nothing in the market forces convergence; the largest moves occur when the architecture is sensitised, without proportional catalysts. _[theoretical, stated; support: Follows from the autonomy of fundamentals premise; no test cited beyond the microstructure literature.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C12-4|C12-4]])
- Reinforcing-dominated regimes trend and balancing-dominated regimes range; when influence shifts rapidly between them, phase transitions occur and volatility surges. _[theoretical, stated; support: Verbal model; the agent-based simulation is the demonstration.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C14-1|C14-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C13-1|C13-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C13-2|C13-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C04-3|C04-3]])

- Trend following harvests a structural feature of markets (the feedback engine) that has never weakened since 1985, rather than a temporary inefficiency; there is no era of available data in which markets behave as the equilibrium model says. _[empirical, stated; support: His own 68-market, 40-year fingerprint study (rolling 5-year Hurst 0.62-0.82) and the simulation.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C18-1|C18-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C17-4|C17-4]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C18-R2|C18-R2]])
- Correlation is a between-instrument diagnostic and variance a within-instrument control; a trend follower needs both, but the engine already runs on variance (ATR sizing). _[experience, stated; support: His own program design.]_ ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C09-1|C09-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C09-2|C09-2]])

**Chain: Passive growth dilutes balancing feedback, so trends and cascades run further**
1. Rising share of passive vehicles and benchmark-hugging capital allocating by index rule → Price-sensitive agents shrink as a share of the whole (not in absolute dollars): Passive displaces them; it does not participate in price discovery. ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C03-3|C03-3]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C04-2|C04-2]])
2. Convergent (value, mean-reversion, counter-trend, market maker) share falls while divergent traders plus passive and forced index flows remain or grow → Less capital pushes back on price in motion: Passive amplifies the existing distribution, so it sits on the reinforcing side. ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C04-4|C04-4]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C05-1|C05-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C14-2|C14-2]])
3. Weaker balancing feedback → More extended directional moves, overshoot and cascades: Not because reinforcing forces are stronger, but because balancing forces are weaker. ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C05-2|C05-2]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C14-3|C14-3]])
4. Directional moves and cascades → More favourable environment for trend following (and S&P rising while fundamentals weaken): Resistance is structurally smaller. ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C14-3|C14-3]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C18-2|C18-2]])

**Chain: Market inelasticity: thin price-sensitive float magnifies flow**
1. Most shares held by price-insensitive holders → The pool absorbing active buying or selling is a smaller fraction of float: Active flow must find a counterparty among the price-sensitive fraction. ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C05-3|C05-3]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C14-4|C14-4]])
2. Thin counterparty pool → Price must move further per dollar of flow: $1 net buying lifts market cap about $5, $1 selling destroys about $5 (range 3-7): Inelastic markets; much larger than classical finance assumed. ([[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C15-1|C15-1]], [[MT017 - Are Markets Becoming Living Systems- - Systematic Investor - Ep. 402#^MT017-C15-2|C15-2]])
3. Large multiplier → Feedback-driven trends are larger than the flows that started them; the same dollar moves price more than 20 years ago: Fewer price-sensitive participants means less capacity to absorb flow. - Fails when: He concedes the 5x multiplier comes from a small set of studies and the exact value depends on methodology.

**Chain: Agent-based model reproduces the statistical fingerprint of real futures markets**

Model with chartists (divergent, reinforcing) and fundamentalists (convergent, balancing) and one knob for chartist share → With zero chartists the market behaves like classical finance (Hurst about 0.54, excess kurtosis -0.2, no five-sigma events, no vol clustering): No reinforcing feedback. ### Forward-looking inputs (reader synthesis)
(reader) Brennan's leading inputs are structural rather than short-term: the share of price-insensitive ownership (passive and benchmark-hugging capital), the balance between reinforcing and balancing agents, the price-sensitive float (inelasticity multiplier), calm-regime leverage and structured-product issuance (fragility build-up), and the cross-market fraction sharing the majority feedback sign. Horizon is multi-year to decades for the passive thesis; the breakout risk after calm regimes is of unspecified timing. Short-term inputs mentioned are the Trend Barometer (leading his lagging funds) and the SocGen survey (sentiment, lagging performance).

### Reader inferences (not stated by the speakers)
- (reader) The map of Brennan's causal story is: passive share up, balancing share down, thin float, larger trends and cascades, trend-following edge persists or rises; each link rests on a different level of evidence (a statistic, a structural argument, a simulation, a cited paper).
- (reader) The simulation is used to argue sufficiency, but the practical claim (trend following is structural and hardening) needs the further claim that passive increases the reinforcing share relative to balancing share in the real world.
- (reader) Brennan's call is conditional on regime: crises tilt toward positive feedback (trends) and QE-suppressed regimes toward negative feedback (oscillation), so trend followers face weaker conditions in suppressed-volatility regimes despite the structural story.
- (reader) The extinction-level unwind and structured-product fragility are the two stated ways the thesis could fail or become violent, with demography as the slow reversal.
