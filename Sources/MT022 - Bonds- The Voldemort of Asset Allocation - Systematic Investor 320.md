# Bonds- The Voldemort of Asset Allocation - Systematic Investor 320

**Speakers:** Andrew Beer (co-founder, DBi, managed futures replication); Alan Dunne (host, standing in for Niels Kaastrup-Larsen)

**Summary:** Andrew Beer discusses trend-following performance in 2024, the structure and distribution of the CTA industry, managed futures ETFs and replication, and bonds as the diversifier that stopped working. Main conclusions: trend returns are noisy, replication and mutual funds track the industry closely, and 60/40 is shifting toward 50/30/20.

**Approach:** trend following, replication, managed futures ETFs, bonds, asset allocation, industry structure
