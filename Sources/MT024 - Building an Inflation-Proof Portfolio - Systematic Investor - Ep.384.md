# Building an Inflation-Proof Portfolio - Systematic Investor - Ep.384

**Speakers:** Yoav Git (co-host, Gresham, fixed income trend manager and researcher); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Yoav Git and Niels Kaastrup-Larsen discuss early-2026 trends (Japan yields, gold, natural gas, EM FX) and Git's ERAS paper: bonds fail in rising inflation, commodity trend is the mirror image, and a 50/50 mix gives CPI+4% in all regimes. They also review Rob Carver's blog on carry and autocorrelation.

**Approach:** trend following, inflation regimes, commodity trend, bonds, carry, autocorrelation, Japan

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.90 - first reader captured the ERAS regime results, autocorrelation and carry arguments well; misses were the host's falling-rates challenge to bond autocorrelation, the long-history caveat, fixed income whipsaw, and a few secondary points.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Yoav Git argues that bonds fail as the defensive building block when inflation rises (CPI minus, and correlated with equities above about 4.5% inflation), commodity trend is their mirror image, and a 50/50 mix delivers roughly CPI+4% in every inflation regime, so allocators need not forecast inflation. He adds that trend profits depend on positive autocorrelation, which differs by asset class and over time, so trend should be tilted by market structure (carry, supply shocks, physical players). Niels frames the same world through geopolitical breakdown and robustness: build portfolios of truly uncorrelated strategies that survive five to seven years, and expect large trend opportunities if world order breaks down.
