# Børge Brende - Podcast - In Good Company - Norges Bank Investment Management

**Speakers:** Børge Brende (president and CEO, World Economic Forum); Nicolai Tangen (CEO, Norges Bank Investment Management, interviewer)

**Summary:** Interview of WEF president Børge Brende by NBIM CEO Nicolai Tangen on a fragmenting world order, resilient growth, the US, Europe and China, energy security and AI. Brende expects no full decoupling, more secure supply chains, high debt limiting fiscal response, and technology-driven productivity keeping growth from stagnating.
