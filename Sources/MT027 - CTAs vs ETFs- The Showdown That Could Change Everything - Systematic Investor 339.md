# CTAs vs ETFs- The Showdown That Could Change Everything - Systematic Investor 339

**Speakers:** Andrew Beer (DBi, replication ETF manager); Tom Roble (SG Prime Services, SG CTA indices); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, hedge fund manager)

**Summary:** Top Traders Unplugged Systematic Investor 339 (March 2025): CTA index performance, regime and time-frame diversification, stacked managed-futures products, replication versus managers and BlackRock's ETF entry. Conclusions: trend and broad CTA indices are nearly the same; short-term models help only at inflections; replication and ETFs are not a threat except BlackRock; bonds' failure as diversifier drives trend demand.

**Approach:** trend following, CTA replication, managed futures ETFs, regime and time-frame diversification, portable alpha

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Beer, Roble and the host agree that trend followers and the broad CTA index are nearly the same bet (98% correlated, differing mainly by leverage) and that managed futures earns its place as a diversifier/completion strategy, not a standalone Sharpe story, a case strengthened because bonds stopped diversifying equities after 2022. They differ on how to adapt: Roble stresses regime identification and multi-time-frame model sets, Beer argues short-term models only help at inflection points and cost Sharpe, and the host warns against over-cleverness and prefers experience; Beer defends top-down replication as an adaptive, cheap structural-alpha trade while seeing stacked ETF/mutual-fund products as tax- and fee-inefficient and BlackRock's ETF as the only real threat to CTAs.
