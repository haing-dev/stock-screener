# Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Investor 345

**Speakers:** Nick Baltas (co-host, QIS/systematic strategist at a bank); Niels Kaastro Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Niels Kaastro Larsen and Nick Baltas review the April 2025 tariff shock: hedge fund positioning, why trend followers struggled with V-shaped reversals, CTA dispersion and crowding. They then discuss pension fund bond allocations, economic-trend (macro data) signals versus price trend, and a Man Group paper on trend's behaviour before and after equity peaks. Conclusions: trend works in sustained drawdowns and not in sharp reversals, dispersion comes from speed and market differences, and new signals need caution beyond backtests.

**Approach:** trend following, CTA dispersion, crowding / positioning, economic trend, QIS, tail events

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.90 - core ideas (V-shaped reversals, dispersion drivers, correlation by horizon, economic trend, Man paper) all captured; missed some positioning/sector details, the career-risk explanation of bond-heavy pensions, PE smoothing and the 2025 economic-trend performance; one measure conflated.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Nick Baltas argues that the April 2025 tariff shock produced V/W-shaped, sentiment-driven reversions across equities, dollar, oil and yields at once, which hurt medium-speed trend models twice, while trend followers are price takers that earn only when drawdowns are sustained; the Man Group 'patience' paper adds that trend also does not earn in the three months before an equity peak because positions reshuffle. He explains manager dispersion by speed, markets, static versus dynamic sizing, signal mix and discretionary risk cuts, says long-run correlation is high because signals agree most of the time, doubts winner-chasing between trend managers, and sees no crowded trend position except gold. Niels (host) frames the episode with a rules-based edge in an information flood, the one-man-moves-markets problem, gold and bonds not preserving real value over long periods, scepticism about backtests and new economic-data strategies, and the case for holding trend through reversals.

**Chain: Why a V-shaped event hurts medium-speed trend twice (April 2025)**
1. liberation-day announcement drops equities → medium-speed models need days to reduce risk, so they cut after the drop: signals and position changes lag price ([[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C08-3|C08-3]])
2. markets rebound after a couple of days → the models re-add exposure into the rebound, or the reversion arrives as they reposition: the reduction and the re-establishment are both late ([[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C08-3|C08-3]], [[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C08-4|C08-4]])
3. equities, dollar, oil (OPEC) and yields all reverted together → a broad reshuffling caught trend followers off guard: synchronous reversal across asset classes ([[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C08-4|C08-4]])
4. different speeds → fast programs lose less first but keep bleeding, slow ones lose more first then catch up: path depends on how quickly signals flip ([[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C09-1|C09-1]], [[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C09-2|C09-2]], [[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C10-1|C10-1]])
- Evidence: April 2025 index numbers (SG Trend -5.15% MTD, BTOP50 -3.86%, short-term traders -0.41%)
- Fails when: the drawdown becomes sustained, when trend positions finally flip and earn; the positioning after the reshuffle may be 'just rounding' (many signals crossed zero) or a new trajectory.

**Chain: Why trend does not help before or just after an equity peak**
1. S&P falls sharply after a rally → trend followers are not positioned for it: positions are long after the rally; price takers ([[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C16-4|C16-4]], [[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C17-1|C17-1]])

3. in the three months before the peak other asset-class trends cease or reestablish → no significant trend performance then either: the algorithm reshuffles positions before the turn ([[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C17-3|C17-3]], [[MT039 - Crowded Trades, Fluctuating Markets- Navigating the New Era of Investing - Systematic Inve#^MT039-C18-1|C18-1]])

- Evidence: Man Group 'Why patience matters during market stress' (2002, 2008, 2022 scenarios) and Kaminski's work; examples Nov 2021, Aug 2007
- Fails when: the equity decline is a V-shape that reverses before positions flip (Volmageddon, SVB, Aug 2024, April 2025).
