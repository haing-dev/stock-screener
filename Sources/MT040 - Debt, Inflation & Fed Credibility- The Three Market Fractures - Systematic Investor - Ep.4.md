# Debt, Inflation & Fed Credibility- The Three Market Fractures - Systematic Investor - Ep.415

**Speakers:** Alan Dunne (co-host, macro); Niels Kaastro Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Niels Kaastro Larsen and Alan Dunne (August 2026) cover the US yen intervention, Fed credibility under Warsh, rising US yields, and August trend-following results.
