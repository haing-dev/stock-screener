# Diversification is more important than ever! - Systematic Investor - Ep.385

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged; caption 'Neils Krup Larson'); Katy Kaminski (AlphaSimplex, trend following researcher; caption 'Katie Kaminsky')

**Summary:** Top Traders Unplugged, late January 2026: weekly systematic investor review of trend following. Metals and multi-sector trends drove a strong start to 2026. Katy Kaminski's annual paper on dispersion: speed, market set and volatility estimation explain return differences.

**Approach:** trend following, CTA dispersion, volatility sizing, crisis alpha, commodities

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Katy Kaminski and Niels Kaastrup-Larsen argue that trend following is strongest when many sectors trend at once (metals, a weak dollar, equities in January 2026) and that diversification across markets, speeds and volatility estimators matters more than ever, because the returns of CTAs diverge mainly through design choices (speed, market set, vol sizing) whose best setting flips from year to year. Both treat trend following as a cyclical, boom-bust and disruption-loving strategy that benefits from regime change (deglobalization, geopolitics, a possible commodity supercycle) and is not crowded.
