# Doug King on Commodities, Risk Control & the Illusion of Trends - Open Interest - Ep.20

**Speakers:** Doug King (founder, Merchant Commodity Fund; former Cargill head of petroleum trading); Moritz Seibert (host, Open Interest; caption 'Morett Sebert')

**Summary:** Open Interest ep.20 (Dec 2025): discretionary commodity fund manager on bottom-up supply and demand trading, cash-versus-futures signals and risk control. Conclusions: commodities are cyclical because of innovation, avoid passive index, rely on basis and physical data, and use volatility targeting with drawdown cuts.
