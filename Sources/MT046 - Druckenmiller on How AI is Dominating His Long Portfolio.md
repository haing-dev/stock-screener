## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Druckenmiller separates a long-run US fiscal/entitlement problem (not tradable now) from his near-term central case that 11 years of free money followed by a 500bp hike year means more shoes to drop and a hard landing that is delayed, not cancelled, by liquidity injections that are now reversing. He holds AI leaders (Nvidia) as a real secular theme in the long book, is sceptical of China and cautiously constructive on Japan, and says he has no fat pitch yet: with an unanalyzable roadmap he will wait for liquidity changes to create better setups over 8-24 months rather than bet while down.
