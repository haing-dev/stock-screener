# ETFs, Size, and the Commodity Problem - Systematic Investor 340

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged); Alan Dunne (macro/CTA analyst, Archive Capital)

**Summary:** Top Traders Unplugged, March 2025: Fed projections and German fiscal shift, Mar-a-Lago accord and dollar risk, trend-following state, Global Investment Returns Yearbook, Quantica paper on trend-following capacity in commodities and ETF drag.

**Approach:** trend following, global macro, CTA capacity, long-run returns, dollar regime

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.89 - first reader was thorough on the Fed, Germany, dollar, Yearbook and Quantica capacity material; missed secondary points (defence spending leakage, Alan's verdict on a Mar-a-Lago accord, short- vs long-term trend at reversals, real vs nominal growth in 2020-24, five-year look-back method, sponsor bias caveat).

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Alan Dunne argues that tariffs and the Fed's stagflationary tilt, German fiscal expansion, and talk of a Mar-a-Lago accord are reshaping dollar, yield and growth dynamics, and that century-long return data (Global Investment Returns Yearbook) show how regime-dependent equity and bond returns are. Both speakers then use Quantica's capacity paper to argue that size and low-fee replication products constrain commodity diversification and Sharpe, while Alan adds that risk allocation, not market count, drives portfolio Sharpe.
