# Episode 17- Howard Marks - Co-Chairman of Oaktree Capital Management

**Speakers:** Howard Marks (co-chairman, Oaktree Capital); Amir (host, Generating Alpha podcast)

**Summary:** Howard Marks on his career from Citi equity research to high yield and distressed debt at Oaktree. Approach is value and risk-focused credit investing. Main points: price matters more than asset quality, quantify risk, find unpopular niches, edge needs insight beyond common data.

**Approach:** distressed debt, value investing, credit, risk management, contrarian

### C03
