## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Marks argues that investment success comes from price paid relative to value rather than asset quality, from quantified and compensated risk-taking in neglected niches, and from insight beyond data everyone shares. His career (Nifty Fifty loss, high yield, distressed debt) and Oaktree's 'demographic luck' illustrate these points.
