# Episode 5- Jim Rogers - Legendary Investor & Co-Founder of Quantum Fund

**Speakers:** Jim Rogers (co-founder of Quantum Fund, creator of Rogers International Commodities Index); Amir and Owen (hosts, Generating Alpha podcast)

**Summary:** Jim Rogers, Quantum Fund co-founder, on his career, travels and views. Discretionary global multi-asset long/short with commodities. Main views: China rising again, US debt and money printing are long-term problems, agriculture to recover; avoid hot tips and invest in what you know.

**Approach:** discretionary global macro, commodities, contrarian, fiscal debt

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.80 - mostly biography and life advice; first reader caught all substantive views (China cycle, timing lesson, multi-asset long/short, know commodity prices, agriculture, money printing, US debt, no hot tips), missing only the Japan-consensus contrast, the neglected-data edge and the 'find the change' principle.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Rogers argues that investors win by finding structural change early and by knowing the world broadly, including commodity prices: China is rising again (a recurring cycle), agriculture is a neglected disaster sector that should improve, and the US, now the largest debtor nation, faces a Britain-style debt crisis while money printing rarely works long term. He insists on investing only in what you know, ignoring hot tips, staying in your own strategy and staying humble (the market is smarter than you, timing matters), while admitting he has no clear loss-cutting rule.
