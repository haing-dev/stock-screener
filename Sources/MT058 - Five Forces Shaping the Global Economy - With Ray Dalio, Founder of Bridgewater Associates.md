# Five Forces Shaping the Global Economy - With Ray Dalio, Founder of Bridgewater Associates

**Speakers:** Ray Dalio (founder, Bridgewater Associates); Interviewer (host, Money Maze Podcast)

**Summary:** Nov 2023 interview on Dalio book The Changing World Order: five forces (debt, internal conflict, great-power conflict, nature, technology) repeating from 500 years of empires, a 1930s-45 analogy for debt monetization, populism and US-China rivalry, plus his process lessons (diversification up to 80% risk cut, systematic rules, believability weighting).
