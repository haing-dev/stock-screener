## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Ray Dalio argues that history repeats through five forces (debt, internal political conflict, great-power conflict, acts of nature, technology), and that the 1930-45 analogue (debt creation and monetization, populist left-right conflict, a rising power challenging the existing order) is happening again, with unwanted government debt forcing central banks to choose between sharply higher rates and printing money. His investor response is process-driven: diversify well across assets and countries, favour places with sound finances, low internal conflict and neutrality, stay humble through stress-testing and believability-weighted decisions, and write down and systematise decision rules.
