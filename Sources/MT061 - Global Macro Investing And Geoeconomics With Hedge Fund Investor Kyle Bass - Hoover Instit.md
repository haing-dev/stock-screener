**Speakers:** Kyle Bass (founder Hayman Capital, hedge fund investor); John Hartley (host, Hoover Institution)

**Summary:** Kyle Bass on how he found the subprime, Japan and sovereign debt trades by following leverage to balance sheets, and his geopolitical view that China will move on Taiwan within years. ## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Bass argues that his macro successes (subprime 2006-08, Greece and Ireland, Japan) all came from tracing leverage from private to bank to sovereign balance sheets until one fails, and that the current system of central-bank balance-sheet growth and deficits is inflating assets faster than wages, damaging demographics and social cohesion, and making conflict more likely. He applies the same architecture-first method to China, concluding that a Chinese move on Taiwan within two to three years is likely, that Chinese assets are uninvestable for Westerners, and that the US remains the best place for long-term capital while Europe is not.
