# Goldman Sachs’s Chief Global Equity Strategist, Peter Oppenheimer, Discusses Macro and Markets

**Speakers:** Peter Oppenheimer (chief global equity strategist, Goldman Sachs); Simon (host, Money Maze podcast)

**Summary:** October 2022 interview: Oppenheimer frames the 2022 bear market as cyclical (inflation-driven rate rises) rather than structural, lists conditions for a market bottom, and explains the rate-driven growth-vs-value reversal. Conclusions: dollar strong near term but PPP-overvalued, commodities in a super cycle, higher cost of capital means lower returns and more alpha.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Peter Oppenheimer (Goldman Sachs chief global equity strategist) argues that the 2022 bear market is a cyclical downturn, not a structural one: inflation from demand plus supply shocks forced a whatever-it-takes central bank tightening, pushing up the level and speed of rates and real yields and hitting equity valuations, but healthy private-sector balance sheets mean it should be around 30% deep rather than 60%. His base case for equities is that near-term pressure is still down until rates and inflation peak and growth deterioration slows; beyond that a higher-rate, less-globalised world means lower index returns, more stock-specific alpha, diversified exposure, a commodity super cycle and a strong-then-easing dollar.
