# Graham Capital Management's Tropin on Why Macro is Killing It

**Speakers:** Ken Tropin (founder, Graham Capital Management); interviewer (Bloomberg Live host, name not given)

**Summary:** Bloomberg Live interview (Oct 2022) with the founder of Graham Capital, a discretionary plus quant macro fund. Argues macro is thriving because central banks are moving rates sharply and diverging. Expects sticky inflation to 2025, long bonds mispriced, gold failed as hedge.

**Approach:** discretionary macro, quant macro, central bank divergence, FX trend

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.85 - first reader caught all main views and methods; missed the 2022 return data point, the Fed over-tightening counter-view, front-end pricing path, real-rate view and the sterling neutral call

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Ken Tropin (Graham Capital) argues macro is thriving in Oct 2022 because central banks are moving rates far more than in 2010-2021 and diverging from each other, which produces FX, rates and equity volatility to trade. He expects sticky inflation (target not reached before 2025, if then), a Fed that has to keep tightening into recession, a mispriced long end (no term premium), and range-bound equities.

**Chain: Why persistent inflation leads to higher long yields and a steeper curve**
1. inflation lasts three to five years because supply side and wages are outside the Fed's control → longer-dated bonds carry duration risk that is not priced: term premium is 'completely missing' at present ([[MT066 - Graham Capital Management's Tropin on Why Macro is Killing It#^MT066-C02-4|C02-4]], [[MT066 - Graham Capital Management's Tropin on Why Macro is Killing It#^MT066-C04-3|C04-3]])
2. missing term premium → 10-year at 3.95% is mispriced, 50-100bp too low, and the slightly inverted curve re-steepens: front end already prices a peak near 4.75% and easing to 4.25% by mid-2023, so the long end must reprice ([[MT066 - Graham Capital Management's Tropin on Why Macro is Killing It#^MT066-C04-3|C04-3]], [[MT066 - Graham Capital Management's Tropin on Why Macro is Killing It#^MT066-C04-X1|C04-X1]])

- Fails when: Inflation falls quickly to target (he judges this unlikely) or a recession forces rapid central-bank easing.

**Chain: Sticky inflation delays Fed easing**
