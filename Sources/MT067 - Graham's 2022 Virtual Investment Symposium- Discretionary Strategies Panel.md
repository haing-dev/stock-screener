# Graham's 2022 Virtual Investment Symposium- Discretionary Strategies Panel

**Speakers:** Pablo Calderini (president and CIO, Graham Capital); Katie Woodridge (chief economist); Peter Chapson (senior discretionary PM); David Keelan (senior discretionary PM); Chris McCann (chief risk officer)

**Summary:** Graham Capital 2022 symposium panel on discretionary macro. Argues inflation ended the 2010-20 anomaly of suppressed volatility; central bank divergence, sticky inflation and collapsed stock-bond diversification create opportunity. Heavy emphasis on risk management: vol sizing, shrinking into events, stress tests over VaR.

**Approach:** discretionary macro, central bank watching, risk management

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.86 - first reader covered nearly all mechanisms and risk methods; missed the beta outlook, PM allocation by pairwise correlation, BoJ YCC-vs-intervention conflict, sizing-vs-noise rule and bottom-up process; several speaker attributions in C07-C09 were wrong

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Graham's discretionary macro team argues that persistent, broad inflation ended the 2010-20 regime of suppressed volatility, the Fed put and negative stock-bond correlation, so central bank reaction functions become the main source of opportunity and risk (terminal rate higher than expected, Europe tightening into recession, Japan's yield curve control unsustainable). They add that in this regime risk is managed by volatility-unit sizing, cutting books where cross-asset trades collapse into one, scaling down into events, and daily independent risk review.
