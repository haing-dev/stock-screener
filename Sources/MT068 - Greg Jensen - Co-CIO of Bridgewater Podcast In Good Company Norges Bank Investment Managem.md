# Greg Jensen - Co-CIO of Bridgewater   Podcast   In Good Company   Norges Bank Investment Management

**Speakers:** Greg Jensen (co-CIO, Bridgewater); interviewer (Norges Bank Investment Management podcast host)

**Summary:** NBIM podcast with Bridgewater co-CIO Greg Jensen (Nov 2025). Argues modern mercantilism, the AI capex boom and record US capital concentration are the three themes; mercantilism and military spending are inflationary, fiscal limits make bonds a weaker diversifier. Advocates global diversification and survival across scenarios; describes Bridgewater's compounded-knowledge and AI-agent process.

**Approach:** global macro, systematic macro, risk parity / diversification, AI agent investing, fiscal limits
