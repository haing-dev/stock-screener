## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Greg Jensen argues the investing world is being reshaped by three things: a shift from 1980s free-market policy to modern mercantilism, an AI capex boom that is still early (the bubble is ahead, not behind), and record capital concentration in US assets. In his view mercantilism and military spending are inflationary (base case US inflation around 3%), fiscal limits are now binding in developed countries so bonds diversify less, and the right response is to survive across many scenarios through global diversification rather than pick winners. He pairs this with Bridgewater's process of compounding written, machine-readable knowledge, a separate AI investor, rare overrides of the system and radical transparency.
