# How Global Conflicts affect the Economy & 2024 Outlook - Systematic Investor 293

**Speakers:** Jim kazang (guest, volatility/options-focused rules-based investor); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Jim and Niels discuss April 2024: trend-following update, option positioning and deleveraging, and the 2024 outlook. Jim argues path and flows matter more than news or direction: market structure and Fed/Treasury liquidity favour upside with squeezes, while rates and a politicized Fed are longer-term risks. Calls for long-dated calls with short stock, hedging a late-May to June tail, and relative bets on Europe/China.

**Approach:** options/vol positioning, trend following, market structure flows, macro policy

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.87 - first reader caught nearly all main mechanisms; missed some secondary flow/event details (QRA, post-OpEx weakness window, call selling, rate-effect asymmetry).

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Jim (a volatility/options-focused rules-based investor) argues that market paths are determined by positioning, option-market structure (gamma, vanna/charm, expiry cycles), forced deleveraging and scheduled policy-liquidity events rather than by news or direction, so one should predict distributions and paths with if-then rules and express them with options. On that basis he expects the April 2024 pullback to end around the May Fed/Treasury events (tail risk window late May to June), 2024 to finish positive because the Fed and Treasury put a floor under markets and stimulate in an election year, a broader rally into year end, structural US weakness later, and relative bets on Europe and China. Niels, the host, adds the April trend-following scoreboard, observes that upside trends turn slowly while downside trends reverse quickly, and cautions that options are powerful in professional hands but cut both ways.
