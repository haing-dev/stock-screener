# Inside the Regime-Adaptive Portfolio - Systematic Investor - Ep.377

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged); Alan Dunne (Archive Capital, co-host; transcript spelling Alan Don)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor Ep. 377: trend following in a policy-shock year, 2025 performance and SocGen index changes, then Alan Dunne's paper on a regime-adaptive portfolio (40% growth, 40% adaptive, 20% diversifiers by risk). Conclusions: new macro regime (stickier inflation, high debt, positive stock-bond correlation) needs adaptive strategies; labels like alternative mislead.

**Approach:** trend following, regime change, risk-based asset allocation, adaptive portfolio

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Systematic Investor Ep.377 (recorded early Dec 2025): trend following had a flat 2025 after policy shocks, but choppy regimes have always ended when fundamentals shifted, so investors should hold long-term. Alan Dunne argues the world has moved from 40 years of disinflation to a regime of stickier inflation, higher debt, rising global yields and a more positive stock-bond correlation, and that portfolios built on old labels (alternative/traditional) are over-concentrated in growth and stable inflation. His remedy is a risk-based 40% growth / 40% adaptive (trend, macro) / 20% diversifier portfolio built with futures leverage and return stacking, which beats 60/40 mainly through smaller drawdowns.
