# Investing in the Age of AI, Uncertainty, and Data Overload - Systematic Investor - Ep.352

**Speakers:** Nick Baltas (systematic investor, guest co-host); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor Ep.352 (June 2025): Baltas and Kaastrup-Larsen discuss AI/data overload, trend following after the April 2025 V-shape, dynamic volatility targeting and leverage, institutional constraints (Junge article, low beta), Asness on missing the best days, and whether to time trend allocations. Conclusions: V-shapes hurt every trend speed, leverage into turning points matters, hold trend as a strategic allocation and do not exit after drawdowns.

**Approach:** trend following, volatility targeting, V-shape reversals, strategic allocation, AI and alternative data, institutional constraints

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.89 - first reader covered the vol-targeting, barometer, low-beta and timing arguments well; missed a few points (all-sector mispositioning in April, path-dependency of de-risking, pension asset mandates, 10% mandatory sleeve) and misattributed two claims.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Baltas and Kaastrup-Larsen argue that the April 2025 V-shape hurt trend followers of every speed because turning points cannot be predicted, so what matters is leverage going into them; Baltas floats a dynamic (lower when signal-to-noise is poor) volatility target while admitting it is hard to implement without hindsight bias. Both conclude that trend following should be held as a strategic, never-timed allocation (insurance-like), that institutional constraints explain much of what alternatives earn, and that systematic managers should neither react emotionally to a bad run nor be dogmatic about their models.

**Chain: Why V-shaped markets hurt trend followers and what leverage does to the loss**
1. Market falls then rebounds sharply (April 2025, Aug 2024, SVB) → Trend signals reverse after the first leg and again on the rebound: A V is two reversions back to back. ([[MT087 - Investing in the Age of AI, Uncertainty, and Data Overload - Systematic Investor - Ep.352#^MT087-C05-1|C05-1]])
2. Positions were wrongly set in every asset class at once (oil, rates, dollar, equities) → No offsetting sector cushions the loss: Directional bets across the book were all against the turn. ([[MT087 - Investing in the Age of AI, Uncertainty, and Data Overload - Systematic Investor - Ep.352#^MT087-C05-X1|C05-X1]])
3. Faster signals → Lose less on the way down but recover less; slower signals ride the whole V: Different trajectories, similar end result; all are negative. ([[MT087 - Investing in the Age of AI, Uncertainty, and Data Overload - Systematic Investor - Ep.352#^MT087-C07-3|C07-3]])
