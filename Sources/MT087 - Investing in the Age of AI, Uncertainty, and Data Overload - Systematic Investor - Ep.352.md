# Investing in the Age of AI, Uncertainty, and Data Overload - Systematic Investor - Ep.352

**Speakers:** Nick Baltas (systematic investor, guest co-host); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor Ep.352 (June 2025): Baltas and Kaastrup-Larsen discuss AI/data overload, trend following after the April 2025 V-shape, dynamic volatility targeting and leverage, institutional constraints (Junge article, low beta), Asness on missing the best days, and whether to time trend allocations.
