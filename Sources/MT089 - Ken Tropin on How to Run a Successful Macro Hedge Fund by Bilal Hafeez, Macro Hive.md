# Ken Tropin on How to Run a Successful Macro Hedge Fund by Bilal Hafeez, Macro Hive

**Speakers:** Ken Tropin (founder, Graham Capital Management); Bilal Hafeez (host, Macro Hive)

**Summary:** Macro Hive interview (c. mid-2023) with Graham founder Ken Tropin on running a macro fund: career, macro as diversifier, why 2010-2021 was quiet and 2022 rich in central-bank action (30x more hikes), and his market views (short equities, weak dollar, steepener). Core ideas: macro/CTA is uncorrelated alpha whose opportunity set follows central bank activity and volatility; diversify alpha sources; daily risk meeting.
