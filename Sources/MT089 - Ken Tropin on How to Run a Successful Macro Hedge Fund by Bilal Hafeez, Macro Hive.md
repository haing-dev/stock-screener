**Summary:** Macro Hive interview (c. mid-2023) with Graham founder Ken Tropin on running a macro fund: career, macro as diversifier, why 2010-2021 was quiet and 2022 rich in central-bank action (30x more hikes), and his market views (short equities, weak dollar, steepener). ## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Ken Tropin (Graham Capital) argues macro funds earn their place as an uncorrelated diversifier, and that the opportunity set is governed by central-bank activity: 2010-2021 was starved of it (13 quarter-point hikes in 11 years by the Fed, ECB and BoE) and 2022 had about 30 times as much. A successful macro firm needs experience, talented and retained people, daily hands-on risk management, diversified alpha sources and constant innovation. His spring-2023 views: inflation is slow to return to 2%, equities are expensive and he leans short, the dollar is slightly weak, and a curve steepener is the best rates trade because duration risk is unpriced.
