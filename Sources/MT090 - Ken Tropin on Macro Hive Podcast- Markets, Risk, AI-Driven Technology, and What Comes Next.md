## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Ken Tropin (Graham Capital, about $20bn, roughly 75% of risk in macro) describes a conservative, innovation-minded philosophy: be tactical, cut risk when conviction is low, build talent and culture, and use AI and new quant tools. His highest-probability macro view in December 2025 is that term premium is underpriced because of the large US deficit, so US curve steepeners work, though timing is hard and policy pressure for lower rates has stalled it. He sees AI equities as real but expensive, a dollar in a trading range because every central bank is somewhat hawkish, and Japan as the surprising driver of global rates.
