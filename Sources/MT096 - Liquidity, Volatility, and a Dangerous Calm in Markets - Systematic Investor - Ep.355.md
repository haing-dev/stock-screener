# Liquidity, Volatility, and a Dangerous Calm in Markets - Systematic Investor - Ep.355

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, hedge fund manager); Alan Dunne (Dunne/Archive Capital, systematic macro commentator)

**Summary:** Weekly Systematic Investor episode (Jul 2025): trend-following H1 2025 performance update, bond/fiscal macro view, and review of Man and Capstone papers on whether trend following drawdowns are different this time. Conclusion: current drawdown is statistically normal; combine long-vol (first responder) with trend (second responder); family-office portfolios look diversified but are mostly equity beta.

**Approach:** trend following, CTA performance, fiscal deficits and yields, drawdown statistics, portfolio diversification
