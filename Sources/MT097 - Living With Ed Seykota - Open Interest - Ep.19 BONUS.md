# Living With Ed Seykota - Open Interest - Ep.19 BONUS

**Speakers:** Dave Drew (systematic trader, former apprentice of Ed Seykota); Moritz Seibert (host, Open Interest series)

**Summary:** Bonus part of an Open Interest interview: Dave Drew recounts six months as Ed Seykota's apprentice and what he learned. Seykota was a technical discretionary trader, not a systematic one; key lessons are matching style to psychology, optimal bet size with high heat, not tinkering or cutting size in drawdowns, sizing off closed-trade equity.

**Approach:** trading psychology, position sizing, drawdown management, discretionary vs systematic, trend following

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.9 - anecdote-heavy episode with few method ideas; first reader captured the sizing and psychology points, missed Drew's backtest-before-implement process, investor-suitability caveat, drawdown-behavior point and data cleaning.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Drew, Seykota's first apprentice, argues Seykota was a price-only discretionary trader, not the systematic trader Market Wizards implied, and that the transferable lessons are psychological and about sizing: fit style to temperament, bet bigger than feels comfortable but not to ruin, scale size with equity without extra drawdown cuts, and do not tinker in drawdowns.
