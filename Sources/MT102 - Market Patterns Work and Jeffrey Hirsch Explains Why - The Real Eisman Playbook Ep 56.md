# Market Patterns Work and Jeffrey Hirsch Explains Why - The Real Eisman Playbook Ep 56

**Speakers:** Jeffrey Hirsch (author, Stock Trader's Almanac; seasonality); Steve Eisman (host, Real Eisman Playbook)

**Summary:** Eisman interviews Jeffrey Hirsch (Stock Trader's Almanac, April 2026) on calendar and seasonal patterns: midterm/four-year cycle, best six months, January barometer, intraday and monthly flows, sector seasonals, Bitcoin, super boom. Patterns persist because humans (and the people who program algorithms) behave habitually and institutions rebalance on calendar dates. Main views: midterm-year bottom Q2-Q3 then strong Q4-Q2 rally; AI as the super-boom technology; credit cycle tightening.

**Approach:** seasonality, calendar effects, behavioral finance, four-year cycle, sector seasonals
