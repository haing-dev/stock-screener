## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** The guest (captioned 'Jem') argues that markets are driven by supply, demand and liquidity, not fundamentals, so 2025 resembles 1998-99: a liquidity-driven bubble kept aloft by structured-product volatility compression, hedge-fund and relative-value flows, momentum releveraging and an administration with a short-term incentive to keep asset prices up. Reflexive positioning (institutions underweight, a wall of worry, year-end flows) makes him bullish into mid-January with rising volatility on the upside, and he prefers owning cheap right-tail calls instead of stock, plus debasement hedges (gold, crypto). Inflation is the eventual check that the government cannot control.
