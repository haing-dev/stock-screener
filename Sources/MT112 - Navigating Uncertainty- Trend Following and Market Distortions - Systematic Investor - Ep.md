# Navigating Uncertainty- Trend Following and Market Distortions - Systematic Investor - Ep.349

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged); Mark Rzepczynski (caption: Mark Raspinski; systematic macro researcher and blogger)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor 349 (May 2025): trend following's difficult 2025 with V-shaped reversals, state-created uncertainty and no follow-through, vol and leverage lessons, and why safe assets (Treasuries) proved fickle. Conclusion: trend remains a dynamic safe-asset substitute, but crisis alpha depends on crisis type.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Trend following lost money in 2025 because the uncertainty was man-made (tariffs, policy), was switched on and off quickly by policy, and was resolved positively, so sell-offs reversed without the slow behavioural follow-through that trend needs. Rzepczynski's lesson is that crisis alpha depends on the type of crisis, that trend is long long-term volatility but short short-term volatility (stops), and that Treasuries proved a fickle safe asset, so a dynamic long/short strategy such as trend can serve as a substitute safe asset over long horizons.

**Chain: Why trend lost money in 2025**
