# New Challenges in the High Stakes World of Oil Trading - Oil & Money 2014

**Speakers:** Moderator (Energy Intelligence panel chair); Chris (Vitol, physical oil merchant); Pierre/Peter (hedge fund oil trader, caption spelling varies); Dr. Fesharaki (caption: the good doctor; energy consultant)

**Summary:** Oil and Money 2014 panel (Oct-Nov 2014): after the Brent fall, speakers explain a ~2 mb/d supply-demand swing from shale, Libya and weak demand, argue prices fall further before supply and demand cure it, and discuss curve contango and storage, refining and LNG. Conclusion: no stable oil price, product and regional gas dislocations ahead.

**Approach:** oil, physical trading, supply-demand balance, term structure, refining, LNG
