# Nick Baltas on Trend Following in 2026- Signals, Structure & Strategy - Systematic Investor - Ep.282

**Speakers:** Nick Baltas (host/co-host, systematic strategist); Neils Kostrup Larsen (host, hedge fund manager)

**Summary:** Weekly Systematic Investor episode: Baltas and Larsen review trend following in early 2026 and 2025 dispersion across speed and universe (slow and small won the last 3-4 years but not historically). They then summarise three December 2025 papers: a consultant primer on trend, nonlinear time series momentum, and ATP's theory of trend payoff. ## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Nick Baltas (systematic strategist) argues that trend following works because information is digested slowly, and that recent dispersion between trend programs (slow and small-universe programs winning for 3-4 years, faster and broader ones lagging) came from V-shaped reversals and a handful of trending markets, not from a better design; so one should not change trend design in response to recent out- or under-performance. He then reviews three December 2025 papers (a consultant primer on trend, nonlinear time-series momentum with sigmoid/tail-flattening position sizing, and ATP's theory that a trend follower earns crisis alpha even with iid returns).
