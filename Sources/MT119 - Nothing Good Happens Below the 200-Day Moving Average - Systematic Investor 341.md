## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Mark argues that nothing good happens below the 200-day moving average (a behavioural regime switch for risk) and that investors in Q1 2025 faced policy uncertainty, which is uncountable and distinct from measurable volatility. Uncertainty slows adjustment by hedgers and speculators (creating autocorrelation and trends) but also lowers signal-to-noise and shrinks quant positions; because analogues are scarce, expert judgement is less reliable and price-following systematic trend exposure (intermediate speed) is a sensible second-best response. The second half covers gold drivers, manager curiosity ('strong opinions weakly held') and portable alpha/return stacking as a way of mixing risk premia with cash, bond or equity cores.
