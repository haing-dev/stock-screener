**Summary:** Panel on customized managed accounts and completion portfolios for institutional allocators. Covers move from strategic asset allocation to total portfolio approach, building bespoke hedge-fund/CTA sleeves (beta caps, vol targets, hybrid strategies), ESG futures, and why equity-bond correlation risk in inflation drives demand for downside-diversifying completion funds.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Institutional hedge-fund and CTA investing has moved from commingled funds to ever more customized accounts (share classes, funds of one, managed accounts, beta caps, hybrid books) and now to outcome-oriented completion portfolios, driven by the 2010s equity bull market, alpha turning into beta, and the shift from strategic asset allocation (SAA) to a total portfolio approach (TPA). The panel's forward-looking claim is that a risk of positive stock-bond correlation under inflation and hawkish policy makes investors want downside-diversifying, conditional-correlation-based risk mitigation rather than average low correlation, while ESG implementation is still non-standard and mostly exclusion.
