# Pierre Andurand Says the World Could Run Out of Cocoa Inventories - Odd Lots

**Speakers:** Pierre Andurand (founder, Andurand Capital Management); Tracy Alaway (Bloomberg, host); Joe Weisenthal (Bloomberg, host)

**Summary:** Odd Lots interview with commodity hedge fund manager Pierre Andurand on his long cocoa, copper and oil theses, May 2024. Approach is fundamental supply-demand balance modelling with stocks-to-grindings ratio, high-frequency export tracking, inelastic demand and slow supply response; also discusses positioning, liquidity and why pod funds miss multi-year deficits.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Andurand argues that cocoa faces a historic, partly structural supply deficit (disease, climate change, low farmer incomes) hitting inelastic demand, so that stocks-to-grindings near 21% heading toward 13% means price must rise until demand is destroyed; he stayed long with skewed payoff. He applies the same balance-sheet method to copper (multi-year deficit with slow supply response, price signal missing because pod funds cannot hold long) and sees limited oil supply disruption and a marginal US election effect.
