# Pierre Andurand on What Europe Needs to Do This Winter - Odd Lots

**Speakers:** Pierre Andurand (founder, Andurand Capital Management); Tracy Alloway (Bloomberg, host); Joe Weisenthal (Bloomberg, host)

**Summary:** Odd Lots interview (21 Sept 2022) with commodity hedge fund manager Pierre Andurand on Europe's winter energy crisis. Approach: supply-demand balance modelling for gas and oil; argues Europe can manage via LNG, 15% heating cuts and fuel switching, while oil upside risks (Russian losses, SPR end, China reopening) coexist with a broken, illiquid futures market where he cut positions.

**Approach:** fundamental commodities, natural gas, oil, supply-demand balance, energy policy, market liquidity, position sizing

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Pierre Andurand argues that Europe can get through the 2022-23 winter without Russian gas if governments plan for zero Russian supply: LNG imports already cover about two thirds of the loss, import capacity is not binding, and a 3 degree thermostat cut (about 15% of residential/commercial gas) plus 5% less power demand closes the balance, so public demand-reduction campaigns matter more than price caps or subsidies. For oil he sees a multi-year supply-demand balance that must push prices higher (Russian losses after the December embargo, end of the SPR release, China reopening, no spare capacity, weak shale response), while admitting the futures market is thin and disconnected, which made him cut his structural bullish energy position to focus on survival.
