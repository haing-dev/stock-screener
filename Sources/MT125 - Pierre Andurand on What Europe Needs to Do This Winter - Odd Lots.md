# Pierre Andurand on What Europe Needs to Do This Winter - Odd Lots

**Speakers:** Pierre Andurand (founder, Andurand Capital Management); Tracy Alloway (Bloomberg, host); Joe Weisenthal (Bloomberg, host)

**Summary:** Odd Lots interview (21 Sept 2022) with commodity hedge fund manager Pierre Andurand on Europe's winter energy crisis. Approach: supply-demand balance modelling for gas and oil; argues Europe can manage via LNG, 15% heating cuts and fuel switching, while oil upside risks (Russian losses, SPR end, China reopening) coexist with a broken, illiquid futures market where he cut positions.
