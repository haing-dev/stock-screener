## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Two days after the November 2024 US election, Alan Dunne and Niels Kaastrup-Larsen read the clean Republican sweep as a policy-expectations shock (tax cuts, deregulation, tariffs as a bargaining chip) that lifted equities and Treasury yields while current US data stayed solid, with the unsustainable deficit as the main risk to yields. They treat Goldman's 3% ten-year S&P forecast and the concentration warning as plausible but unproven, and argue that in an unpredictable policy environment systematic, forecast-free strategies such as trend following (despite a bad October when bonds, equities and the dollar reversed together) belong in portfolios.
