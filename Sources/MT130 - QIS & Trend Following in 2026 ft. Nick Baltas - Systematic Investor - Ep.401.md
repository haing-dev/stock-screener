## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Nick Baltas argues that systematic QIS products and trend following are wrappers for macro views whose returns depend on whether they harvest a genuine risk premium (durable, only time-varying under crowding) or an arbitrage-like flow imbalance (eroded by capital). The 2026 commodity curve carry drawdown, the worst in 40 years, was the strategy's known supply-shock risk realising, not a break, and diversification across premia and strategic rather than reactive allocation is the response. Moritz Seibert adds the CTA-side view on crowding charges, universe breadth in 2026 and execution slippage.
