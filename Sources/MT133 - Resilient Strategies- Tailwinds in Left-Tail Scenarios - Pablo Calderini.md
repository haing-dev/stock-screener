# Resilient Strategies- Tailwinds in Left-Tail Scenarios - Pablo Calderini

**Speakers:** Pablo Calderini (Graham Capital Management, macro portfolio manager)

**Summary:** Graham Capital talk (mid-2024): central-bank regime history, left-tail risks, stock-bond correlation drivers and the case for adding discretionary/systematic macro to a 60/40 portfolio.

**Approach:** macro, diversification, stock-bond correlation, tail risk, monetary policy

**Cross-check completeness estimate of first read:** 0.82 - first reader caught the headline themes, statistics and tables (central-bank regimes, vol, stock-bond correlation, drawdown record, 60/40 results); missed supporting mechanisms (post-COVID policy sequence, Middle East reading, macro style and vol reduction reasons) and misordered the Q1 2024 rally versus the Fed pivot.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Pablo Calderini (Graham Capital macro PM) presents a client talk arguing that macro adds value to equity-heavy portfolios because it has conditional low-to-negative correlation to equities and bonds: it follows central-bank policy and changes direction by regime. He explains why 2010-2020 starved macro (the central-bank put suppressed volatility), why the 2022 inflation shock changed that, and why mid-2024 calm (low VIX, MOVE and FX vol) sits against large tail risks (inflation rebound, China-Taiwan, Middle East) and a positive stock-bond correlation. Adding 10-20% macro to a 60/40 portfolio since 1990 raised return, cut volatility, lifted Sharpe and shrank drawdown.

**Chain: How adding macro improves a 60/40 portfolio**
