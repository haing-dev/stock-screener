## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Ten systematic-investor panelists, recorded 17 Dec 2025, argue that 2025 return dispersion among trend managers (market set, speed and vol-sizing around the April Liberation Day shock) is large and not predictable ex ante, that designs are never obvious and must be judged against each manager's stated objective, and that allocators struggle with the randomness and the long data needed to separate skill from luck. A second thread (Jim Kasang) holds that the post-2022 flow into non-correlated assets (gold, crypto, structured products, hedge funds) is early and driven by a supply-demand gap in non-correlation, with panelists adding that perceived non-correlation may not survive a shock and that allocators' common responses can create correlation.
