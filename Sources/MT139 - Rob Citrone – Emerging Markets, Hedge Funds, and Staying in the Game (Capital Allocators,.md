## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Citrone, founder of Discovery Capital, describes a top-down plus bottom-up EM and global macro approach inherited from Julian Robertson (Tiger) and George Soros: roughly 80% of ideas come from macro analysts, cross-referenced with outside contacts, with shorts chosen for asymmetric skew and held when conviction stays high but cut completely when conviction is lost. In July 2022 he is bearish on risk assets (Fed cannot rescue markets this time, quantitative tightening still ahead, China, Europe and tech shorts, short JGBs, short Turkey) and constructive on the US long term, India, Brazil and Argentina.
