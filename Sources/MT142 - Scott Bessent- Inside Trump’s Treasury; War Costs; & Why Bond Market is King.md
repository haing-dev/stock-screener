# Scott Bessent- Inside Trump’s Treasury; War Costs; & Why Bond Market is King

**Speakers:** Scott Bessent (US Treasury Secretary, ex-Soros CIO, Key Square founder); Wilfried Frost (host, Master Investor Podcast)

**Summary:** Wilfried Frost interviews Treasury Secretary Bessent (recorded March 2026, during the Iran conflict) on his macro investing principles and on Treasury policy. ## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Scott Bessent, speaking as US Treasury Secretary during the March 2026 Iran conflict, explains his macro-investing method (bet against elite and consensus framing at turning points, require a mispriced vision plus a timed catalyst, shelve trades whose timing is wrong, keep risk within what you can hold) and carries the same lens into policy: functioning, liquid markets and the Treasury market matter most; an oil shock hurts through duration not level; announced supply releases are priced in early; extraordinary central-bank measures should be brief; and tariffs are being rebuilt under other statutes. His US-relative-strength case rests on energy, technology (AI compute) and military power.
