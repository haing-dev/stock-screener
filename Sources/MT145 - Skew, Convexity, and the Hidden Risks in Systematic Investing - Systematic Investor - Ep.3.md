**Speakers:** Nicole Kajin (founder, Alpha Quest, short-term CTA); Alan Dunn (systematic investor co-host); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

She treats skew and convexity, not Sharpe, as the true risk metric. Her approach trades short-term breakouts in vol-compressed markets and plans to short crowded high-Sharpe single-stock factors.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Nicole Kajin argues that decades of political and Fed support (a Fed put, with US equities at over 200% of GDP) made equity risk look free, which rewards high-Sharpe, highly hedged and leveraged multi-manager platforms whose Sharpe is really negative-skew tail risk, and pulled much of the CTA industry into long-biased, short-tail style drift that no longer diversifies equity corrections. Her response is to measure risk beyond standard deviation, trade short-term breakouts where volatility measures disagree (cheap insurance through delta-1 momentum), keep long-cycle factor exposure, and short abnormally high-Sharpe single-stock factors; Niels and Alan push back that trend followers are adaptive and that index-level critiques mix diversified CTAs with pure trend.
