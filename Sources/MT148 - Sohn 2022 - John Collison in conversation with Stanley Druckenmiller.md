# Sohn 2022 - John Collison in conversation with Stanley Druckenmiller

**Speakers:** Stanley Druckenmiller (Duquesne family office founder); John Collison (Stripe co-founder, interviewer)

**Summary:** Druckenmiller describes his macro style: forecast the economy from leading-industry stocks, size concentrated bets, and wait for fat pitches. In 2022 he saw a hard landing as probable because inflation above 5% has never been tamed without a recession, and was cautious on bonds and short on energy supply.

**Approach:** discretionary macro, top-down, risk management, leading indicators

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Druckenmiller argues that policy far out of step with circumstances produced inflation and an asset bubble that is now bursting, and that history (no soft landing once inflation exceeds 4.5-5%) makes a hard landing and recession in 2023 probable. His method is to forecast the economy from leading and lagging industry stocks, to size concentrated bets by conviction and streaks, and to wait for fat pitches, which in mid-2022 means standing aside from bonds, expecting to re-short equities on a rally, holding energy longs and expecting a short dollar.
