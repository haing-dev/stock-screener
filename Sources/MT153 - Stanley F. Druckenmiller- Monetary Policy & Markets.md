**Speakers:** Stanley Druckenmiller (macro investor, ex-Duquesne/Soros); Karl Sokoloff (Real Vision interviewer; caption spells Carol Sokolov); Raoul Pal (Real Vision CEO, intro; caption spells Ralph Powell)
