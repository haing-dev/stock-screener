# Steve Cohen and Jawad Mian at Sohn 2025

**Speakers:** Steve Cohen (founder, Point72, owner of the Mets); Jawad Mian (interviewer)

**Summary:** Steve Cohen at Sohn 2025 sees slowing growth, 45% recession odds, a tariff rate near 14-17% and a range-bound market with a higher floor after the policy pivot. His approach is analysis first, pattern recognition second, a consistent process through slumps and lowered expectations after an easy 2024; the rest is personal reflection.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Cohen argues that tariffs will slow US growth to about 1.5% (recession odds about 45%, lowered by the China retreat) while the Fed stays cautious on tariff inflation, so with multiples high the index has little upside and should trade sideways in a range with a higher floor after the policy pivot. His operating advice is to rely on facts first and pattern recognition second, keep the bar higher in a new regime after an easy 2024, lower expectations, and return to a consistent process during slumps; most of the conversation is personal reflection with no market content.
