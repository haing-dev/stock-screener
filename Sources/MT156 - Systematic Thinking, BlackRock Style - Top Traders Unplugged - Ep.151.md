## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Jeff Rosenberg (BlackRock Systematic) argues that the post-Covid move from too little to too much (persistent, about 3%) inflation removed the 'divine coincidence' that made bonds reliable diversifiers, with fiscal dominance and possible financial repression adding a tail risk, so investors need other uncorrelated return streams. He engineers those streams (a broad-but-scalable trend basket, a credit-lens defensive-alpha equity long/short, blends of uncorrelated payoff profiles) on the fundamental law of active management, and holds trend for diversification, not timing. The hosts accept the macro case but worry that cheap, narrow trend ETFs trade breadth for scalability and may not behave like real CTAs in stress.
