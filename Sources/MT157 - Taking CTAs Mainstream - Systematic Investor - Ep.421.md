**Speakers:** Andrew Beer (Andrew Beer, DBi / Dynamic Beta founder, managed futures ETF); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged; caption 'Nils Castrolen')

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Andrew Beer argues that 60/40 was a historical accident of a 20-year bond bull market, that every equity crisis broke the previous diversifier playbook while the SG CTA index (low correlation, crisis alpha, controlled index drawdowns, a net Sharpe of 0.34) held up, and that managed futures will only go mainstream by being reframed in allocators' language as incremental, tactical diversification and relabelled 'contrarian tactical alpha'. Niels Kaastrup-Larsen adds the complex-adaptive-systems rationale for trend following, current performance numbers and the view that allocators time the space badly and are held back by due-diligence fixation on details.
