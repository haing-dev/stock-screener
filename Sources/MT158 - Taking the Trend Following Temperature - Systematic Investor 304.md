# Taking the Trend Following Temperature - Systematic Investor 304

**Speakers:** Graham Robertson (partner, head of client portfolio management, Man AHL); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Graham Robertson (Man AHL) reviews H1 2024 trend following and argues trend delivers equity-like returns with zero correlation, strongest in sustained sell-offs and as a partial bond substitute. AHL's approach is maximum diversification across many price drivers (especially alternative markets), a constant volatility target and uncapped equity exposure; AI has not revolutionised the strategy.

**Approach:** trend following, diversification, crisis alpha, alternative markets, volatility targeting, AI
