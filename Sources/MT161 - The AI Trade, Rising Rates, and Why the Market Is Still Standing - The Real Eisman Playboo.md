## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Recorded early August 2026 with Strategas' Jason Trennert and Chris Verrone: the US equity market has absorbed a 30-50% correction in AI leaders, a war, oil swings and rising long yields because internals are healthy and the economy is strong, so the bar for an equity-ending bond yield is much higher than the 4.5% 10-year that capped 2023-25. Trennert's cautions: market concentration, hyperscaler free cash flow turning negative and requiring external financing, a Fed (new chair Warsh) withdrawing forward guidance, and stocks ignoring rising yields as a late-cycle warning. Eisman concludes the strategists are bullish unless the 10-year rises significantly or the AI story breaks.
