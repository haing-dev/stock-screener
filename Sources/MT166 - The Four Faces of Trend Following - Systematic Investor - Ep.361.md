# The Four Faces of Trend Following - Systematic Investor - Ep.361

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged, hedge fund manager); Rich Brennan (co-host, Systematic Investor, trend follower / outlier hunter)

**Summary:** Brennan splits trend followers into four archetypes (replicators, core diversifiers, crisis risk offset, outlier hunters) and argues rule choices (breadth, vol targeting, momentum type, symmetry, speed) should follow the objective, not a universal best. A 68-market test shows 30-market subsets underperform the full book 88% of the time. Niels adds that short-term trend funds have not delivered crisis alpha in 2025.

**Approach:** trend following, CTA archetypes, diversification breadth, volatility targeting, absolute vs cross-sectional momentum, manager selection
