# The Market Creates Its Own Trends — Richard Brennan - Systematic Investor - Ep.418

**Speakers:** Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, hedge fund manager); Rich Brennan (co-host, Systematic Investor, trend follower / outlier hunter, co-author of The Trend Following Manifesto)

### C05
- **Rich Brennan** Taking partial profits on winners converts open profit to redeployable equity and smooths drawdowns, but cuts exposure to rare exceptional trades and gives up positive skew, moving away from pure outlier hunting.  #mech/risk_management

  ^MT170-C05-1
- **Rich Brennan** Backtests of profit-taking have little statistical power because outliers are rare, one extraordinary trade can flip the verdict, and 125-year data shows bigger events than the 40-year sample.  #mech/research_method

  indicator: sample size of outliers · asset: multi · horizon: years · testable: partly - low power · confidence: high
  ^MT170-C05-2
- **Rich Brennan** Fractal sampling: longer samples reveal bigger drawdowns and bigger positive outliers, so type-II error (missing outliers) is the main risk for an outlier hunter.  #mech/research_method

  asset: multi · horizon: years · testable: partly · confidence: medium

### C14
- **Rich Brennan** Stability breeds instability (Minsky): leverage rises and hedges shrink during calm, so a disturbance triggers stops and margin calls and an outlier marks a transition rather than a large ordinary fluctuation.  #mech/crowding_positioning
  > a long period of stability encourages people to assume it's going to continue. So under that circumstance, leverage rises, hedges shrink and positions accumulate around boundaries
  indicator: leverage, hedge ratio · asset: multi · horizon: months · conditions: calm preceding a break · testable: partly · confidence: medium
  ^MT170-C14-1
- **Rich Brennan** Non-stationarity: a long successful pattern attracts capital and imitation, so relationships change; a backtest is evidence, not a promise.  #mech/research_method
  > Average attendance can appear stable while the rules underneath it are continuously changing.
  asset: multi · horizon: years · testable: partly · confidence: high
  ^MT170-C14-2
- **Rich Brennan** Use historical testing to reject rules that fail their designed behaviour and to reveal turnover, drawdowns and reliance on exceptional trades, but never project one realised average as a constant or ignore unwelcome evidence.  #mech/research_method
  > an average annual return calculated from one realized history is evidence about what occurred. It's not a physical constant
  asset: multi · testable: yes · confidence: high
  ^MT170-C14-3
