## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Nick Baltas and host Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (Systematic Investor Ep.410, recorded Friday, around 24 July 2026) read four recent papers as one story: trend following works through a small set of principal components (equity, duration, plus commodities for diversification), so a defensive trend allocation should be sized and layered by the shock it must cover (an inflation shock for equity-bond portfolios, an equity fall for equity-only ones). Baltas's second argument is that themes are a tagging layer that raises within-theme correlation (so risk models must be theme-aware) and that rising narratives precede and feed price momentum, while the host doubts that themes add much beyond sector grouping.
