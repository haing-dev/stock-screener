**Speakers:** Marat Molyboga (CRO and director of research, Efficient Capital Management); Katie Kaminski (co-host, Alpha Simplex); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor 403: June 2026 CTA update (May small gains, equities and energy trends after Iran shock, hard fixed income), Molyboga's views on manager access and behavioural mistakes, and Kaminski's paper on geopolitical risk and managed futures.
