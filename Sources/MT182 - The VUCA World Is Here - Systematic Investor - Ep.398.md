## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Mark Rzepczynski argues that the 2026 Middle East oil supply shock and the wider 'VUCA' (volatility, uncertainty, complexity, ambiguity) environment favour trend followers because supply shocks leave central banks without a clear response, so prices are free to trend, and because ambiguity makes investors slow down and spread decisions over time, producing longer trends. His research adds that managed futures returns are insensitive to VIX risk regimes and risk shocks, unlike other hedge fund strategies, which gives a diversification advantage beyond low beta though with little alpha. Niels Kaastrup-Larsen reports a strong April for CTAs, doubts that replication or extra context (AI, sentiment) improves on price-only trend following, and stresses that slower systems have done better in an era of instant information.
