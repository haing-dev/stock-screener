# The War in Ukraine, Relations With China & Wider Geopolitics (With General David Petraeus)

**Speakers:** General David Petraeus (former US general and CIA director, KKR Global Institute chairman); Simon (host, Money Maze Podcast)

**Summary:** January 2023 Money Maze interview on geopolitics. Petraeus assesses Ukraine, China, Iran, cyber and NATO, and for investors argues the world has moved from benign globalization to great-power rivalry where geopolitics constrains economics, with selective decoupling, just-in-case supply chains, and no near-term replacement for the dollar.

**Approach:** geopolitics, global macro regime, supply chains, export controls, dollar reserve status
