## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Toby Crabel (short-term systematic futures, recorded 23 March 2026) argues that edges do not vanish because they are published but because market structure and participants change: the opening range breakout he wrote up in 1989 decayed mainly because 24-hour trading removed the open as a reference point, and today large algorithmic traders use the liquidity at obvious highs and lows, so reversals are more common than follow-through. His response is continuous adaptation (about 10% of the portfolio changed each year, shorter holds, balanced generalised mean-reversion and momentum strategies, heavy execution investment, volume-price research) rather than trusting any single optimised model.
