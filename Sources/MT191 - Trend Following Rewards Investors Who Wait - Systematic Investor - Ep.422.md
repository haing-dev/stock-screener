# Trend Following Rewards Investors Who Wait - Systematic Investor - Ep.422

**Speakers:** Katie Kaminski (guest co-host, trend-following researcher/practitioner); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor episode: Katie Kaminski and Niels Kaastrup-Larsen review 2026 trend performance, geopolitical risk and inflation (her Copenhagen talk), the AQR inflation/risk-mitigation paper and Quantica's 'Paid for Presence'. Conclusions: geopolitical risk foreshadows inflation and makes bonds/60-40 vulnerable; trend and commodities respond to different inflation regimes; trend returns come from a few big months and investors lose return by timing it.

**Approach:** trend following, managed futures, crisis alpha, inflation, geopolitical risk, risk mitigation, investor behavior
