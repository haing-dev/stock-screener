# Trend Following as a Return Enhancement Overlay - Systematic Investor 297

**Speakers:** Alan Dunne (co-host, macro/allocator perspective, Dublin; caption 'Alan Don'); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor 297 (May 2024): Alan Dunne and Niels discuss macro (Fed independence, excess savings running out while risk is priced low, data-dependent central banks), May CTA performance, a SocGen quant macro heterogeneity paper, the UBS family office survey, trend following as a return-enhancing overlay on equities, and Brian Hurst's multi-manager paper. Main conclusions: trend adds in both crisis and strong-equity quarters; multi-manager success relies on low-correlation strategies plus leverage but is vulnerable to tail correlation.

**Approach:** trend following, return stacking, quant macro, multi-manager, family offices, central banks, consumer excess savings, macro commentary
