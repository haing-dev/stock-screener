# Trend Following during Trump Tariff Turmoil! - Systematic Investor 344

**Speakers:** Katie Kaminski (guest co-host, trend-following researcher; author of the 'crisis or correction' paper); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Systematic Investor 344 (April 2025): Niels and Katie Kaminski analyse trend following during the tariff shock using her paper 'Trade War: Crisis or Correction?'. Conclusions: trend typically loses in the first phase of equity drawdowns under ~15 percent and earns crisis alpha only in deeper or longer ones; dispersion between managers is normal after shocks; speed, market universe, correlation and volatility handling drive differences; return stacking creates a new portfolio construction problem.

**Approach:** trend following, crisis alpha, drawdown analysis, tariff shock, manager dispersion, portable alpha, risk management, volatility scaling
