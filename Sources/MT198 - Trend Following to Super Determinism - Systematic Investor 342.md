# Trend Following to Super Determinism - Systematic Investor 342

**Speakers:** Richard Brennan (Dunn Capital, trend follower); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Niels and Richard Brennan review the April 2025 tariff sell-off, Q1 2025 trend following and the passing of Bill Dunn. The second half is a philosophical talk linking trend following to complex adaptive systems and superdeterminism. Main idea: trend followers react to price structure rather than predict, stay humble and diversified, and follow process.

**Approach:** trend following, philosophy, regime shift, risk management
