# Trend Following too complicated- - Systematic Investor 296

**Speakers:** Mark Rzepczynski (systematic researcher, caption 'Mark with sininsky'); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Mark and Niels discuss the SG Capital Consulting investor survey, mid-May 2024 CTA results, de-globalisation and dispersion, using economic data in trend models, and the VUCA risk framework. Conclusion: in a disordered, high-VUCA world follow price trends with diversification and ensembles; industry complexity is partly marketing.

**Approach:** trend following, macro, VUCA, manager selection
