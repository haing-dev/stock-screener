## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Scott Bessent describes a Soros-Druckenmiller style process in which micro research and an inferential or framing edge build a theory of systems that are accelerating or about to break, which is proved empirically and then checked against Wall Street positioning and option skew before any trade; the best trades are asymmetric bets against 'it has never happened' beliefs (sterling 1992, subprime, Abenomics). He argues the US is now the unstable system: a 7% peacetime deficit, shortened debt maturities, fiscal dominance risk and reliance on foreign funding, with gold (and eventually the dollar or rates) as the release valve, while US-China decoupling and China's weak confidence are the main geopolitical-economic stress.
