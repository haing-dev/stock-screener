# Upcoming Big Changes in Global Markets - Systematic Investor 315

**Speakers:** Cham (guest, volatility and flows strategist); Alan (host, sitting in for Niels Kaastrup-Larsen)

Macro view is structural/populist inflation (stagflation decade), steepeners, and election scenarios (Trump vs Harris) with China as a tactical trade.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Cham argues that markets are driven by identifiable structural flows rather than psychology: year-end releveraging of institutions, dealers buying back tail hedges as high election skew decays and holiday-thinned volume speeds time decay, so equities should rise into roughly 13-15 January 2025, a set-up he also calls a classic blow-off top. His macro view is bearish: after 40 deflationary years a populist, fiscal demand-push regime (inequality, deglobalization, conflict) makes inflation structural, so he holds curve steepeners, expects a decade of stagflation and treats election, China and Taiwan outcomes as swing factors.
