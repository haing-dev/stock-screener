# What is the fiscal rule-

**Speakers:** Guest (former Norwegian prime minister and finance minister; not named in captions); Interviewer (Norges Bank Investment Management; not named in captions)

**Summary:** NBIM interview on Norway's fiscal (spending) rule: oil revenues go to the pension fund and only the expected 3% return is spent. The rule worked better than expected as the fund grew; new risk is equity-market exposure and the key lesson is credibility of self-imposed rules.

**Approach:** fiscal rule, sovereign wealth fund, institutional design
