## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Narratives flip within days (AI disinflation to Iran stagflation, tariff chaos to debasement trade), so allocators should look at statistics and structure rather than prevailing stories. Alan argues trend followers diversify 60/40 through dynamic bond/equity sizing and commodity/currency positioning, that the AI productivity boom carries a Greenspan-style demand response and a Fed-policy-error risk later in 2026-27, and that CTA allocators should avoid narrative-chasing and packaging effects; Niels adds trend-barometer data, the March oil-spike positioning and views on replication and manager choice.
