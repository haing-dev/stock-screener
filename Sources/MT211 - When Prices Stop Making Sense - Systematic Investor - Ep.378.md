# When Prices Stop Making Sense - Systematic Investor - Ep.378

**Speakers:** Alan Don (guest host, Top Traders Unplugged, sitting in for Niels); Mark (systematic/CTA researcher, Chicago)

**Summary:** Systematic trend-following discussion (Ep.378, Dec 2025) after the Fed's third 2025 cut: prices are primal but not value, signal vs noise and unmeasurable uncertainty, thin holiday liquidity. Covers bubbles (gold, cocoa, AI credit), where momentum works best (industry/factor > country/stock), and the total portfolio approach vs trend overlays. Conclusion: use price signals but smooth them, treat uncertainty explicitly, and use trend as a diversifier when bonds hedge less.

**Approach:** trend following, momentum, signal vs noise, bubbles, total portfolio approach, Fed policy, diversification

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Mark argues that for a systematic modeller 'prices are primal' (they reveal the current supply-demand balance) but are not value, so models must handle noise and, above all, uncertainty that cannot be measured as volatility (messy Fed, thin holiday liquidity, the CME outage, tariff-split copper prices, distorted macro data). He links relative safe-asset scarcity (Treasuries no longer a reliable hedge, higher term premium, financial repression) to the gold and silver surges, treats bubbles as hard-to-value assets with regime-change stories rather than classic speculation, and argues momentum is pervasive but strongest where assets are hard to value. The host adds the reflexive wealth/leverage loop, AI-related credit froth and the argument that a 30% trend overlay already delivers what a total portfolio approach is trying to do.
