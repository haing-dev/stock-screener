# Why Cooperation Is Essential in Crisis - IMF Managing Director Kristalina Georgieva

**Speakers:** Kristalina Georgieva (Managing Director, IMF); Nicolai Tangen (CEO, Norges Bank Investment Management, host)

**Summary:** Interview (Dec 2025) with the IMF head on the institution's role, the state of the world economy, tariffs, debt, inflation, climate and AI. She sees growth resilient at about 3% thanks to stronger institutions, agile private sectors and a smaller-than-feared trade shock, but argues debt leaves no buffers. AI is seen as profitable but with stretched valuations, a likely partial correction, and large labor-market and inequality effects.

**Approach:** IMF / official sector view, global growth, tariffs, fiscal debt, AI and productivity, climate risk
