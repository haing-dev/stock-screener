# Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Opportunity

**Speakers:** Kenneth Tropin (founder and chairman, Graham Capital Management); Moderator (iConnections host, unnamed)

**Summary:** iConnections fireside chat (June 2025) with the founder of Graham Capital, a quant and discretionary macro manager. Macro demand rises because rates move far more, stocks look expensive, bonds are risky with large deficits, and geopolitics is unstable; macro is uncorrelated rather than reliably negatively correlated. He favors adding fundamental and carry signals to trend, supervised AI, and warns that a bond-market scare (duration risk) is the key risk.

**Approach:** macro hedge fund, trend following, quant and discretionary blend, diversification, duration risk, AI in research, multi-strategy comparison

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Kenneth Tropin (Graham Capital) says interest in macro is rising because rate-setting activity is about 20 times higher than in 2010-2021, equities look stretched, bonds are risky because of record deficits and no term premium, and geopolitics is unstable, so a return stream uncorrelated with stocks and bonds is valuable. He argues that people, collaboration, a custom-client offering and always adding new, non-correlated alpha sources (carry, relative value, fundamentals alongside trend) are how a macro manager stays competitive against multi-strategy pods. His chief market worry (June 2025) is a bond-market scare from deficits and tariffs.

**Worldview premises**
- Macro opportunity scales with the volume of central-bank action: 13 quarter-point moves by Fed, ECB and BoE in the 11 years to 2021 versus 60 in the next 36 months, so rate trading has about 20 times more action. _[empirical, stated; support: Count of rate moves, repeated from his 2022 talk (MT066 uses 26 hikes in 2022).]_ ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C02-1|C02-1]])
- When equity valuations are stretched and bonds are no longer a safe diversifier (record deficits, no plan to fix them), allocators look for a stream uncorrelated with both, which macro offers. _[theoretical, stated; support: Observed 3-4 times more client meetings than five years ago; valuation and deficit levels.]_ ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C02-2|C02-2]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C02-X1|C02-X1]])

- Trend following works when trends persist and fails when trends are absent or reverse; it is the strategy that was loved in 2022 when little else worked. _[experience, stated; support: 2022 experience, April 2025 reversals.]_ ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C03-4|C03-4]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C04-4|C04-4]])

**Chain: Why a deficit-driven bond scare would hit both bonds and equities**
1. record deficit with no plan and tariffs adding inflation → investors no longer willing to hold duration without a premium: 25 years of no term premium leaves holders exposed ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C07-2|C07-2]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C07-3|C07-3]])
2. bond sell-off in a way not seen in a long time → yields up and equities down: bonds are the usual hedge; losing it hits both, and stretched equity valuations are vulnerable ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C07-4|C07-4]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C02-2|C02-2]])
3. tariffs inflationary and likely hawkish new Fed chair → Fed cannot be as dovish as it would like: inflation constraint plus personnel ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C07-1|C07-1]])
- Evidence: 1980 analogy and current deficit size; no model.
- Fails when: Deficit credibly addressed, or inflation falls enough that the Fed eases without disorder (not discussed).

**Chain: How macro delivers diversification without a hedge guarantee**
1. tactical ability to switch long equities to short, and non-equity markets in the book → return stream not tied to the 60/40 mix: flexible direction and exposure to FX and commodities ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C03-2|C03-2]])
2. an extended market decline → positioning turns short and the fund benefits: trend and tactical signals follow the move ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C03-3|C03-3]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C03-1|C03-1]])
- Evidence: 2008, 1987, 2003; negative correlation in two thirds to three quarters of bad periods; moderator's quarter-by-quarter observation.
- Fails when: Sharp, fast declines when the fund is still long equities and positions have not turned.

**Chain: Why policy volatility hurts medium-term models and helps short-term ones**
1. tariff announcements reversed within days (April 2025) → trends that reverse before medium-term signals can capture them: signal horizon longer than the policy cycle ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C04-1|C04-1]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C03-4|C03-4]])
2. fading of policy chaos toward the midterms → moves that prevail become easier for models to capture: he judges the pace of policy change cannot accelerate further ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C06-3|C06-3]])
- Evidence: Last six to eight weeks of market behaviour at recording; no data.
- Fails when: Policy volatility continues or accelerates.

**Chain: Team correlation in a discretionary book under big macro moves**
1. large dominant moves (gold, Bitcoin, weak dollar) → teams converge on the same trades, raising correlation above the usual 10% and crowding positions: everyone wants to be in the moves that matter ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C06-X2|C06-X2]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C06-1|C06-1]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C06-2|C06-2]])
2. teams with very different time horizons → lower correlation: short-term tactical traders differ from one- to two-month holders ([[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C06-1|C06-1]], [[MT214 - Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Oppor#^MT214-C06-X2|C06-X2]])
- Evidence: Average team correlation of about 10%, higher recently.
- Fails when: A single dominant macro move captures every horizon at once.
