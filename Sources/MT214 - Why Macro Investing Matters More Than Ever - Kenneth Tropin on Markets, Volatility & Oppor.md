# Why Macro Investing Matters More Than Ever - Kenneth Tropin on Markets, Volatility & Opportunity

**Speakers:** Kenneth Tropin (founder and chairman, Graham Capital Management); Moderator (iConnections host, unnamed)

**Summary:** iConnections fireside chat (June 2025) with the founder of Graham Capital, a quant and discretionary macro manager. Macro demand rises because rates move far more, stocks look expensive, bonds are risky with large deficits, and geopolitics is unstable; macro is uncorrelated rather than reliably negatively correlated. He favors adding fundamental and carry signals to trend, supervised AI, and warns that a bond-market scare (duration risk) is the key risk.

**Approach:** macro hedge fund, trend following, quant and discretionary blend, diversification, duration risk, AI in research, multi-strategy comparison
