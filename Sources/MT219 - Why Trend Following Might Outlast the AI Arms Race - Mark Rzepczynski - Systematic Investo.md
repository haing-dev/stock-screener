## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Mark Rzepczynski argues that the 2026 bond sell-off is a return to normal (nominal yield = real rate of about 2-2.5% + inflation of about 3% + a positive term premium, so about 5-5.5%), not an anomaly, and that investors should study the micro foundations and plumbing of markets (who trades, dealer intermediation capital, convenience yield, geo-economic fragmentation) rather than only price action. For trend following he draws three conclusions: fragmentation raises diversification across markets, stock-out risk makes market-specific crisis alpha in low-inventory, low-substitute commodities, and trend following has no alpha half-life, unlike crowded machine-learning strategies whose models converge.
