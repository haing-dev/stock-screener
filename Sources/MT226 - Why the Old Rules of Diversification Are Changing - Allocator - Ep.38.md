# Why the Old Rules of Diversification Are Changing - Allocator - Ep.38

**Speakers:** Mike Pile (deputy head of BlackRock portfolio management group, ex-global CIO strategist, ex-US deputy national security adviser for international economics); Alan Dunn (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Interview with BlackRock's Mike Pile on the shift from the demand-driven 2010s to a supply-driven, scarcity regime (AI buildout, energy shocks, active fiscal). He argues bonds now serve as income not duration diversification, so 60/40 should become growth core + income + market-neutral diversifiers, with systematic multistrategy at the core of hedge fund allocation. AI-supply-chain earnings are bottleneck-driven but valuations are modest; AI is also raising breadth in research.

**Approach:** regime shift, stock-bond correlation, multistrategy hedge funds, portable alpha, factor neutralisation, total portfolio approach, AI theme
