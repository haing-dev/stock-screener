# Yield Storm Ahead- Preparing for a New Era - Systematic Investor 330

**Speakers:** Jim (guest; captioned 'Jim kasang', systematic investor at Kai); Niels Kaastrup-Larsen (host, Top Traders Unplugged)

**Summary:** Jim and Niels (Systematic Investor series, Jan 2025): options skew, vanna/charm and opex cycles as drivers of an expected equity decline, and a structurally inflationary regime with rising yields. Guest argues rates drive everything (P/E and margin compression as in 1968-82), equities are no inflation hedge, hedging is now harder with an upward-sloping curve, and Fed independence is at risk. Also an asymmetric China call-option trade and a trend-following update.

## Argument map

**Episode thesis:** Systematic Investor 330 (January 2025): guest Jim (captioned 'Jim kasang', systematic investor at Kai) argues the world has entered a structurally inflationary, populist-protectionist regime in which rising interest rates, not economic strength, drive asset returns (1968-82 analogue: equities down 67% real, P/E from 25x to 4.5x). He reads option-market structure (sticky long-dated skew, vanna flows, quarterly opex and structured products) as pointing to a stair-step equity decline in Q1 2025, expects the 10-year yield to rise further because stuck, hard-to-hedge participants make the 'pain trade' likely, and sees a Fed in a box under political pressure. His one flagged counter-scenario is a Trump-China deal and China mean reversion, which he proposes expressing with calls as a convex barbell. The host adds the trend-following data, long-end bond stress in the UK and France, and the group-think/recency-bias theme.
