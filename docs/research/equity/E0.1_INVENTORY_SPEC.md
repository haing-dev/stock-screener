# E0.1 Inventory and typing of the General Transcripts: coding spec v1 (2026-11-17)

Step E0.1 of `EQUITY_POD_SHOP_PLAN_v0.3.md`. One row per document, coded from the short note only (front matter,
Summary, Topics covered, Quotes). The transcript text is not read at this step. Coder: Sonnet. A tenth of the
documents (drawn by a seeded script) is recoded blind by Opus; a field with low agreement is dropped. Coders are told
nothing about returns, rankings or tiers, and must not use outside knowledge of how a person or fund performed.
If the note does not say, code "unclear" (or 0); do not guess from fame.

Scripts: `tools/equity/inventory_pack.py` (builds the packs), `tools/equity/inventory_merge.py` (checks the form,
merges, agreement table, investor table).

## Output: one JSON object per line, one line per document, keys exactly as below

| Key | Values |
|---|---|
| `doc` | the DOC number given in the pack (integer) |
| `kind` | `investor_talk`: at least one person who manages or managed other people's or their own fund's money speaks in their own words. `secondary`: hosts or authors discuss an investor, a book or a case; that investor is not present. `company`: an executive or founder speaks mainly about their own company or industry. `expert`: an author, academic, analyst, strategist, economist or adviser who does not run a portfolio. `other` |
| `subject` | main subject, one of: `stock_picking`, `portfolio_and_risk`, `macro_and_markets`, `company_or_industry`, `private_markets`, `career_and_life`, `other` |
| `people` | list of the people who SPEAK, hosts left out unless the host presents their own investing. Each: `{"name": full name as best the note gives it, "role": investor / allocator / executive / expert / host, "firm": firm or fund named in the note or "", "ran_money": yes / no / unclear}`. `investor` = picks securities for a fund, partnership or managed accounts. `allocator` = chooses managers or asset classes (endowment, sovereign fund, fund of funds, family office CIO). `ran_money` = yes only if the note says or plainly implies the person manages or managed a portfolio with their own discretion |
| `absent` | names of investors who are discussed at length but are not present (list, may be empty) |
| `style` | up to two, for the investing described: `quality_compounders` (durable, high-return businesses held for years), `deep_value` (cheap against assets or earnings, mean reversion), `growth` (fast growers, technology, disruption), `small_micro_cap`, `special_situations` (spin-offs, events, activism, post-distress), `long_short` (long/short equity, short selling, multi-manager platforms), `macro_multi_asset`, `credit_distressed`, `quant_systematic`, `private_venture`, `index_allocation`, `sector_specialist`, `none` |
| `sector` | sector name if `sector_specialist`, else "" |
| `region` | `us`, `global`, `europe`, `asia`, `emerging`, `other`, `unclear`: where the investing described takes place |
| `explicit` | how explicit the stock selection criteria are: 0 none; 1 directions only ("good businesses at fair prices"); 2 named measures or a checklist, few numbers; 3 concrete rules with numbers or thresholds |
| `technique` | list, only areas where the document gives a method one could apply, not a passing mention: `valuation`, `analysis_accounting`, `screening_idea_sourcing`, `portfolio_construction_sizing`, `selling`, `risk_management`, `short_selling` |
| `technique_depth` | 0 none; 1 passing; 2 one area in some depth; 3 method is a main subject, with concrete steps |
| `short_side` | yes / no: is the practice of shorting stocks described |

## Agreement (computed by the merge script on the recoded tenth)
Exact match for `kind`, `subject`, `region`, `short_side`; exact and within one step for `explicit` and
`technique_depth`; any overlap for `style`; share of investor names found by both coders, and agreement on
`ran_money` for those. Proposal: a field under 70% agreement is not used in the tier rule.
