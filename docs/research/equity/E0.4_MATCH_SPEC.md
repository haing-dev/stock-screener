# E0.4 phase A2: matching library investors to 13F filers and CRSP funds (spec v1, 2026-11-17)

Input: one block per investor with the firms and roles the notes give and the candidates found on WRDS by name
(`eq_match_v3`): 13F filers (lines starting `F`, with the manager number, names, number of quarters filed since
2010-06, median number of positions and median value) and CRSP mutual funds (lines starting `M`; `M*` means the
investor's surname is in the fund's manager field). The packs hold no return of any kind, and the matcher must not
use anything it knows about how a person or fund performed. General knowledge of who runs which firm may be used
to settle identity only; say so in `why` when it was used.

Output: one JSON object per line, one per investor:
`{"id": "I016", "mgrnos": [14144], "fundnos": [], "start_ym": "", "end_ym": "", "why": "one short sentence"}`

Rules
1. **13F filer.** Match only if (a) the filer is plainly the firm named in the notes, not a firm with a similar
   name, and (b) the notes present the investor as the founder, owner, chief investment officer or named lead
   portfolio manager of that firm. An analyst, a partner among many, a sector head, a former employee or one manager
   inside a large firm with many books is not matched: the firm's filing is not that person's record.
2. **Several filer numbers** only when they are the same book under name variants or successive entities. If two
   candidates of the same firm file in the same quarters, keep the one with the longer record.
3. **Fund.** Match a CRSP fund only if the investor personally manages it: the surname is in the manager field
   (`M*`), or the notes name that fund as the investor's own. At most three funds, the ones the investor is best
   known for running in the notes; no index funds, no bond or money funds. Give the `fundno` shown on the line.
4. **Months.** `start_ym` and `end_ym` (YYYY-MM) stay empty unless the block shows that the investor took over or
   left the filer or fund between 2011 and 2025 (for a fund: the "mgr since" date when it is after 2011-01).
5. **No fit:** empty lists and a reason. A wrong match is worse than no match.

Grades, primary style, region and explicitness are not set by the matcher. A script (`tools/equity/freeze_investors.py`)
derives them from this output, the WRDS coverage figures and the E0.1 inventory by the rules of
`STAGE0_REGISTRATION.md` section 6. A tenth of the investors (seeded draw) is matched a second time, blind, by
another agent; agreement is reported.
