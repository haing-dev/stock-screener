# Foundation ranking of investors from the hedge fund leaderboard (2026-11-20)

User request 2026-11-19: rank the investors to build the foundation on by (1) 5-year manager-weighted return on the leaderboard (`LEADERBOARD_2026-11-19.md`, 2021-2025), (2) how clearly and extensively they describe their process in the material we hold, (3) a final ranking. Strategies may be new to our library.

## Material counted
- Processed library (`Processed Transcripts`, manifest of 2026-11-19: 2,319 documents), searched by title and speaker for every top-50 fund and manager.
- New since the leaderboard: 19 interviews in `General Transcripts 2` (Ruffer 5, Sosin 4, Paulson 4, Zlatev 2, Kantor 2, Shaver 1, Gross 1) and 18 articles (`.md`, added 2026-11-19 to the raw `General Transcripts` folder; not yet in the processed library).
- Of the top 50: 21 have something, 29 have nothing.

## Rule
1. Start from the leaderboard order (5-year MW return per year).
2. Grade the material. **Core**: about 15,000 words or more over at least two sources in which the investor explains how they invest, with named criteria or rules (2 or 3 on the 0-3 scale of `E0.1_INVENTORY_SPEC.md`), and the material is about the activity that produced the 13F return. **With limits**: enough material but about a different or wider activity than the 13F shows, or clear but a single, short or old source, or a theme without selection rules. **Parked**: under about 6,000 useful words, or nothing on how stocks are chosen.
3. Final order: core by return, then with limits by return. Parked names keep their leaderboard order.
Process clarity for the new documents is my single read of the library notes and articles (not double-coded like the E0.1 inventory).

## Notes
- **Cliff Sosin (CAS)**: 4 long interviews, about 73,500 words (one names no guest; about 80% in Carvana), clarity 2-3 (4-8 holdings, contested stocks, one buy or sell a year, data to track the thesis). CAS is not on the 5-year leaderboard: it failed the universe filter (5+ holdings every quarter or $1bn; not checked which). HedgeFollow shows it at +230% over 3 years to Q3 2026. A 5-year figure needs one more WRDS report.
- **Ruffer and Graham** are the best documented after Druckenmiller, but their 13F is a small part of what they do (Ruffer about $2bn of 13F against $25-30bn of assets; Graham trades futures). Their material fits the macro book more than a stock-picking pod.
- **Paulson**: the interviews explain merger arbitrage and the 2007 trade; the 2021-25 return came from a 9-stock book (gold miners, Madrigal), which only the 2021 inflation and gold interview touches.
- Returns are copies of the top 20 13F holdings (no shorts, fees or dividends), and earlier 13F records did not persist (scorecard 2026-11-18). The ranking orders whom to study; it is not evidence a strategy works.
- Most useful to find next, in leaderboard order: Gendell, Harnisch, August (longer and newer), Samaha (Madison Avenue), Lowenstein (Kensico), Greenspan (Slate Path), Sternberg and Stein (Spruce House).
