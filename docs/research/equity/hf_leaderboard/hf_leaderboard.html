How to read it

  • MW weights the top 20 by their share of the portfolio at the start of the quarter; EW weights them equally. Price returns, no dividends, no fees, no shorts or options: a copy-the-13F number, not the fund's own return.
  • WRDS prices end Dec 2025, so the 5 years are 2021–2025; HedgeFollow's 3-year columns run to Q3 2026 and are shown for comparison.
  • Concentrated books dominate: Gendell's IESC is 70% of the portfolio, Peconic holds 5 stocks.
  • Ranking on past 13F returns orders whom to study; in the scorecard earlier this week, 13F records did not persist (rank correlation +0.14 / −0.05).
