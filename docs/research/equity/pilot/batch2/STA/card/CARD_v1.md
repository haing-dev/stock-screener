# Investor card: Murray Stahl (Horizon Kinetics), gate G4

Input: 132 verified claims (11 rule_status both, 3 third, 118 no). `date_said` is the document date. STA3 quotes passages from earlier years; where a claim's `period_meant` or note gives the year he first said it, that year is shown in brackets.

## 1. What he buys and why

He buys hard-asset, land and royalty companies whose value does not show in reported earnings: "dormant assets ... potentially productive, but ... dormant at the moment" (STA3-C039), which the market discounts "at a very, very high rate" (STA3-C040) and which act as a "call option on everything" (STA3-C056, C053; Franco Nevada STA1-C025, C027). He wants "scarcity value of a hard asset encased in scarcity value of corporate structure", meaning a "rare company that's not likely to access the capital markets" (STA3-C047, C048). Royalty and land firms need no reinvestment, so shareholders keep the earnings (STA1-C022, STA3-C054). He likes firms "not included in any index" that "keep buying back their stock" (STA3-C080; TPL STA3-C074, C075, C023). Owner-operators, where management owns more equity than anyone else, are underweighted by indexes and trade cheaply, but this is a principle and not a verified rule (STA3-C012, C029, C036, C037, C038). His reasoning is that indexation is a price-insensitive buyer that leaves such firms unanalysed and has cut the number of undervalued securities (STA3-C010, C013, C014, C019). After decades of disinflation, indexes hold almost no hard assets (STA3-C057, STA1-C014, C015), and he expects inflation to come back (STA1-C001, C004, C005). He also buys where a dividend cut or a lack of production has pushed the price down (STA1-C030). He holds for a very long time: he wants a compelling reason before he sells (STA3-C082, C005, C002).

## 6. Gaps (what a screen needs that he does not state)

- Owner-operators: repeated five times (STA3-C012, C029, C036, C037, C038) but never a verified rule. The definition "own more equity than anyone else" (C036) could be computed (management = largest holder), but no ownership threshold is given.

- Price: no valuation multiple, discount or yield. He says to adjust P/E for the part of earnings that must be reinvested (STA1-C021, C023) and that land is not marked to market (STA1-C024), but gives no formula.
- Rprice1: no size of dividend cut (zero in the example), no size of price fall, and "cost-effective" production is undefined.
- Sell: no general trigger. "Compelling reason" is undefined. No holding period number (long-term only, STA3-C005).
- Size: no position size, concentration or crypto weight (13F needed).
- Avoid: no exclusion rule.
- Dates: all STA3 claims carry date_said 2026-09-08, but most were first said in 1995-2026 (per period_meant and notes). Rule dating must use those years, not the document date.
