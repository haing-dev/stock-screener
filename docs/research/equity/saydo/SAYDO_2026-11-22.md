# Say-do tables, first run (2026-11-22; descriptive, no return)

Job `eq_saydo_v1` (eq_ref 1.1.0 c6021bb1..., V04_REGISTRATION A9). Report `../eq_saydo_v1_report.txt`; table
`SAYDO_TABLE_2026-11-22.csv` (masked ids M01.., one row per id and measure). What it holds: for each conviction
purchase (R2 versions a and b, 2010-2025) the stock's percentile inside its GICS sector, among the 1,500 largest US
lines that pass price and liquidity, at the end of the quarter BEFORE the purchase quarter. 0.50 = the sector median.
Cells with fewer than 20 purchases are blank. No price return of any stock or portfolio is computed.

## Counts
4,573 conviction purchases matched (version a 1,141, version b 4,444); 3,405 were in the universe the quarter before
(roster 76%, control 68%; per investor 28% to 99%, median 78%). The rest are smaller or less liquid companies, new
listings and foreign lines: for a few investors most purchases are outside the test universe (R3), which limits what
a recipe tested on that universe can say about them. 26 investors have rows (three have no 13F).

## What stands out
1. Investors differ far more than the pooled lines suggest: earnings yield runs from 0.22 to 0.92 and book to price
   from 0.11 to 0.74 across investors. The pooled averages are the wrong level to write recipes from.
2. An investor's tilt on valuation, size, sales growth and momentum is the same in odd and even years (correlation
   0.79 to 0.92), so "revealed by purchases" thresholds on these are stable enough to use (odd years set, even
   years check). Piotroski and revisions are not (0.37): investors do not select on them consistently.
3. High return on equity is common to nearly everyone (no investor at or below 0.40), control included. It does not
   tell investors apart and cannot by itself be called a roster investor's edge.
4. Size: purchases sit near the top of their sector by market value. Part of this is mechanical (large funds, top-five
   weights), so size is a constraint to hold fixed in recipes, not a criterion.

## Limits
- The mask is weak against anyone who also has `eq_conv_v2_report.txt` (purchase counts per named investor match the
  counts printed here). Packs for strategy writers must carry the table WITHOUT the count line and with the sector
  shares rounded; the pack builder does this (not built yet).
- The WRDS form uses Compustat values that may be restated; the LSEG form (first reported) repeats this when its files
  exist.
- Percentiles are inside today's test universe (1,500 largest); 24% of roster purchases are outside it.
- Nothing here is evidence that any purchase or recipe paid.
