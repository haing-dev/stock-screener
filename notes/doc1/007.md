### 7. Greg Jensen — Co-CIO of Bridgewater (NBIM, Nov 2025)

*Co-CIO of Bridgewater.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zRkNO9yNYoI)

**Takeaway:** Three forces drive markets now: modern mercantilism, the AI build-out, and record capital concentration in US equities. Diversify globally and build portfolios that survive many possible worlds.

- Mercantilism: trade is treated as zero-sum and the US is shifting from an "exceptional" to a "normal" place to invest.
- AI: "the bubble is ahead of us, not behind us." A resource grab for power, chips and fewer than 1,000 top scientists is under way. Nvidia's vendor financing is about controlling its ecosystem, not padding revenue.
- Inflation base case \~3%. AI accounts for 1–1.5pp of US growth amid a two-track economy.
- "We're all Brazil now": developed countries are hitting fiscal limits. Expect a capital shortage as savers become spenders.
- 15 years of "just own US equities" made diversification look like a trap. Go global; Europe is investable and China helps diversify.
- Bridgewater compounds understanding: every belief is written down, stress-tested and coded. An AI-first fund has generated alpha since 2024.
- Risk control, not brilliance, is why Bridgewater survived. COVID was its biggest failure, from staying in the systematic straitjacket.
- Poker lesson: think in ranges, not single hands. Culture: radical transparency with feedback flowing upward. "Pain + reflection = progress."
