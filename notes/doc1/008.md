### 8. David Rubenstein — What makes great investors (NBIM, Dec 2025)

*Co-founder of Carlyle.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6FPr2fkWsH4)

**Takeaway:** Great investors tend to be hungry, numerate and humble people who cut losers fast and go against consensus. Great leaders persuade mostly by example.

- Common traits:
  - often a middle-class upbringing, giving the hunger to endure a business where you're often wrong;
  - good at maths;
  - humble;
  - quick to exit what isn't working;
  - good with people.
- Wealth inflates ego (Buffett excepted). He is giving away nearly all his money so his children build their own careers.
- Academic stars aren't necessarily the best investors. Being smart helps; luck helps more.
- The best investments go against conventional wisdom. That's hard because people want to be liked.
- Carlyle's edge came from a constraint: being based in Washington, it specialized in defense and regulated sectors, then expanded into multiple strategies globally.
- Misses: he passed on Facebook ("a dating company") and sold early Amazon stock.
- Leader archetypes: visionary, builder, transformer, commander. Persuasion has three tools: speaking, writing and, most effective, leading by example.
- Interviewing: prepare deeply, use no notes, and listen and pivot. People love talking about their upbringing and their mistakes.
- He reads 100+ nonfiction books a year. "When you enjoy what you're doing, it's not work."
