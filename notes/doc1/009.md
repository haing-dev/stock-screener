### 9. Mala Gaonkar — Founder of SurgoCap (NBIM, Jan 2026)

*Ex-Lone Pine founding partner; ran the largest hedge fund launch by a woman.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GUc4krTatOM)

**Takeaway:** Find the few businesses with long-lasting moats where technology meets non-tech industries. Use data science and checklists to debias fast, intuitive "system 1" judgment.

- Risk means loss of capital, not volatility. Her differentiator is how long a moat lasts.
- "Every business is a technology business": map the tech stack of non-tech firms. Old technologies disrupt in new ways (lithium-ion in autos, gaming GPUs in AI).
- Data science (survey bots, adoption tracking, scraping) makes research less biased. Attach every thesis to an unbiased tracking data point.
- "Telescope in one eye, microscope in the other": you can't abstract away 70 years of history.
- From FOMO to "TOMO", thoughtfully missing out. Red flags: buying purely on valuation, "messiah" founders, getting too excited.
- Value = incremental return on capital × the capital deployable at that return. Watch execution risk when capex is huge relative to the business.
- Shorts: "wrong for two years, right for a week." They need a catalyst within about 9 months.
- Lone Pine lessons:
  - Its Nokia short was right on the business, but Microsoft bought Nokia's phone business, so think systemically.
  - Avoid public LBOs.
  - She sold Nvidia too early and never revisited it (sunk-cost bias).
- 100% conviction is a red flag.
