### 10. Dilhan Pillay — CEO of Temasek (NBIM, Sep 2025)

*CEO of Temasek (\~US$300B).* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dsi84KyiP6Y)

**Takeaway:** You can't predict the future, so build an institution around long-term themes, scenario planning, geopolitical capability, partnerships and capital recycling.

- Temasek is an equity-owning holding company, not a fund manager. It must recycle capital, and China's "true alpha" was monetized and redeployed to the US and Europe.
- Seven very smart people recorded 12-month predictions and were \~80% wrong. The answer is clear objectives, disciplined allocation and organizational "muscle": a DC geopolitics office since 2017, plus portfolio strategy, risk and AI.
- Themes: digitalization and AI, future consumption, longer lifespans, sustainable living (really energy security).
- Its private share rose from \~20% to \~50%. Be more careful now: PE needs excellent manager selection, and private credit is fine only with cash yield rather than payment-in-kind (PIK).
- Partnerships should add skills, not just spread risk: "relational, not transactional."
- Energy transition: "brown → light brown → green." India is a consumption story.
- AI at Temasek: train associates to work "two levels above" and aim for a narrower range of outcomes.
- On Singapore: "the worst thing is to protect our own companies."
