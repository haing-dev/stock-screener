### 11. Sinan Xin — "Conviction is dangerous" (Odds on Open, Feb 2026)

*Emerging-markets tech long/short manager.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h9o3gA377Rg)

**Takeaway:** Edge starts with knowing yourself. Build a portfolio that isolates stock-picking skill, treat "conviction" like a loaded gun, and invest in relationships and behavioral self-awareness, the edges AI won't commoditize.

- Your background is your edge. Know where you're an insider and where you're an outsider.
- Look for big change, where the spread of outcomes is wide enough for research to yield a differentiated view. Find your "right to win."
- In COVID he built a dropshipping site to understand Shopify and digital ads.
- "Conviction" is often bias. Earned conviction comes from bull and bear cycles.
- Isolate skill:
  - ≤25% in any one country;
  - style-agnostic;
  - \~20 longs and 30 shorts;
  - 30–40% net exposure.
- Size with a loss budget. If a stock can fall 50%, it can't be 10% of the book unless you accept losing 5% of NAV.
- His biggest edge is decade-long relationships: a China e-commerce executive he has called every quarter for 10 years.
- Tools become table stakes; behavior is the durable edge. Executives with polished English felt trustworthy, yet their stocks did worse.
- Career: choose what you'd stay up all night doing. Personal fit beats the hottest sector.
