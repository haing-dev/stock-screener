### 12. Derek Pilecki — Finding edge in financials (Odds on Open, Apr 2026)

*Gator Capital; >20% annualized over the past 5 years.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eTYfrjWPnZs)

**Takeaway:** Stay in a sector where you have an edge. Buy positive change the market under-reacts to, add to winners rather than losers, and don't get bored doing the simple thing well.

- Generalists ignore financials, which creates opportunity. He was underweight banks into SVB, then went overweight regionals in 2023 once deposits proved stable.
- Moving from 25 to \~40 names raised returns, because ranking ideas is unreliable.
- "Positions can become less risky at higher prices." Add when the market confirms you; falling prices often mean you're wrong.
- Hurdle: an under-followed name that can double in 3 years.
- Edge comes from repetition (100–120 management meetings a year) and a different macro view: post-crisis banks are well capitalized. Private-credit risks: hidden double leverage, zero recoveries on failed SaaS loans.
- He uses AI to diff 10-K risk factors year over year. With fewer active readers, footnotes pay.
- "No reward for degree of difficulty." Trade smaller in cold streaks and bigger in hot ones.
- Breaking in: publish \~10 stock write-ups to build a following.
