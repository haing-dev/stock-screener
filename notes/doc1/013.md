### 13. David Orr — Ex-poker pro betting against beta (Odds on Open, Jan 2026)

*Founder of Militia Capital; >40% annualized.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NpqZ2QVRhYw)

**Takeaway:** Treat investing like poker. Make many positive-expected-value bets, cut whatever isn't working within months, and never "stay wrong" to protect your ego.

- He left poker when solver bots ended his edge.
- The "Betting Against Beta" paper: high-beta stocks earn *lower* returns. He started by shorting bad high-beta stocks.
- He hunts lopsided bets: 2020 Eurodollar options paid \~200:1. Once the asymmetry is gone, move on.
- He holds \~350 positions. Simple long themes: airports, gas pipelines, Japan governance reform. Shorts: structurally doomed businesses and empire-building managers, but never frauds.
- Pod shops' virtue is that they can't stay wrong, but their fees of \~4–5% a year are "distasteful."
- "The market moving against you is one of the strongest tells." Avoid 30% drawdowns, which need +43% to recover.
- Learn with a $5–10K account, 500–1,000 reps and public "fund letters." Operations roles are an underrated way in.
