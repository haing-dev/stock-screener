### 16. Quantedge — Inside Asia's $6B quant fund (Odds on Open, Jul 2026)

*CEO of Quantedge, Singapore.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3LFV27B3A-U)

**Takeaway:** The edge isn't information. Extreme diversification across \~300 markets lets Quantedge run high volatility (\~25%) for \~20% net annual returns, protected by locked-up capital and an all-in, long-term culture.

- It started around 2005 with \~$3M and is now \~$6B, of which \~$5B is gains. Day-one investors are up \~40x.
- Information ratio ≈ skill × √(independent bets). With 300 markets, ruin risk is low even at high volatility.
- Staff put essentially all their liquid wealth in the fund. They refuse to halve volatility for allocators, because the low-vol space is crowded.
- Never run a black box: every signal needs a simple economic rationale, and PMs must be able to explain each day's trades.
- Core drivers stay simple: expected returns, volatility, price, liquidity, correlations. Generative AI stays out of production models.
- In 2018 they introduced fixed-term share classes, a choice they estimate cost \~$20B of assets. "Funds die from redemptions at the wrong time, not drawdowns."
- They hire almost only graduates, reward people collectively and aim to run for 50 years.
