### 17. Versor Investments — Quant strategies managing $1.4B (Odds on Open, Oct 2025)

*Partners Nishant Gurnani and Dwayne Lewis.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Tpdw1OcbS0)

**Takeaway:** Be "empirical market scientists." Start from a hypothesis and a well-framed problem, then use AI and alternative data as tools; never mine data first and tell stories after.

- Every research spec states its hypothesis and causal mechanism. "Humans are incredibly good at finding patterns where they don't exist." Keep thin or contradictory data as a running experiment rather than trading it.
- Merger arbitrage has three outcomes, not two. \~92% of deals close, \~8% fail, and \~16% close at a *higher* price, so predicting bumps is an edge.
- The deal model covers failure odds, bump odds, the downside (a biotech target can go to \~zero), time to close and path volatility, giving a per-deal Sharpe for sizing.
- The index-futures strategy trades 24 markets in relative value, has positive convexity and low correlation to trend followers.
- Alpha in illiquid markets is "a mirage" unless you can trade size.
- Hiring: a deep dive into the candidate's own research plus a live coding test ("you can tell in 2 minutes"). They like people used to messy data, such as astrophysicists.
