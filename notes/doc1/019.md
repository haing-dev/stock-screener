### 19. Omer Cedar (ex-Two Sigma) — Why you should bet against conviction (Odds on Open, Jul 2026)

*Built Two Sigma's alpha-capture program; founder of Omega Point.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uqYbVIwCBy4)

**Takeaway:** Human judgment is still a quant input. Capture it often, trade *changes* in opinion, fade experts who get even more extreme, and follow the first one who breaks from the crowd.

- In March 2020 quant models lost 5–10% while discretionary PMs didn't. Humans can reason about a future with no data points.
- Two Sigma turned PMs' questions into a structured survey across 3,000–4,000 stocks. Marshall Wace's TOPS started as an intern's summer test.
- The edge comes from sampling frequency.
- Deltas beat levels: the strongest signal is an analyst coming off the fence. When already-extreme experts get more extreme, bet against them.
- Contributors stay for information, not revenue share.
- Avoid "hot hands": performance mean-reverts.
- He predicts \~10x more hedge funds in 5 years, and GenAI may make markets *noisier*. "Context becomes currency."
- Skilled PMs' #1 mistake is starting a well-researched idea at 1–2% instead of 4–5%.
