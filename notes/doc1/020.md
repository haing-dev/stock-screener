### 20. Ying (ex-Balyasny) — Quant is blackjack, fundamental is poker (Odds on Open, Jul 2026)

*Former quantamental PM covering insurance; founder of Implied.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fG-pl1mFQVY)

**Takeaway:** Quant is anything you can decide from historical patterns without knowing the company. Fundamental is "how is this time different." Combine them, with quant for sizing and data and humans for judgment.

- "Fundamental investors are not good at sizing; quant funds are."
- Creative data: highway-patrol accident data across 15 states gave a daily insurance loss ratio; celebrity tweets mapped Malibu wildfire damage.
- Data gathering is \~100% automatable, processing \~80%, judgment \~0%.
- Demand for talent grows. AI is a quant wave for words that collapses pattern-matchable alpha and raises payoffs for differentiated views.
- LLMs alone fail: they pattern-match, lack context and expectations, and behave like smart grads with no domain knowledge.
- Some sectors are a positioning game, not a fundamentals game.
- Time horizons: hours to days is blackjack (pure odds), weeks to months is poker (reading others), years is surfing trends.
- "Choice matters more than hard work." Journal every decision. Strong emotions and rigidity are tells of a bad investor.
