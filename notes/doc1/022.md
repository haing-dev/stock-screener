### 22. Cory Paddock — Great traders know when a regime change is coming (Odds on Open, Oct 2025)

*Co-founder and CEO of GBE, a proprietary power-trading firm.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SUDKvKev1Js)

**Takeaway:** In power markets the biggest edge is a view on a paradigm shift before the market trades it. Historical data is a trap when the grid keeps changing.

- "The perfect amount of darkness": thousands of pages of obscure market rules form a barrier that crowded equities lack.
- Traders watch 5-minute grid data, not just prices. Power is extremely recency-biased: "If you want to trade an insight from 8 years ago, we're happy to take the other side."
- Paradigm shifts: coal → gas → wind → solar, and now, for the first time in his career, rising demand from data centers, EVs and heat pumps.
- LLMs help with training but hallucinate on market rules and can be argued into agreeing with you.
- Power returns are weather-driven and uncorrelated with equities.
- Returns are chunky: 5–10 trades make most of a year. Never force setups; judge process over outcome.
- Incentives: avoid traders who accept blow-up risk, and remove pay uncertainty. He is bullish on Gen Z traders.
