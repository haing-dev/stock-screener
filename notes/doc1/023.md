### 23. Ernie Chan — Generating alpha from market crashes (Odds on Open clip, Sep 2025)

*Founder of QTS Capital and PredictNow.ai.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fIrlQDvcy_M)

**Takeaway:** QTS's core strategy is "crisis alpha" that pays off in crashes. Regime change only hurts strategies that ignore regime variables, so feed models everything and let AI decide what matters.

- An FX strategy worked for months and then its alpha vanished, a common pattern.
- The crisis strategy behaves like owning options. Since 2011 a crisis has come roughly every two years.
- If your strategy suffers when rates rise, why weren't rates an input?
- AI can add news context (e.g., Microsoft's OpenAI deal) to simple causal pairs trades.
