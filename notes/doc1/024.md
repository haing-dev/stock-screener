### 24. Zach (25) — Running a capacity-constrained multi-manager fund (Odds on Open, Mar 2026)

*Founder of a small multi-manager fund; ex-biotech alpha-capture PM.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EacHSxUjy78)

**Takeaway:** Turn small size into an edge. Back niche, capacity-constrained relative-value PMs the big pods can't use, giving uncorrelated returns without salaries or heavy pass-through costs.

- Only hire PMs to do exactly what they've done successfully for 3+ years. Asking for "5–10% different" lowers success rates.
- He prefers relative value: skill is easier to judge and returns are uncorrelated.
- Sizing: inverse-volatility weights by default, with a discretionary tilt toward PMs whose niche is "printing," under a hard risk cap.
- "No top PM's secret sauce is 'we read more Ks and Qs.'" Top PMs are \~90–95% conventional plus a clever twist. "Clever beats working harder."
- Big pods have too much capital and crowded PMs, leading to "air pockets" in risk-off. Pass-through fees run \~4–6.5%.
- He expects mega-pods plus a long tail of niche boutiques, with the middle squeezed.
- To start: keep a "hedge fund" document and resolve one launch item a day for years.
