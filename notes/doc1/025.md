### 25. Dimitri Bianco — "Your finance degree is worthless" (Odds on Open, May 2025)

*Head of quant risk at a fintech; YouTuber.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i01UEfGh2eM)

**Takeaway:** Quant careers reward a deep love of the actual maths and statistics, not status or money. Pick skills you'd enjoy for 20 years and leaders who respect quant work.

- Quant firms want maths and statistics people who know finance. Rewriting his resume as pure statistics got him two offers in a month.
- "People don't quit companies, they quit leaders." Grind-culture firms treat quants as commodities.
- Pay math: $180K for 80-hour weeks earns less per hour than $100K for 40.
- Chase interests, not the market. Go deep on one rigorous project. Test: what would you do if every job paid nothing?
- His grandfather's advice: "You'd better learn to love to work."
