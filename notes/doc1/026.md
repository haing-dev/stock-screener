### 26. Chris Kantos — How NLP can generate alpha (Odds on Open, Aug 2025)

*Head of quant research at Alexandria Technology.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OoMhgMA2BoM)

**Takeaway:** Text becomes alpha only when models are trained on the right document-specific labels. Match the data to your horizon: news decays fast, earnings calls drift for months.

- Dictionary methods are arbitraged away, and FinBERT misreads earnings calls. Train a classifier per document type: a reporter's praise is positive, a CEO's self-praise is not.
- News alpha lasts intraday to a few days. Earnings calls stay useful \~1.5 months; using only forward-looking statements lifted the Sharpe from \~1.2 to \~1.7.
- Arms race: companies pre-test their calls against algorithms. "Lazy Prices" is now arbitraged.
- Reddit data was useless until meme stocks. Retail sentiment × short interest flags squeezes.
- "You choose to ignore what you choose to ignore." His former firm calculated Madoff's returns had \~1-in-22-million odds of being real.
- Don't take research at face value: most "hockey-stick" backtests hide a flaw.
