### 27. Ernie Chan — Use GenAI to manage risk, not predict returns (Odds on Open, Aug 2025)

*Founder of QTS Capital; CEO of PredictNow.ai; author of* Quantitative Trading. [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VzF-tvz3DAk)

**Takeaway:** Finance suffers from data scarcity, so use machine learning mainly for risk management and portfolio optimization. GenAI's real promise is pre-training and semi-supervised learning on unlabeled data. Always try to disprove your own hypothesis.

- Structural breaks leave maybe \~20 usable years of data, against millennia of text for LLMs.
- Aim lower: risk management and portfolio construction, with explainable near-linear models such as regression trees.
- GenAI advantages:
  - pre-training on loosely related series (electricity demand, shipping);
  - semi-supervised learning: pre-train on unlabeled Fed transcripts, hand-label a few, and skip paying vendors for sentiment scores.
- Replicating a fully disclosed paper took months, and top AI engineers earn eight-figure bonuses.
- Big funds have money but bureaucracy; small funds lack talent. It's a "Nash equilibrium."
- Expect \~50% of chatbot code answers to be wrong. Causal reasoning matters more than syntax.
- Repeatedly tweaking against out-of-sample data makes it in-sample: a Sharpe of 1.5 in backtest becomes 0.5 live. Use the deflated Sharpe ratio and falsification.
