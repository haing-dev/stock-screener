### 28. Bill Gebhardt — Why AI can't find alpha (Odds on Open, Dec 2025)

*Runs a trend-following commodity fund; Cornell finance PhD; ex-Barclays Global Investors.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=csaXRXHQhys)

**Takeaway:** Data is too scarce and relationships too unstable for AI to discover durable alpha. The winning setup is human judgment on top of a robust system, with AI speeding up research rather than generating ideas.

- After five systematic years he added a discretionary filter: avoid choppy, oversupplied markets and lean into strong trends. It's like AI-augmented chess players beating machines alone.
- "Backtests mostly convince yourself." Investors believe only live records.
- One set of signals runs across every liquid market and timeframe, with no parameter optimization.
- He watched fundamental Sharpe ratios fall as information spread, and trend timing ended up beating his own discretion.
- The best environment is low volatility with a strong trend.
- Why AI struggles:
  - Seasonal energy gives \~2 data points a year.
  - Language is stable; markets aren't.
  - Any stable structure AI found would be arbitraged instantly.
  - ML is "fancy regression" that can't extrapolate.
- AI now writes a master's-level research plan in 15 minutes.
- Juniors who work remotely with AI lose learning, so work in an office.
- "Full flexibility finds amazing systems that don't last": start from a philosophy of how prices move.
