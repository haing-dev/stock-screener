### 29. Yoshi Yokokawa (ex-Lehman, Alpaca CEO) — AI won't give you edge (Odds on Open, Oct 2026)

*Co-founder and CEO of Alpaca (API-first brokerage).* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E-kC8n9Q1_E)

**Takeaway:** AI shortens the trial-and-error loop for finding edge, but edge only pays if a human executes it with discipline, and execution is a profession most people can't master.

- "Is there edge for a retail quant? Yes, when you create one."
- Executing rules through losing streaks is unnatural, "like boxing form." Greed and fear don't change.
- Successful systematic retail traders pause when the regime changes, retune, then return.
- The retail/institutional line is blurring: monthly API developers went from \~20K to 80K+ in six months. Expect many more "AI hedge funds."
- "Try not to compete with anyone": go one layer down, as infrastructure.
- Shortcuts come from optimizing for the true objective. Product-market fit can expire, so stay paranoid.
- Markets are a fair "street fight" that judges results only. "Nothing lasts forever" was his Lehman lesson.
