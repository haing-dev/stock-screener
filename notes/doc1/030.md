### 30. Ex-IMC options trader — AI is good enough to beat the S&P 500 (Odds on Open, Sep 2026)

*Former IMC semiconductor-options trader; founder of a perp-futures trading app. The transcript is machine-translated and garbled in places.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sdBhXlC3Vxs)

**Takeaway:** Market-making edge comes from seeing the flow, not predicting the future. Frontier models could beat the S&P risk-adjusted only inside well-engineered harnesses.

- Two frameworks: predict the future, or observe the present and buy cheap and sell rich. Options trading is volatility trading.
- Flow is the signal. A few big market makers see most US options flow: "trade against the stupid, follow the smart." "The position is more valuable than the person."
- Blow-ups come from correlation errors: after DeepSeek, Nvidia fell \~17% while its peers fell less.
- Narrative edge ranks private conversations first, then Twitter/Substack, with Bloomberg/WSJ last.
- "AI is not a bubble" means it compounds above the equity risk premium, not that it avoids 50–70% drawdowns. His own levered AI book swung from +470% to −70%.
- In zero-sum industries moats matter; in hypergrowth, growth beats moats.
