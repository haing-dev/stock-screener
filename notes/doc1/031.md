### 31. Akash (ex-Third Point) — AI agents are encoding PM workflows (Odds on Open)

*Built Third Point's data team from 2017; now at RavenPack.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xLmNgapNMb0)

**Takeaway:** Alpha lies in a differentiated, quantified opinion. AI agents now encode analysts' judgment into workflows, so each analyst can cover 3x more names, and quant and fundamental investing are converging.

- Six months went into point-in-time data infrastructure. In year 1 a team of 4–5 onboarded \~100 datasets.
- Dan Loeb: "If you cannot put a number on it, you don't have a differentiated opinion."
- Analysts want facts, not forecasts; confidence intervals matter most. Energy didn't suit alternative data.
- Off-the-shelf risk models are for reporting. Alpha needs custom factors (e.g., an AI supply-chain factor).
- The tooling path ran search → RAG → knowledge graph → agentic harness. Opus 4.5 raised accuracy from \~70% to \~90%.
- An earnings-preview workflow uses \~35M tokens to replace \~1,000 hours of work. Coverage grows from \~20 to 60–70 names. "Your judgment is being encoded."
- Loeb culture: up at 5am, finds the flaw on page 26, "kind but not nice."
