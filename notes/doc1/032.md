### 32. Matei (System 2) — How billionaire hedge fund managers use GenAI (Odds on Open, Mar 2026)

*Founder of System 2, a data-science firm for fundamental funds.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Eh8pYEVtDcg)

**Takeaway:** Alpha means being right when others are wrong, which happens at outliers, exactly where data and AI are least reliable. AI rewards insatiably curious investors and punishes lazy ones.

- The outlier paradox: you need the data most when it's least reliable. One model drew equal market shares because "it had no data."
- LLM memory can become a personal echo chamber, as can founders treated like gods.
- Fine-tune models on past expert-call transcripts to generate the *next good question*.
- Map causal graphs to find second-order effects: oil layoffs → a Texas-heavy apparel brand.
- Every answer spawns three questions, and curious clients sometimes know more than the company's IR team.
- Research vendors face negative network effects, so System 2 does custom work and goes "whale hunting."
- Billionaire traits:
  - frugal (cheap birthday cards);
  - curious (Python classes in his late 50s);
  - undiversified ("bet it all");
  - in love with the craft but not with running the business, so many funds carry key-man risk.
