### 35. Jack Dorsey — CEO of Block (NBIM, Feb 2025)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=e_Y9tI4dALA)

**Takeaway:** Build on open protocols, not walled gardens. Twitter's mistakes were rushing into advertising and owning the whole stack. Bitcoin is to money what HTTP is to the web.

- Twitter grew out of a childhood obsession with city maps and dispatch. Users invented @replies, hashtags and retweets: "participation means creation."
- With an ad model, "users become the product and advertisers dictate direction."
- "The algorithms are programming us." He wants an algorithm store where you choose or build your own feed.
- He backed Nostr (built by individuals, signed with personal keys) over Bluesky (corporate).
- If Bitcoin becomes currency, Block can launch in any country without local bank partners. Visa and Mastercard should build on open rails.
- Entrepreneurs are artists who know where the work begins and ends.
- There is no rejection, only whether you choose to learn.
- Daily do and don't-do lists, tiny goals (meditate 1 minute a day), 3 hours of morning learning.
- "Observe for a long time, then do."
