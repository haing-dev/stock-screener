### 36. Nikesh Arora — CEO of Palo Alto Networks (NBIM, Apr 2025)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WXH5Zg7HAcI)

**Takeaway:** Obsess over product, take managed risks, and reason from first principles about where a technology wave lands. In a crisis, people's need for certainty comes before strategy.

- "The attacker has to be right once; we have to be right 100% of the time."
- Attackers evolved from hobbyists to ransomware to nation states, and now target the supply chain. Security "flows like water" to the weakest point, so focus on resilience.
- Cyber is young and fragmented and will consolidate into platforms; he sees room for a 20% player.
- Larry Page: "Tech companies die when they stop focusing on product." Masa Son: risk appetite that grows with age.
- Hired with no cyber background, he learned through 18 months of questions and meetings with 300 startups. Market cap went from \~$18B to \~$120B.
- Seat-based software must rethink pricing, and phone apps will be disintermediated by agents.
- Platformization meant accepting short-term pain. "I'd have made my smart decisions faster."
- In COVID: "Your job isn't to talk about winning but to create certainty." No layoffs.
