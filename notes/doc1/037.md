### 37. David Vélez — CEO of Nubank (NBIM, Oct 2025)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=npVZsYaoJs0)

**Takeaway:** Find a large, oligopoly-protected market full of consumer pain and attack it with a 20x cost advantage and fanatical customer love; growth then comes by word of mouth.

- As a VC he "secretly envied the person on the other side of the table."
- Banks hold 80–85% share in most countries. Opening his own Brazilian account took 5 months, with a card at 1,200% interest.
- \~30 experts said it was impossible. "They ignore you, laugh at you, fight you, then you win."
- Purple was the "most anti-bank color." Customer acquisition cost \~$2–3 over 12 years. 100M customers served with 6,000 staff versus incumbents' 100,000.
- Credit first: it is 70% of the profit pool. A waiting list made the card scarce, and invitations fed the credit model.
- NPS hit 90–94. After a bug, it refunded customers proactively.
- Expansion is a "call option," not betting the house. "AI will put a bank *and a banker* in everyone's pocket."
- "Companies grow exponentially; humans grow linearly." Hire missionaries, not mercenaries.
