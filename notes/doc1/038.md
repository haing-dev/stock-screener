### 38. Forrest Li — CEO of Sea (NBIM, Oct 2025)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6FTdUO8D20o)

**Takeaway:** Ride the big technology wave (mobile, now AI), build local solutions from first principles where no playbook exists, and stay humble.

- Sea's value grew \~100x in 10 years on the mobile wave. He believes a trillion-dollar path runs through AI.
- Shopee had no e-commerce playbook, so it built its own logistics and address system. "Put your best talent on the unglamorous problems." Competitors "come and go in waves."
- AI handles \~80% of millions of daily customer inquiries at human-level satisfaction. AI teammates play with shy gamers. Anyone in the company can build agents.
- Fintech grew from necessity: he once delivered bags of game cards to 7-Elevens himself.
- At the 2021 share-price peak he felt "really unsettled" and warned staff against complacency.
- Leaders take the hardest, unpopular decisions themselves; he personally signed the freeze on pay and bonuses.
- He named himself after Forrest Gump. Advice: do small good things; the ripple effects can be big.
