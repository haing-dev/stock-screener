### 39. Jayshree Ullal — CEO of Arista Networks (NBIM, Dec 2025)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zWtZ1UwKJlI)

**Takeaway:** The AI build-out is an "explosive megatrend," not a classic bubble: constrained by power, funded by responsible companies and pulled by real demand.

- Chips are the engine; networking is the highway. Network speed upgrades used to take 5 years and now take 12–18 months.
- Power is the binding constraint: data centers now need tens of gigawatts, and finding power takes 3–5 years.
- Unlike the dot-com era it can't be overbuilt: "Please build it, we need it now."
- "I won't apologize for customer concentration" (Microsoft and Meta are each >10% of revenue).
- Arista didn't take on Cisco head-on. It found white spaces: high-frequency trading, then cloud.
- Average support resolution is 25 minutes, ICU-style.
- Culture: "do the right thing." It once replaced customers' gear at its own cost, nearly going bankrupt.
- Next 3 years: AI goes from mainframe-scale clusters to distributed. Advice: pursue your path of excellence.
