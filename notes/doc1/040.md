### 40. Dylan Field — CEO of Figma (NBIM, Jan 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IWyTkUS7yC4)

**Takeaway:** As software becomes abundant and easy to build, moats shrink and design, craft and taste become the differentiator. AI "lowers the floor and raises the ceiling."

- Design went from "lipstick on a pig" to a core differentiator. Great companies have a "why now" (for Figma, WebGL).
- "Keep simple things simple; make complex things possible": software should be a staircase to mastery.
- AI lets you sample a whole option space and choose your path deliberately. Taste is a loop. AI only emulates taste through skilled prompting.
- Remaining moats are liquidity (data, marketplace, social) and regulation. "You won't vibe-code your way to Salesforce," but competition will soar.
- Use AI to learn, not only to cut costs: his example was a son who lets AI do homework versus a daughter who makes 3x the problem sets.
- Hiring: degrees are ignored. Hire for "slope" (rate of improvement) and update your priors on people.
- Mistakes: launched after 4 years out of perfectionism and should have hired faster ("I was a bad manager"). Microsoft had to tell him to charge.
- He spins emergent use cases into separate products (FigJam) and dogfoods everything.
- "Show your work" so people can reason without you. Push back hard only on one-way-door decisions. Kindness means being direct.
- "I fear not trying more than rejection." "Optimism is a choice."
