### 41. Reid Hoffman — Co-founder of LinkedIn (NBIM, Feb 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qy7L6XbaKio)

**Takeaway:** AI is the most impactful technology of our lifetimes and not a collapse-style bubble. Organizations that try to remove every risk before deploying will never "get on the road." Great investments are contrarian *and* right.

- At worst, expect price corrections, not contagion: overpaid-for data centers still run profitably. "Intelligence will be as available as electricity."
- "If you're not finding frontier models useful in substantive work, you're not trying hard enough." Use them for medical second opinions and deep research.
- Record every meeting and let AI produce follow-ups. "Minutes of compute buy instant research."
- Large firms drive every risk to zero first, "like eliminating all risk before driving from Oslo to Trondheim." Compliance can kill companies.
- Europe as referee in the US–China AI match: "the referee never wins; get on the pitch."
- Blitzscaling, "taking the risk of going big amid uncertainty," is exactly what the AI labs are doing.
- Entrepreneurs: super-ambitious, take smart risks, are "multi-tool athletes," and fit the problem (Chesky's design background suits Airbnb).
- With Airbnb he offered to invest minutes into the pitch. The arc runs from crazy at day 0 to feasible at years 2–3 to obvious at years 5–7.
- "I saw why smart people thought it was dumb, and why I was right": LinkedIn, Facebook, Airbnb.
- Advice: pitch yourself as AI-native.
