### 42. Robert Gentz — Co-founder of Zalando (NBIM, Mar 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iRJBbClw4do)

**Takeaway:** A clear flywheel (more selection → more converted traffic → more selection) lets leadership align and scale extremely fast. Crisis-born frugality and a "dare to fail" culture sustain it.

- They tested demand with 20 flip-flops a day. Search data disproved "nobody buys shoes online."
- The 2008 crisis built character: they debated half a day over a €60 book.
- Revenue went from €5M to €1.2B in 4 years, and staff from 50 to 2,500.
- The flywheel runs from exact long-tail searches → brand → many brands → new countries.
- Logistics became a B2B product for brands (e.g., Next).
- Data and AI: sizing knowledge graph, body-scan sizing, discovery feed, a natural-language assistant, city trend data.
- Versus Temu and Shein, Zalando plays a quality game rather than a price game.
- An "enabling" introvert leader who trusts by default and steps in only on ambiguous or long-term calls and on culture.
- 14 leadership principles. Without calculated risk you play "poker without blinds."
- Europe needs a positive "European dream": a real single market. Advice: "Stay in Europe. Make it work."
