### 43. Brian Armstrong — CEO of Coinbase (NBIM, Mar 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jmdf35zSdA8)

**Takeaway:** Compete on trust. Stablecoins are the only payment rail that is fast, cheap *and* global. Entrepreneurs must be right about things that aren't yet consensus, and "action produces information."

- At Airbnb, payouts cost 7–10% and took 3–5 days. The Bitcoin paper delivered provable digital scarcity.
- "If it's consensus, you're too late." Being on the spectrum makes him comfortable asking "dumb" questions.
- The moat is trust: compliance, US domicile, public audits. Coinbase holds >12% of all crypto.
- Stablecoins are a \~$300B market growing \~100% a year and are the \~16th-largest holder of Treasuries. That's tiny next to \~$7T in money-market funds, so they don't threaten bank deposits.
- CBDCs: governments are good at policy, bad at software.
- > 50% of Coinbase's code is AI-written. One engineer who refused to try AI tools was fired.
- Agentic commerce: agents can't open bank accounts but can hold wallets. Its x402 protocol processed 100M transactions.
- "Anything innovative eventually intersects with government."
- He co-founded NewLimit (epigenetic reprogramming). "Show up for a decade; be willing to look foolish."
