### 44. Alain Lam — CFO of Xiaomi (NBIM, Apr 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9gbwHbIx9UI)

**Takeaway:** Xiaomi wins with "amazing products at honest prices," a loyal user base and China speed. Concentrate resources (10x R&D on one first car), own the core technology and outsource the rest.

- Its first phone sold at RMB 1,999 into a market of low-quality, high-price products.
- Local suppliers co-develop custom parts, which brings speed.
- An EV is "consumer electronics on wheels": \~3,000 R&D staff on a single model, launched in under 3 years. 50,000 sold in 30 minutes, 20%+ to buyers who never test-drove.
- Own the motors, battery and assisted driving; outsource the rest.
- Humanoid robots go into its own factories first. Physical-world data is the bottleneck.
- AI: 100+ material formulas simulated, X-ray defect inspection, sales prediction, plus its own open-source LLM.
- Lei Jun drove 150 car models; every executive drove 3,000 km before launch.
- Focus on the 3 things that matter each quarter. Read about failures: "99% of problems already have a solution."
