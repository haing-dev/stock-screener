### 45. Fabricio Bloisi — CEO of Prosus (NBIM, May 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yU68BbLp1Y0)

**Takeaway:** Run an "ambidextrous" organization: disciplined at scale, with startup-speed "jet skis." Build ecosystems on high-frequency services. AI is *under*-hyped.

- He failed for \~7 years before iFood. Bought for \~$5M at 20K orders a month, it now does \~200M.
- "I don't believe in being smart": work with people better than you. Hire entrepreneurs and fire when it doesn't work.
- Jet skis: 5–10 people test 20–30 versions before any big budget. Never fund $1B "because of an idea."
- Build on high-frequency food delivery (address and payment known), then cross-sell, as Tencent and Alibaba did.
- Europe forced him to sell Delivery Hero: "Europe succeeds in avoiding European big tech."
- In the last \~60 days, agents went from making individuals faster to running the company for 1–3 days. All executives learned to code, and decisions now run in weekly cycles.
- He spreads what works within two weeks and holds brutally transparent "strategy share" sessions.
- India runs profitable $3 e-commerce in 50 languages. "Innovation should start in Bangalore."
- Advice: "It's a lie that the world is how it is today."
