### 46. Yu Kai — CEO of Horizon Robotics (NBIM, Sep 2026)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mRzItLhpXFU)

**Takeaway:** "Sometimes a terrible business means a great business": long cycles and safety qualification are entry barriers. Be the platform ("the Wintel of robots"), not another car brand.

- His chips run in \~1 in 3 Chinese cars. He has had the same vision for 11 years: a unifying computing platform for all robots.
- China's car market is "crazy" (580+ new models in H1), and car compute has consolidated to Nvidia, Huawei and Horizon.
- 5–8 years from chip to production keeps VC-funded entrants out. "A good business lets you sleep at night."
- Roadmap: hands-off by 2028, eyes-off by 2030, mind-off by 2035.
- The AI formula is compute + meaningful corner-case data + bigger models.
- China is "a supply-chain wonderland" and "one village." Partner with Europe rather than fight it.
- Whoever wins the EV supply chain wins robotics. Humanoid volumes are \~5 years away.
- Zen: "when working, work; when sleeping, sleep." Do what you love, and love what you do.
