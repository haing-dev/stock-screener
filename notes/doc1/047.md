### 47. Saul Perlmutter — Why doubt is a superpower (NBIM, Dec 2025)

*Physics Nobel laureate (dark energy); co-author of* Third Millennium Thinking. [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Negpb5iCqTA)

**Takeaway:** Science's real method is a culture: think probabilistically, hunt your own mistakes, seek out people who disagree, and pair doubt (the brakes) with can-do confidence (the accelerator). These tools transfer directly to investing.

- We can now solve planet-scale problems. The leftover problem is how to think together.
- Bet in probabilities (90% on this, 70% on that), not true or false. That makes debate less ego-driven.
- "Individual humility, collective arrogance": \~95% of an experimentalist's life is hunting errors, yet it takes confidence to stay on a hard problem for years.
- **Blind analysis** beats confirmation bias: hide the result until every error check is agreed. Personal version: pick which sources you trust *before* seeing what they say about your case.
- For an investment decision:
  - Collect independent views in writing *first*, then aggregate. (Norway's Supreme Court lets the senior judge speak first and has the lowest dissent.)
  - Then do scenario planning: pick two unrelated driving forces and test all four extremes.
- The best investments and the best science are both counterintuitive. His team set out to measure the universe slowing down and found it accelerating.
- Gut feel is real but also sees patterns in noise. Vet intuition with statistics, and feed it with sustained focus.
- AI is double-edged: it can fake learning the basics, and it is overconfident.
- It took 5 years to get the first good supernova. Rival teams swapped telescope nights and shared the prize.
- Advice: ignore the doom framing of the news. "This is your moment."
