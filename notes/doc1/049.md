### 49. Lars Strannegård — President of the Stockholm School of Economics (NBIM, Mar 2025)

[Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fNQ6MOCF53U)

**Takeaway:** As machines get better at being machines, education should make people better at being human: empathy, sense-making, cultural literacy and agency. Art acts as "intellectual and emotional itching powder."

- Exposure to art you don't understand builds humility and empathy. Machines mimic empathy but can't make sense or tell truth.
- His classrooms are art installations. One built from rulers prompts debate on quantification: "what isn't counted doesn't count."
- *Bildung* is "what remains after you forget what you learned." The FREE model: Fact-based, Reflective, Empathetic, Entrepreneurial. It is "a vaccination against stale mental models."
- Every KPI improved after the change, though correlation isn't causation. "All big donations started with art."
- Great leaders are authentic, create meaning ("building a cathedral, not banging stones") and have energy.
- The Scandinavian model is flat, and "only the argument matters."
- Learn languages; Europe needs self-confidence. "Every education is watching the sky through a straw; you need many straws."
- Policing AI in exams is futile. The real question is the division of labor between humans and machines.
