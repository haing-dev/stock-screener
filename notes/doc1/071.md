### 71. Jim Flynn — The future of biotechnology (Qatar Economic Forum, May 2025)

*Managing Partner of Deerfield Management; 36 years in the business; Deerfield's own labs are developing 25 drugs.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wvt5O1-zNw4)

**Takeaway:** In May 2025 science and sentiment were "almost opposite". Biology is a "finite puzzle" being solved ever faster, while biotech had just finished a bubble and collapse, so "Things are cheap again."

- Three bubbles in 36 years: each brought 1–2 years of doubling or tripling, then 1–2 years falling "back to the same point." In May 2025 sentiment was "terrible", the sector cheap, and US regulatory uncertainty kept investors nervous.
- Bubbles open the IPO window to companies "too early, too risky, require too much cash." These "just have to go away" and management value expectations must reset, which takes time.
- Tractability prior: genetics-linked diseases and single-mutation cancers fall first. Brain diseases (schizophrenia has >100 associated genes; Alzheimer's) are "big and complicated" and need at least 15 more years of discovery.
- "So drugs are 20%. All of the infrastructure and everything else is 80%." Rising drug spend works if prevention shrinks the 80% "commensurately."
- Hospital-centric care is "super expensive" and treats disease after the fact (>3M preventable US cardiac procedures a year). Moving care into the home is "an innovators game", slowed by "sticky" reimbursement infrastructure.
- Insurers set premiums as "a guess", then push back on costly treatments short term. Longer term they back anything that bends chronic disease; Deerfield works with insurers on best care at lowest cost.
- NIH (May 2025): a "hugely successful investment" over decades, though overhead paid to some institutions is "a fair debate." Every administration looks to healthcare "for some pennies"; that tug of war lasts forever.
- Proof that innovation is cost-effective for whole societies is "5 to 10 years off" for most countries, and reimbursement rules must flex. Health span (quality of years) is easier to move than lifespan.
- Predictions (May 2025): software diagnoses most people within 5–10 years, and \~40% of nursing work is replaceable by LLMs. "cancer in 15 years is going to be mostly a thing of the past."
- Deerfield's labs safely dosed a 2-year-old with autism caused by a missing SHANK3 gene. GLP-1s touch many chronic diseases beyond weight, and software is working out per-patient answers.
