### 78. Stanley Druckenmiller — How AI is dominating his long portfolio (Bloomberg Live, Jun 2023)

*Macro investor, formerly at Soros; trades five or six asset classes and says "I've never had a down year" in 40 years.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=53wThFFuOqU)

**Takeaway:** After 11 years of free money and 500bp of hikes in a year, he expected "more shoes to drop" and a hard landing (Jun 2023). Yet AI dominated his long book, because a company whose earnings rise sustainably through a recession can rise in one.

- Recession-proof growth: "history has proved if you ... have very good earnings in a recession and they're sustainable ... those stocks will do just fine." Nvidia's orders and earnings could rise \~70% even in a hard landing.
- Screen implication: earnings resilience protects a stock in a downturn, so test for revenue and earnings growth through past recessions (2008–09, 2020).
- Secular moves last years, not months: the dot-com run lasted 2–2.5 years, \~4 for Cisco and Sun. Nvidia, up from the low 100s (Oct 2022) to 380–390, was a 2–3-year hold "not for 10 months" (Jun 2023).
- AI is real, unlike crypto: possibly "as transformative as the Internet", with top coders 7–8x more productive than five months earlier. Not all AI names will survive, and AI had dominated his longs for 5–6 months.
- The hard-landing case (Jun 2023): "the biggest broadest asset bubble ever" met 500bp of hikes in a year. Silicon Valley Bank and Bed Bath & Beyond were "probably the tip of the iceberg".
- Liquidity mechanics (Jun 2023): draining \~$10T of COVID money was paused by the BOJ's $400B of buying and a $700B Treasury cash (TGA) drawdown. Both reverse: \~$800B of issuance, ongoing QT, and student-loan payments from September.
- The delay raises, not lowers, hard-landing odds, because the terminal rate ends higher and inflation stickier. He timed recession for \~end-2023 but admits "the real answer is I don't know".
- Worries (Jun 2023): profits −20–30% (not the usual 40–50%, since the recession is anticipated), office real estate, and credit tightening over 6–9 months. Banks hold 1–2% Treasuries, and recession losses come from cards and commercial property.
- Knowing when not to play: "my record is as much knowing not when to play as when to play." He saw no fat pitch in Jun 2023, expected "really fat pitches" in 8–24 months, and wouldn't spend cash early.
- Shorting is for professionals: he doubts he has made money on shorts over 40 years, since a short can lose 10x your money. In 1999 a $200M internet short lost $600M in three weeks, though all 12 companies later went bankrupt.
- Fiscal (Jun 2023): a 7.7%-of-GDP gap, with interest rising from \~6% to 27% of outlays by 2050 (CBO). It's a long-term worry, not a 3–6-month trade, and taxes "are going to be much higher in 20 years".
- China under Xi (Jun 2023): "he's not a capitalist", so expect an undynamic economy for 10–15 years after a 6–9-month stimulus "sugar high". Japan looked interesting (broad market, ending deflation, shareholder-value push), but his record trading it is poor.
