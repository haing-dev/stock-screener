### 98. Panel: Andrew Ballou, Eduardo Marques, David Rosen & John Lennon — Digging Deeper: value longs and AI-loser shorts (Sohn Conference Foundation, May 2026)

*Ballou runs a concentrated New York long/short fund (founded 2017); Marques runs \~$2bn of mid-cap long/short at Pertento Partners; Rosen runs deep-value Rubric Capital; Lennon runs Pleasant Lake Partners.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jqUwhN3Qqik)

**Takeaway:** With capital crowded into momentum strategies and AI, anything "not AI" has become "a source of funds" (May 2026). These value-leaning managers buy the de-risked laggards cheaply and short quality compounders exposed to future AI deflation.

- Rosen: quants, macro funds and pods all need momentum, so falling stocks have "literally no buyers". He can be 50% of the volume and the stock still drops 10%.
- Rosen: Viatris trades at 6–7x earnings, converts \~100% of earnings to cash and returns half to shareholders. With drugs bought from distressed Idorsia reading out in 6–9 months, he sees "more than a double the next 12 months" (May 2026).
- Rosen: diligence on the seller, Idorsia, found "a 10-bagger if all these things hit" (CHF 4 to CHF 42). Rubric bought \~10% of the company in \~3 weeks.
- Rosen: catalysts are descheduling insomnia drug QUVIVIQ plus a wakefulness label (+CHF 22), and ADHD/autism data plus drugs parked with its debt (+CHF 16). Trial details are unpublished, so "nobody is valuing this".
- Ballou: Carvana is the largest and most profitable used-car retailer, yet holds \~1% of a \~$1tn market with 40,000 players. It grows >30% at \~20x pre-tax next-year free cash flow and could be a 10-bagger in 5–10 years (May 2026).
- Ballou: Carvana's growth is forecastable, since each buyer refers \~10 friends and people buy a used car every \~7 years. Selling loans to the same investors yearly forces conservative underwriting, and scale reconditioning adds $500–1,000 of collateral value.
- Marques: treated as the cyclical it is, AI hardware (\~23% of the S&P) has valuations that "look really full" (May 2026). He prefers Korea, where SK Hynix trades at "six-ish" 2026 earnings versus Micron's \~10x.
- Marques: holdco SK Square lets you "effectively buy SK Hynix at a 47% discount", with buybacks and dividends promised to close the gap. Non-voting Korean preferreds also trade at "massive discounts to common".
- Marques: Samsung Life, the Lee family's vehicle for \~10% of Samsung Electronics, trades at 0.48x mark-to-market book and \~4x earnings including Samsung dividends.
- Marques: these discounts have long existed ("they've existed forever"), but Value Up reforms copy Japan's, foreign money is returning and US retail now has access (May 2026).
- Lennon: on next year's consensus Samsung is the world's most profitable company, Hynix #2 and Nvidia #3, at single-digit multiples with buybacks. He allows it may be the top.
- Marques: "the best short ideas are actually high quality compounding great businesses." AI "service level deflation" doesn't show in near-term earnings yet, which is why quality stocks fell on good numbers (Q1 2026).
- Marques: short REA, the highest-multiple classifieds stock, whose "5% a year forever" price rises without audience growth are "broken". Within 2–3 years agentic AI could replace the marketplace model.
- Lennon: Reddit, down \~50% in an "AI loser" basket while usage grows, trades at \~14x next-year and \~10x 2028 earnings. AI-licensing repricing could make it "between a double and triple" (May 2026).
