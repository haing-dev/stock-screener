### 99. Michael Buckley, Muhammad Andreala \[?\], Nick Shalek & Tom Morgan — Sohn 2024 Lightning Round: Natera, Carpenter, Ethereum and curiosity (Sohn Conference Foundation, Apr 2024)

*Four \~5-minute pitches hosted by Boykin Curry (co-CIO, Eagle Capital): Buckley of Duquesne, Andreala \[?\] of Advent, Shalek of Ribbit Capital, and Morgan, who pitched no stock.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4OyXYR5GvRY)

**Takeaway:** Three of the four pitches pair a durable moat with an inflection the market under-prices. The cleanest is Carpenter, a sold-out three-supplier oligopoly whose price rises could take EPS from \~$4.20 to \~$10 (Apr 2024).

- Andreala \[?\]: Carpenter is one of three global suppliers of jet-engine nickel alloys, with 40% share. Capacity is sold out, and qualifying new capacity takes 5–10 years.
- Andreala \[?\]: pricing is up 40% in two years, "just the beginning", and a fixed-cost mill business converts it at high incremental margins. Prices already realized beat management's plan to triple EBIT by FY27.
- Andreala \[?\]: EPS goes from \~$4.20 (FY24) to \~$10 in two years, "almost all of that is price". At $71 it trades at 17x versus comps at 20–30x, so 20x gives $200 and 15x $150 (Apr 2024).
- Andreala \[?\]: a big Carpenter price rise is only \~30bp of GE's cost, and engine makers raise aftermarket prices "well north of inflation". So customer pushback or vertical integration is unlikely.
- Buckley: Natera's Signatera blood test "catches cancer on average 9 months sooner than a CT scan". Its flywheel runs from >$1bn of R&D to \~4x rivals' publications, then reimbursement, oncologist adoption and data.
- Buckley: oncologist adoption reached 40% and goes to 65% by 2030 in his base case, with test volume up \~10x yet only \~30% penetrated. Natera won permanent IP injunctions against two of three main rivals.
- Buckley: many tests in uncovered cancers are "not getting paid anything at all", so wider coverage lifts net price. That brings profitability "nearly two years before consensus", >$2bn EBIT by 2030 and a \~$40bn market cap (\~4x) (Apr 2024).
- Buckley: the 2024 catalyst is a study of starting chemotherapy on a Signatera result alone, which could make it standard of care. A miss "wouldn't change a trajectory of adoption"; stock-based pay dilution is the offset.
- Shalek: Ethereum's >$7bn of fees, all paid in ETH, fund "a programmatic buyback and dividend", and supply is declining. So ETH can be valued with a DCF and complements Bitcoin as a store of value (Apr 2024).
- Shalek: Ethereum's $88bn of stablecoins and >$100bn of tokenized collateral are "a fraction of 1%" of target markets. Scalability, usability and compliance still need building, "but it's all solvable"; incumbents' odds of a rival network are "maybe a two" in 10.
- Morgan: the left brain is "a junior analyst" who "lives in the model", the right brain the intuitive PM. Curiosity signals relevance, and "you don't let the junior analyst run the hedge fund he will burn it down".
