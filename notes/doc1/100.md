### 100. Steve Cohen — Slowing growth, a "toppy" market and handling slumps (Sohn Conference Foundation, May 2025)

*Oversees portfolio managers at Point72 after \~47–48 years of trading; owns the New York Mets; a year after stepping away from the screens. Interviewed by Jawad Mian.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o6WeRpuYiyA)

**Takeaway:** In May 2025 Cohen saw "significant slowing growth" but no recession yet, and with high multiples that means a sideways, "toppy" market. He told his PMs that 2024's easy regime was over: raise the bar, lower expectations and lean on process.

- Macro (May 2025): \~45% recession odds, lower after China "basically capitulated" on tariffs, and an effective tariff rate of 14–17%, revised from 16–20%. Expect 2026 growth of \~1.5% "maybe lower" and no quick Fed move while it fears tariff inflation.
- Slowing growth plus high multiples is "a mix that doesn't give you much upside", so he is more conservative at the index level. Expect a trading range; a retest of the lows, 10–15% down, is "not a calamity" (May 2025).
- The test he's watching: "I want to see when the data turns down how the market's going to react". He's "not convinced" markets will hold up through weak data.
- Regime change: after "a phenomenal 24" in which "Everything worked", he told PMs to keep the bar higher for 2025. Be careful with tariff-hit, data-dependent names that "have rallied back significantly".
- Many PMs had outsized 2024s and pressure themselves to repeat, but a harder world means lower expectations. "I don't create the world, I got to live in the world."
- Sideways is normal: "markets can go sideways, and that's perfectly normal", even if for a while it felt as though markets rise every year.
- Slumps: go back to process, since doubt after even two bad days is normal. A hitter can go "0 for 20" while hitting the ball hard and just be unlucky.
- In noisy markets trust your analysis of the facts, then pattern recognition, since situations "rhyme". Preparation is the step you can't skip; a first drawdown is "a badge of honor".
