### 103. David Einhorn — "Eventually is now" for gold (Sohn Conference Foundation, Jun 2022)

*Of Greenlight Capital, introduced as "making history from the \[Sohn\] stage for decades"; gold was an existing Greenlight position. He presented remotely.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YvbjYTebx88)

**Takeaway:** "The fed is bluffing" (May 2022): with $24tn of public debt and supply-driven inflation, it can't raise rates enough without blowing up the deficit. Forced to choose between inflation and funding the Treasury, "it has to pick the treasury", so gold is a buy.

- Rate arithmetic (May 2022): $24tn of public debt, up 6x in 20 years, with \~$7tn rolling within a year. Each 1% adds \~$70bn to the deficit, and "a full volker" 19% adds $1.3tn.
- The Fed's own balance sheet funds long assets overnight, so $5tn of reserves cost $50bn more per 1%. An average recession adds $700bn–$1tn to the deficit, and QT adds Treasury supply.
- Real-rate rule: "to defeat inflation you need positive real interest rates", as with Volcker's 19% against 14% inflation and Greenspan's 8.5% against 6%. Real rates were the most negative in 70 years (May 2022).
- A 2.5–3.5% "neutral" rate with 8.5% inflation is "akin to trying to clear your snow covered driveway with an ice cream scooper". A balanced-approach rule implies \~7%.
- Old-economy longs: a decade of underinvestment in cement, steel, fertilizer, chemicals, mining, energy and housing means "excess profits for an extended period of time". ESG and software's pull on capital starved them.
- Capital-allocation tell: "higher prices aren't fueling new capacity instead they're fueling buybacks", at "very low p e multiples". With no supply response, demand must be crushed, so inflation persists.
- Regime change: 1980–2020 was structural deflation. In supply-driven inflation, hikes "might perversely increase the inflation" by hurting supply more than demand, and they pay savers more, feeding demand.
- The dollar has been weaponized: freezing Russia's reserves pushes central banks to gold. Gold is only 4% of reserves in China and Saudi Arabia and 8% in India; Russia's tripled to 22% since 2014.
- Gold, not crypto, because gold "is already recognized as a globally acceptable central bank reserve asset". Backing reserves requires "the price of gold to go higher perhaps much higher".
- Endgame (forecast for 2023): as rates cross 3–4%, the Fed capitulates to keep the Treasury funded. Critics of tightening into a downturn should "just wait until they're forced to loosen into an inflationary spike".
- Greenlight's 1 May 2020 letter called the COVID shock inflationary. Official CPI understates it: on the old housing method, inflation "would rival the peak from 1980" (Larry Summers).
