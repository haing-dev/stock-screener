### 109. Josh Resnick & Leon Shaulov — AI winners, Amazon, Google, AMD and Sphere (Sohn Conference Foundation, Apr 2024)

*Tech, media and telecom investors Josh Resnick (Jericho Capital) and Leon Shaulov (Maplelane Capital); moderated by CNBC's Leslie Picker.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ll3ttXCSqmg)

**Takeaway:** AI is a once-in-decades build-out: Shaulov sees the largest cluster going from 32,000 GPUs to \~1m in 3–4 years (Apr 2024). Cash-rich companies investing heavily are pulling away from levered laggards, making 2024 "the best long environment" of Resnick's career.

- Shaulov: chips 3–4x more efficient on 30x larger clusters make models \~100x better, at \~$3–5bn per frontier model (Apr 2024). Street EPS estimates for Nvidia went from $15 to $32 in nine months: "everybody was wrong by 100%".
- Shaulov \[?\]: $500–800bn of AI spend for $3–4tn of savings or revenue is "15 to 20% Capital intensity", a formula that works. Rates hurt unprofitable, moatless, sub-scale firms; for cash-rich ones the 10-year yield barely matters next to ROI.
- Shaulov: Amazon has "three businesses and all three hitting at the same exact time", each with \~$50bn of revenue (Apr 2024). Retail margins head toward Walmart's and Costco's (30–42x multiples), AWS reaccelerates from 14% to \~20%, and EPS is \~$7.50 over 18 months.
- Shaulov: Google trades as "an AI loser" at a cheap multiple (Apr 2024). With opex cuts and 20% growth, 24x on $9.50–10 of EPS is \~70% upside; Gemini cannibalizing search is unknowable, so use probability trees.
- Shaulov: a $400bn GPU market won't have one player; hyperscalers "will want a second player", as with Intel and AMD. At 8–12% share, an $8–10bn MI300 run rate gives \~$10 of EPS, or ~~$250 at 25x; 2024 output (~~$5–6bn) is sold out.
- Resnick: AI pays off first in advertising (Meta, Google, AppLovin, The Trade Desk). Meta times ads by time of day and grows 25% at 50x Pinterest's size, and the gap with AI laggards will keep "widening".
- Resnick: 2024 is "a fair market" after 2022's indiscriminate tech selloff and 2023's snapback. Firms that earn, invest heavily and reward shareholders are bought, and laggards are sold to fund them.
- Resnick \[?\]: Sphere is "my favorite stock in our portfolio": in fragmented, personalized media, people "are thriving on Collective experiences". It has an 18K screen and 160,000 speakers, and more should follow in Seoul and the Middle East.
