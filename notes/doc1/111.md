### 111. Mike Alfred — Breaking down Bitcoin and AI (Tim Kotzman, Nov 2025)

*Investor in Bitcoin, Bitcoin-treasury companies and Bitcoin-mining/AI data-centre firms; founder and managing partner of Alpine Fox and an IREN director since Oct 2021.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pS3JNFZCrKc)

**Takeaway:** AI and Bitcoin are both gated by power, so back operators excellent at turning power and land into compute, not commodity-price bets. In Nov 2025 Alfred read Bitcoin's \~30% drawdown as a liquidity squeeze and expected risk assets and quality treasury names like Strive to rally.

- Underwrite data-centre companies on "how good are they at taking power and turning into compute", with mining chips for Bitcoin or GPUs for AI. A business resting only on a rising commodity price brings "long periods" of pain for shareholders.
- Delivery delays and guidance cuts at operators hailed as winners 6–9 months earlier are "systemic issues", not temporary (Nov 2025). Strong operators build redundancy, sometimes 3–4 internet connections per site.
- Holding Bitcoin "doesn't absolve you of your sins"; skilled operators that didn't hold all their Bitcoin proved the better investments. Asset-light GPU neoclouds can't scale "without owning anything".
- The sector stays fragmented, but top operators will "suck the oxygen out of the room" as Microsoft and Meta shift work to proven partners.
- Treasury companies (Nov 2025): issuers run by "Event planners... and social media influencers" brought "carnage", leaving the past year's buyers down \~80%. His pick from the rubble is Strive: "there's no exploding debt there's no liquidation mechanism".
- Treasury stocks are partly beta: even "the worst structured securities will still go up" with Bitcoin, as risk, liquidity and sentiment barometers. Separate quality from beta.
- Macro (\~20 Nov 2025): the Fed "has been too restrictive", shown by a \~4-year bear market in crypto ex-Bitcoin. He expects 100–200 bp of cuts within 6–9 months: "2026 is actually going to be the year that people thought 2025 would be."
- Bitcoin (Nov 2025): bullish for 3–9 months, perhaps "Q2 of 26 and Bitcoin's 180,000", with the bull market possibly running into 2027–28. Long term $1–3m a coin "unless the government stop printing money"; austerity is the one thesis-breaker.
- A smoother path without 80% crashes is possible if credit markets, nation-state reserves and target-date funds create "a structural bid". It is "probably not" likely yet: "we could have a 60 or 80% draw down at some point."
- Management access: some is essential, but too much makes you think the company "is perfect or better than it is". Investors who echo management are taking a risk; stay "naturally very skeptical".
