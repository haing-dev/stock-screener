### 112. Mike Alfred — The hidden AI trade: land, power and compute (Real Vision, Jun 2026)

*Founder and managing partner of Alpine Fox and a long-time Bitcoin investor; an IREN director since Oct 2021 who has "never sold a share".* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-p3DhlY2TEk)

**Takeaway:** The AI bottleneck is scarce, high-uptime, low-cost power and land, not chips (Jun 2026). Alfred's IREN bet, bought near $1 at the 2022–23 lows, is that a developer that also owns the compute will be "very hard to compete with".

- Buy in size where site value covers the downside: in Q1 2023 he valued IREN's sites at 2–4x its market cap. If the business failed, liquidation would return a multiple of the market cap; if it executed, 50–100x.
- IREN bottomed at $1.02 and Cipher at $0.39 in late Dec 2022; IREN hit \~$76 in Nov 2025 and was \~$58 in Jun 2026. His position is unchanged since 2022–23: "I don't think we're anywhere near fundamental value yet."
- Colocation landlords (Cipher, TeraWulf) skip GPU capex, the costliest layer, and should be "quite successful". Vertically integrated IREN and xAI/SpaceX own the compute too; asset-light CoreWeave relies on "20 plus external providers" for physical infrastructure.
- Bitcoin mining taught that paying a host means "your economics don't quite work" and you lose control of service. If IREN pulls it off "at scale, it'll be very hard to compete with them", and the market "still doesn't understand these economic models".
- "Bitcoin miners" was the wrong label: infrastructure developers (land, buildings, grid interconnects) differ from asset-light miners hosting machines elsewhere. Not all "pivoted" to AI: IREN signed an AI memorandum of understanding with Dell in 2019.
- Siting from first principles: low-cost, excess-renewable West Texas and the Oklahoma Panhandle, not consensus Northern Virginia. The choke point "hasn't been chips": some Nvidia GPUs sit in warehouses with nowhere to plug in.
- xAI/SpaceX "flipped" surplus Grok compute to Anthropic and Google on good terms, so a neocloud may be SpaceX's biggest near-term opportunity (Jun 2026). Anthropic is "clearly out in front" and, he believes, EBITDA-positive this quarter, pre-IPO.
- AI is in the "first or second innings", with 3–4 more boom-bust cycles likely over 2–3 decades, so buy 50%+ drawdowns. Since Q1 2023, Bitcoin and AI stocks have delivered similar returns.
