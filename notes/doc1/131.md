### 131. Rod Wong — RTW Biotech Opportunities' strong 2023 and a coming M&A "feeding frenzy" (Edison, Jan 2024)

*Founder and CIO of RTW Investments, interviewed by Rob Murphy of Edison Investment Research about the London-listed RTW Biotech Opportunities fund (ticker RTW).* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RpdBwXbyE1c)

**Takeaway:** RTW Bio's NAV rose over 23% in 2023, more than double its small-cap biotech benchmark, led by Merck's takeover of Prometheus and the RTW-incubated Rocket Pharma. Rod expected the late-2023 rebound to continue in 2024 on M&A, as pharma replaces revenue lost to patent expiries, and on the Fed's pivot.

- Results: the 2023 NAV gain was "a little bit over 23%", vs "just over 10%" for the Russell 2000 Biotech and "a little bit less than 4%" for the larger-cap Nasdaq Biotech. Since the Oct 2019 IPO it is up "a little bit over 80%", vs "just under 5%" and "just under 30%". Rod credits subspecialist analysts, strict selectivity and "a moderately concentrated portfolio", which made RTW "a kind of preferred partner" for promising biotechs.
- Prometheus was "over 12% of the return": Merck bought it at a "north of 70% premium". It led TL1A, "the most promising new Target" in inflammatory bowel disease, and RTW had co-led its pre-IPO financing about three years earlier.
- Rocket Pharma added "a little bit north of 8%". RTW co-founded and incubated it in 2015, and it is now "the second largest independent gene therapy company". In 2023 it agreed "a very efficient trial design" with the FDA for its Danon disease gene therapy.
- Outlook (Jan 2024): the rebound of the last two months of 2023 should continue, driven by M&A and the Fed's December pivot. The sector was "extremely oversold" after a bear market "almost three years long", and investors had "kind of forgotten about our space".
- New technologies are delivering: 2023 saw "the highest number of new drugs approved in history", and "nearly a quarter" were based on new modalities such as gene therapy, RNA, cell therapy and bispecific antibodies.
- M&A: pharma's "looming patent expirations" made 2023 a record M&A year, and much revenue is still to be replaced. "nearly a third" of "acquirable sector market cap" (companies under $25bn) was bought in 2023, so the remaining assets could see "a bit of a Feeding Frenzy".
- Arix Bioscience deal (announced 1 November): the combination would be "the second largest listed biotech fund" in the UK, with better visibility and liquidity and less duplicated overhead. Arix's earlier-stage assets complement RTW's; for Arix holders it is "far superior to" a wind-up, and for RTW it is immediately accretive. Arix holders vote on 29 January, with closing about a month later.
- The listed fund is an "ideal structure" for full-life-cycle investing: it "avoids the kind of misalignments" of pure venture or pure public funds, can hold through cycles, draws on the whole \~75-person team and can weight private deals more heavily. It anchors RTW's UK push (e.g. Immunocore, now "the world's leading" TCR company), and Rod wants more value from UK and European science kept local instead of moving to the US for growth capital. The host noted it still traded at a discount to NAV.
