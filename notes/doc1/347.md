### 347. Adena Friedman — Highlights: Nasdaq's reach, why companies list, and leading by empowering (NBIM, Dec 2024)

*Short cut of Nicolai Tangen's "In Good Company" interview with Nasdaq CEO Adena Friedman.* [Watch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6fsq-TYpMh0)

**Takeaway:** Nasdaq is now a "global technology provider to the financial industry" as well as an exchange, and it wins listings by taking companies young and keeping them as they grow.

- Nasdaq runs the Nordic and Baltic exchanges (not Norway's) and supplies technology to 130 markets. Each country gains from its own exchange for "local innovators", but "connected into a global ecosystem".
- It lists companies of all sizes: Amazon was worth $300 million when it went public, and companies that came small "they'd stay".
- Listing vs staying private is cyclical (are public investors ready to "underwrite risk" as rates change?) and structural ("the burden of being a public company").
- Concentration in a few giants is the "natural progression" of a tech-driven economy; 5,000 companies are still listed on Nasdaq.
- Leadership: moving from manager to leader means "empowering people" to do their jobs better than you could. "kindness is a strength", because "relationships break ties" and trust wins more business. Advice: stay curious and say yes.
