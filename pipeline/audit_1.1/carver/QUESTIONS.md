# Rob Carver: ten questions on the pack

## Instructions

- Your only source is `pack.json` in this folder. It holds claims from podcast transcripts (each with a verbatim
  `quote`, script-checked against the transcript, plus a machine-written `claim` summary and sometimes `conditions` or
  a `correction`) and a machine-written digest of blog posts (`blog_digest`, cited by `post_id`, e.g. B04). Do not use
  outside knowledge of Carver's books, blog or papers, and do not use any other file.
- The verbatim quotes are the most reliable layer. The claim summaries and the digest are machine-written and may
  overstate, understate or misread; where a claim summary goes beyond its quote, say so. Digest numbers were not
  checked against the posts: say "digest" when you rely on one.
- Answer each question in about 120-200 words. Cite ids (claim ids such as MT026-C07-1, or post ids such as B24) for
  every statement. Where the pack does not support something, write "the pack does not show this".
- For each question give:
  (i) what Carver says, with claim ids / blog post ids;
  (ii) whether it is SHOWN (tested, numbers given), ASSERTED (stated, no test shown), or your inference (mark it
  "(reader)");
  (iii) the evidence grade, one of strong / moderate / weak / assertion, with the reason (sample, costs, in sample or
  hypothetical, overlapping data, one system, conflicts of interest, internal inconsistency, etc.);
  (iv) any number that matters, with its id.
- After the ten answers, give a final list of every number you cited, one per line: number, what it measures, id.
- Do not run code that loads market data. Write your answers to `ANSWERS.md` in this folder.
