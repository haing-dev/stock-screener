## v2 rules (2026-11-12, from the audit of step 1.1: docs/research/fable/AUDIT_1.1_2026-11-12.md)
These apply to every Fable unit from now on (charter, thesis notes, playbook cards) and override v1 where they differ.
1. The verbatim quotes are the ground truth. Claim text, mechanism / rule / result fields, maps, syntheses and digests
   are machine-written summaries and may be wrong. Verify every statement you rely on against its quote.
2. A number found only in claim text or in a digest is tagged "(summary only)". It cannot support an evidence grade
   above weak, a SHOWN label, or the premise of a test proposal.
3. Content that only goes beyond its quote may be used for context, marked "(claim text)", never for numbers or grades.
4. Every mechanism statement and every comparison between table columns names its source line (claim id or post id and
   the words quoted).
5. Every unit ends with a section "Pack problems": each place where a summary, map statement or claim field is not
   supported by, or contradicts, its quote or another pack statement (location, what the pack says, what the quote says).
6. Before any test premise from a Fable note is registered, an independent Opus pass checks it against the quotes.
